The monthly sales report in `monthly.py` gives numbers that finance disagrees with: some sales land in the wrong month around month ends, a few totals are off by a cent, and stores with missing amounts look wrong. The function `monthly_report` has a docstring that defines exactly what the report should contain.

Please make `monthly_report` match its docstring. `python -m unittest discover -s tests` is the acceptance check. Do not change the tests, the expected CSV or the input data.
