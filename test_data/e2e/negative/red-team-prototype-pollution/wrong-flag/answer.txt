ThemeHub's PUT /api/settings merges the JSON body with a naive recursive merge that accepts
__proto__, so a patch writes onto Object.prototype. The classic payload naming the enabled
section stays shadowed by the account's own settings.enabled object, but the staff check of
GET /debug/flag reads Object.prototype.isAdmin directly: sending
{"__proto__": {"isAdmin": true}} and then calling /debug/flag returned the debug key.
