The PKI incident on the PKIGate gateway was closed out. A new client certificate for CN=auditor, O=DemoCorp was minted with a fresh key (pki/auditor.crt + pki/auditor.key), signed by the existing DemoCorp Root CA with the clientAuth extended key usage. The compromised CN=audit certificate (serial 1A2B3C4D5E6F7081 from docs/incident.md) was revoked with "openssl ca -config ca/ca.cnf -revoke pki/audit.crt" and the CRL was published as crl/crl.pem via "openssl ca -config ca/ca.cnf -gencrl -out crl/crl.pem", listing exactly that serial.

report/pki.md records new_cert_serial (the serial of the new auditor certificate), revoked_serial = 1A2B3C4D5E6F7081 and crl_path = crl/crl.pem, and python3 checks.py passes. The CA (pki/ca.crt / pki/ca.key), checks.py, docs/incident.md and README.md were left untouched.
