The documented endpoint GET /api/v2/users was shadowed by the legacy catch-all route ("GET", "/{path}", not_found), which sat at the TOP of the ROUTES list in server/app.py, so first-match routing sent every request, including /api/v2/users, /health and /openapi.json, to the catch-all 404 page even though openapi.json documents the endpoint.

The fix reorders ROUTES so all exact routes (/api/v2/users, /health, /openapi.json) are registered before the /{path} catch-all, which stays last for genuinely unknown paths. GET /api/v2/users now serves the 4-user list with a working dept filter, /openapi.json is served unchanged, and /api/v3/anything still gets the catch-all 404. python3 tests/api_test.py passes all 7 checks; report/shadow.md records shadowed_endpoint, catchall_position = first, and fixed_by.
