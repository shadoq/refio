The emergency PKI migration off the SHA-1 legacy CA is complete. A new SHA-256 CA (O=CorpShop) was built in pki/current/ (ca.crt + ca.key), the gateway certificate was re-issued from the same CSR (pki/legacy/server.csr) into pki/current/server.crt with sha256WithRSAEncryption and serial 5A73E6C18D0B9F42, and pki/legacy/crl.pem was published, signed by the legacy CA and revoking the old server certificate (serial 1C4B82F0A97D3E55, sha1WithRSAEncryption). proxy/gw.conf now points at pki/current/server.crt and pki/server.key only, so the gateway serves the new chain; the legacy CA stays on disk for audit but nothing references it anymore.

report/migration.md carries the eight keys (old_serial, new_serial, old_sig, new_sig, crl_entries = 1, trust_anchor, proxy_cert, proxy_key), python3 checks.py passes and bash verify.sh exits 0. verify.sh, checks.py, docs/incident.md, README.md, the legacy CA/CSR and pki/server.key were left untouched.
