Summary

Overall throughput grew compared with the previous quarter. The northern warehouse reopened its second loading dock in February. Average dwell time fell from 31 to 27 hours. Customer complaints about late deliveries dropped to 14 for the quarter.

Definitions used in this report

Q1 means January, February and March. April figures belong to Q2 and are not part of this report.

--- page 2 ---
Palletisation

Goods leave the warehouses on pallets. The capacity of one pallet depends on the product:

  bolts     500 units per pallet
  gaskets   120 units per pallet
  hinges     80 units per pallet

Pallets are counted per product per month: add up the units of one product shipped in one month, then round that figure up to whole pallets (a pallet that is only partly full still counts as one). Pallet counts of different products and months are then added together.

Outlook

Forklift fleet replacement is planned for the third quarter. The budget for pallet wrap was exceeded by 4 percent.
