# PERF-1181: nightly reconciliation takes 3+ minutes on the real ledgers

## Summary

The finance reconciliation job (reconcile.py over the two 50 000 row ledger
exports) used to finish in under 5 seconds. Since the rewrite that added the
per-account pairing it takes 3 minutes and 7 seconds on the batch host, and
the job now regularly bumps against its 5 minute timeout.

## What we know

- `profile.txt` is a cProfile dump from the 2026-11-17 nightly run (trimmed
  to the top entries). The time is inside the pairing loop, not in CSV
  reading.
- The batch host is slower than a laptop: anything from minutes there can
  still be seconds locally, so do not trust wall-clock comparisons.
- The sandbox copies under `data/` are smaller (20 000 rows each) but show
  the same shape.
- The output must stay byte-identical: the downstream diff tool asserts on
  every line, `data/expected.out` is the accepted output for the shipped
  inputs.

## Ask

Find the hot spots from the profile, fix them (index instead of scanning,
avoid re-doing work inside the row loop), keep the output byte-identical and
prove it with `python3 check.py`.
