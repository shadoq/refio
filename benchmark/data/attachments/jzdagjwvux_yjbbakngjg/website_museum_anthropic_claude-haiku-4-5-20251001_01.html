Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night.

What is the dress code? ▼
We recommend smart casual to formal attire. Think gallery opening elegance. Comfortable shoes are recommended as you'll be exploring multiple galleries throughout the evening.
Can I bring a guest? ▼
Each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring a guest, they will need their own ticket. Group discounts are available for parties of 10 or more.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person up to 7 days before the event. For cancellations due to unforeseen circumstances, please contact our support team.
What time should I arrive? ▼
Doors open at 18:45 for early arrivals. The official welcome reception begins at 19:00. We recommend arriving by 19:15 to enjoy the full experience without missing any performances.
Are dietary restrictions accommodated? ▼
Absolutely. During checkout, you can specify any dietary requirements or allergies. Our catering team will ensure all meals are prepared accordingly. Please note any restrictions at least 2 weeks before the event.
Can I take photos? ▼
Photography is permitted in most areas of the museum. However, some installations and performances are photography-free zones to preserve the experience for all guests. Please respect signage and artist requests.
Is the venue accessible? ▼
Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with elevators, accessible restrooms, and designated accessible seating areas. If you require specific accommodations, please contact us during ticket purchase.
What if I have more questions? ▼
Contact our support team at support@artifactmuseum.com or call +48 42 XXX XXXX. We're available Monday-Friday 10:00-18:00 CET. For urgent matters, use the live chat on our website.
