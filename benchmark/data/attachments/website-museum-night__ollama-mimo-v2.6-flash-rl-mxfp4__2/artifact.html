The Concept

After the lights dim, the collection wakes.

For one night, The Old Gallery opens its doors after closing time. Corridors usually sealed behind velvet rope become stages. Sculptures are lit from angles never intended. Sound drifts through rooms that have been silent for a century.

ARTIFACT is not an exhibition — it is a cinematic passage through art, performance, and the quiet mystery of a museum after dark. Guests move freely between hidden rooms, live sets, and curator-led encounters, guided only by golden pools of light.

Every ticket grants access to the full nocturnal program: eight hours of art, music, and guided discovery in one of Warsaw's most storied buildings.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Permanent halls and normally closed archives open exclusively for ticket holders, re-lit as a single continuous night scene.

02

Live performances

Ambient musicians, movement artists, and projectionists transform the sculpture hall into a living canvas from dusk until dawn.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups are led through restoration ateliers and storage vaults rarely seen by the public, capped at twelve guests per tour.

Before You Arrive

Doors open at 19:00 and the Curator Welcome begins at 19:30. We recommend arriving between 19:00 and 19:20 to allow time for security screening and cloakroom service. Last entry is 01:00.

There is no formal dress code — come as you would to an evening gallery opening. Many guests choose dark, comfortable attire suitable for walking and standing across the evening.

The Hidden Collection Tour at 20:00 is exclusive to Curator and Patron Pass holders. A limited number of standby places may be released at 21:30 at the tour desk in the Lower Vault.

Yes. The venue has a step-free entrance on ul. Kredytowa, lift access to all public floors, and accessible restrooms on levels 1 and 2. Companion tickets are available free of charge — please contact us in advance.

ARTIFACT is recommended for guests aged 16 and over due to late hours, low lighting, and sound levels. Visitors under 18 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

Your reservation is linked to the email address used at booking. Present any photo ID at the entrance desk and our team will reissue your pass within a minute. Screenshots of the confirmation email are also accepted.

A welcome aperitif is included with every pass. A late bar and small plates are served in the Atrium until 01:45. Curator Pass holders receive two drink tokens; Patron Pass holders enjoy the private lounge bar all night.
