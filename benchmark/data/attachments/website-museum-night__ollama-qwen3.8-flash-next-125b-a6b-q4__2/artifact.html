The Concept

After Dark, the Gallery Speaks

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living, breathing nocturnal experience. Curated by night, experienced by all — wander between centuries of art while ambient composers score your steps and projections breathe new life into marble and canvas.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Rooms rarely open to the public, lit only by hand-guided spotlights. See the permanent collection as you never have — in silence, shadow, and gold.

Live Performances

Ambient musicians, VJs, and a midnight sound session weave an original score through the halls — performed live among the exhibits.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend to Vault Level −1 with our curators for a guided walk through the storage collection — artifacts that have never been displayed.

The Venue

Address

Getting There

  • Metro Line M1 — Politechnika station, 4 min walk
  • Tram 4 / 15 / 18 — stop Piękna
  • Bus 131 / 175 — stop Wiejska
  • Paid parking on ul. Wilcza (limited after 20:00)

Accessibility

Step-free access throughout the ground and first floors. Elevator access to Vault Level −1 for the hidden collection tour. Hearing-loop in the Auditorium. Assistance dogs welcome. Please note the midnight walk route includes one short flight of stairs.
