Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is ARTIFACT: Museum Night?

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a premium after-hours museum experience in Warsaw. For one night, the museum transforms into an immersive world of art, performance, and discovery. Join us for an unforgettable evening.

When does the event take place?

The event takes place on 18 October 2026, from 19:00 to 02:00. Doors open at 19:00 and the museum closes at 02:00.

Where is the venue located?

The Old Gallery is located at ul. Starego Muzeum 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland. Take the metro to Centrum station and walk 5 minutes.

Is the event suitable for children?

The event is designed for adults 18+. Some performances may contain mature themes. We recommend checking individual event descriptions.

Can I bring a guest?

Each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring a guest, please purchase an additional ticket. Group bookings are available for 10+ people.

Is there a dress code?

We suggest smart casual attire. The venue is air-conditioned, so a light jacket may be comfortable. Comfortable shoes are recommended for gallery walks.

What if I need to cancel?

Tickets can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the event for a full refund. After that, tickets are non-refundable but transferable to another attendee.

Are there food and drinks available?

Light refreshments, wine, and cheese will be available in the VIP lounge for Patron Pass holders. Night Pass and Curator Pass holders can purchase drinks at the bar.
