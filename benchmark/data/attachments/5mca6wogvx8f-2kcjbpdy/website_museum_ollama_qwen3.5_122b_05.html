The Experience

An exclusive journey through art, sound, and shadow. Step beyond the velvet rope into a world where history meets the avant-garde.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Explore curated masterpieces in a unique lighting environment, designed to reveal hidden details unseen during the day.

Live Performances

Immersive soundscapes and live cello performances echoing through the grand halls of the gallery.

Hidden-Room Tours

Guided access to restricted archives and restoration studios, led by senior curators.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Location & Access

Address: ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 1, Warsaw

The Old Gallery is a historic neoclassical building located in the heart of the Royal Route.

Travel Tips

  • 🚇 Metro: Station 'Ratusz Arsenał' (5 min walk)
  • 🚌 Bus: Lines 175, 180 stop nearby
  • 🚗 Parking: Limited street parking; recommend Park & Ride at 'Muranów'

Accessibility

The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with elevator access to all floors.
