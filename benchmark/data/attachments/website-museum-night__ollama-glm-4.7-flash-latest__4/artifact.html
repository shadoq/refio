The Concept

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a cinematic after-hours experience that transforms galleries into immersive stages. Step into velvet darkness, guided by golden spotlights, and discover art in unexpected ways.

After-hours Exhibitions

Exclusive access to curated collections after closing time, with ambient lighting and curated soundscapes.

Live Performances

Intimate live music, sound installations, and performance art in hidden gallery spaces.

Guided Tours

Secret passages and hidden rooms revealed by curators and artists during exclusive night tours.
