The Concept

One Night. Every Room Awake.

Once a year, The Old Gallery lowers its lights and keeps its doors open long past midnight. ARTIFACT gathers exhibitions, sound, and shadow into a single velvet darkness — an invitation to wander slowly, listen closely, and look again at works you thought you already knew.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Every gallery stays open until 02:00, lit low and hung differently — the collection as you have never been allowed to see it.

Live Performances

Ambient sets, a solo cello in the vault, and light works that respond to the rooms themselves — music made for marble and silence.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small groups are led through vault corridors, storage rooms, and stairwells that stay closed to the public the other 364 nights.

Travel Tips

Arrive after 21:00 for the quietest galleries, and before 01:00 for last entry. The cloakroom is free all night for coats and bags.

Accessibility

The courtyard entrance is step-free, elevators reach every floor, and accessible restrooms are on the ground floor. Companions of visitors needing assistance enter free.

Parking & Public Transport

Metro Nowy Świat–Uniwersytet is a five-minute walk, and trams stop at Plac Trzech Krzyży. Street parking is limited after 20:00 — we recommend public transport.
