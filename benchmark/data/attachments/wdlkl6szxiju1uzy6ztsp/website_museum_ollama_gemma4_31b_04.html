A Night of Silent Wonder

Step into a world where the boundary between observer and art dissolves. ARTIFACT is not just an exhibition; it's a curated sensory journey through the depths of human creativity, set against the velvet backdrop of midnight.

Experience rare masterpieces under golden spotlights, accompanied by ambient soundscapes and avant-garde performances.

Frequently Asked

What is the dress code? +
We suggest "Dark Elegance" or formal attire to match the cinematic atmosphere of the evening.
Are photographs allowed? +
Photography is permitted in most areas, but we ask that you disable flashes to preserve the lighting design and art.
Can I buy tickets at the door? +
Due to capacity limits, this is a ticketed event only. We cannot guarantee entry for walk-ins.
