Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Concept

🎨

Immersive Art

Experience curated exhibits in a unique after-hours setting with interactive installations and sensory experiences.

🎭

Live Performances

Exclusive performances by renowned artists across multiple disciplines including music, dance, and spoken word.

🍷

Cultural Dialogue

Engage in thought-provoking discussions with curators, artists, and fellow art enthusiasts over curated refreshments.

Program

19:00

Opening Ceremony

Welcome by the curator and special guest. Experience the first glimpse of the exhibition.

19:30

Performance: "Echoes of Time"

Contemporary dance piece exploring the relationship between memory and physical space.

20:15

Exhibit Tour: "Hidden Histories"

Guided tour of the special exhibition featuring rarely seen artifacts and their stories.

21:00

Live Music: "Nocturnal Sounds"

Experimental electronic performance inspired by museum collections.

22:00

Artist Talk: "The Future of Museums"

Panel discussion with leading artists and museum directors about the evolving role of cultural institutions.

23:00

Interactive Installation: "Dreams in Motion"

Participatory art experience where visitors' movements create visual projections.

00:30

Midnight Refreshments

Special cocktails and small bites inspired by the exhibition themes.

01:00

Final Performance: "Ephemeral"

Immersive multimedia performance marking the closing of the event.

Frequently Asked Questions

The event begins at 19:00 with the opening ceremony and concludes at 02:00 with the final performance. All areas will be accessible throughout the event hours.
This event is for guests aged 18 and above. Valid ID will be required for entry. Children under 18 are not permitted.
Small personal bags are permitted, but large backpacks and suitcases must be checked at the coat check. For security reasons, all bags will be subject to inspection upon entry.
Yes, the venue is fully wheelchair accessible with designated viewing areas. Please contact us in advance if you require specific accommodations or assistance during the event.
We recommend elegant evening attire. While formal wear is not required, we encourage guests to dress in a manner that reflects the sophistication of the event. No athletic wear or beachwear permitted.
Photography is permitted in most areas, but flash photography and tripods are prohibited. Some exhibits may have specific photography restrictions, which will be clearly marked. Please respect all signage and staff instructions regarding photography.
