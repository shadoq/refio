The Concept

An Institution, After Dark

When the doors close to the public, ARTIFACT begins. For one night, The Old Gallery is transformed into a living, breathing stage — where sculpture halls become concert rooms, forgotten storage becomes exhibition, and silence becomes sound. This is not a tour. It is a séance with culture.

Getting There

The venue sits five minutes on foot from Centrum metro station. Taxis and rideshares can drop off directly at the main entrance on Muzealna street. We recommend arriving 15 minutes before your selected entry time.

Accessibility

The Old Gallery offers step-free access throughout the main galleries, accessible restrooms on the ground floor, and complimentary wheelchair loan available at the entrance desk.

Parking & Transport

Limited street parking is available on Muzealna street after 19:00. The nearest public car park is Plac Bankowy, an 8-minute walk. Night buses 180 and 222 stop directly outside the venue.
