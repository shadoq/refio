The Concept

When the galleries fall quiet, the collection begins to speak.

ARTIFACT turns the city's museum after dark into a slow, luminous journey. Halls that are usually hushed by daylight open their doors to candlelit rooms, live sound, and conversations with the people who shaped the collection.

Walk at your own pace through spotlit corridors, hidden chambers, and courtyards under the stars. Every hour brings a new layer of the night.

“Nothing here is ever fully seen in the light.”

— Curatorial statement, ARTIFACT 2026

After-hours exhibitions

Main galleries reopened for a night, with lighting designed to reveal details the day never shows.

Live performances

Ambient sound, movement, and projection artists composing new works inside the sculpture halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led by curators through rooms normally kept closed to the public.

Program

Eight hours, one unfolding night.

Follow the evening hour by hour. Each moment is designed to lead into the next.

    Venue

    The Old Gallery

    ul. Muzealna 12, 00-001 Warsaw

    A former industrial hall turned museum, the Old Gallery holds its shadows well. Brick vaults, long corridors, and a glass-roofed courtyard make it the ideal stage for a night that belongs to the collection.

    Travel tips

    • Arrive before 20:00 for the quietest galleries.
    • Cloakroom available on the ground floor.
    • Comfortable shoes recommended.

    Access & transport

    • Step-free entrance on Muzealna street.
    • Accessible restrooms on every level.
    • Assistance available on request.
    • Metro line M1 and bus stop 2 minutes away.
