Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear? +
Smart casual to formal. Think "museum gala". Comfortable shoes are recommended for the tours.
Is food included? +
Yes, a welcome drink is included in all tickets. Curator and Patron passes include additional culinary experiences.
Can I bring a camera? +
Personal cameras are allowed for non-flash photography. Professional equipment requires prior permission.
Is the venue accessible? +
Yes, The Old Gallery has elevator access to all floors and wheelchair-friendly restrooms.
What is the cancellation policy? +
Tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable until 48 hours before the event.
Will there be coat check? +
Yes, complimentary coat check is available at the main entrance.
