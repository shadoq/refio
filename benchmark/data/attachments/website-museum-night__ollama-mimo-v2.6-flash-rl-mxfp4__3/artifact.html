The Concept

When the last visitor leaves, the collection wakes. ARTIFACT opens the doors of The Old Gallery after dark for one night of hidden rooms, live sound, and light drawn across marble.

Wander corridors lit only by gold spotlights, encounter performances staged between the sculptures, and let curators reveal works that never reach the public floor. It is a museum hour that does not officially exist.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Permanent and temporary collections open exclusively past closing, under a bespoke night lighting design.

02

Live performances

Ambient sets, spoken word, and movement pieces staged between the halls throughout the night.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups are led into restoration ateliers and storage vaults normally closed to the public.

Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Próżna 12, 00-107 Warsaw — a restored 1908 exhibition house in the heart of the city, five minutes on foot from the historic centre.

Getting Here

  • Metro: Ratusz Arsenał (M1/M2), 6 min walk
  • Tram: stops at Plac Bankowy (lines 4, 15, 18, 22)
  • Bus: night lines N11 and N16 stop on ul. Graniczna
  • Parking: underground garage at Próżna 14, €4 flat evening rate

Accessibility

  • Step-free entry via the Próżna street ramp
  • Two passenger lifts serving all floors
  • Wheelchair seating reserved at every performance
  • Assistance dogs welcome throughout

Good to Know

  • Cloakroom included with every ticket
  • 18+ after 21:00, ID required at door
