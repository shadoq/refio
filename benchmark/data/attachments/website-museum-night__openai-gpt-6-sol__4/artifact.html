Good to know

Before you step inside.

The little details that make a night feel effortless.

What is ARTIFACT: Museum Night?

It is an after-hours museum experience bringing together gallery access, live performances, guided encounters and conversations in one evening at The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

When and where does the event take place?

ARTIFACT takes place on 18 October 2026, from 19:00 until 02:00 the following morning, at The Old Gallery in Warsaw. Doors open at 19:00; last entry is at 01:00.

What is included with my ticket?

All tiers include access to the galleries, open talks and performances, and a digital evening guide. Curator adds a guided walk, welcome drink and priority entry; Patron also adds an artist meet-and-greet, reserved conversation seating and a keepsake. Ticket selection on this demo site does not create a real booking.

Can I arrive late or leave and re-enter?

You may arrive at any time before last entry at 01:00. Re-entry may depend on venue capacity; please check with the welcome team before leaving the building.

Is the venue accessible?

A step-free entrance is indicated on the illustrative map above. Please speak to the welcome team on arrival for help navigating the venue or finding seating. For specific access needs, contact the venue directly before attending.

Is there a dress code or age limit?

There is no dress code: come as you are and wear shoes comfortable for exploring the galleries. The evening is designed for adults; check with the venue about admission for younger visitors.
