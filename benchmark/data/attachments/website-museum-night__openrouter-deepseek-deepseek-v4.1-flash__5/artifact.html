ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

A cinematic after-hours journey through art, sound, and hidden rooms. One night only, when the gallery doors stay open past midnight.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Scroll

When the last visitors leave, the museum becomes something else. ARTIFACT: Museum Night invites you into the galleries after dark — where light, sound, and silence turn the collection into a living, breathing experience.

After-hours exhibitions

Wander the permanent collection in near-darkness, lit only by curated spotlights and the glow of the city outside.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection art, and intimate performances staged among the sculptures and marble halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group tours unlock the archive wing and rooms rarely opened to the public. Limited places, book early.

The Place

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Getting there

Metro: Ratusz Arsenał (M1), 6 min walk. Tram: lines 4, 15, 18 to Plac Teatralny. Bus: 128, 175 to Stare Miasto. Bike racks available at the main entrance.

Parking

Paid underground car park at Plac Zamkowy, 4 min walk. Limited street parking after 18:00.

Accessibility

Step-free access via the main entrance. Elevators to all floors. Accessible restrooms on every level. Assistance dogs welcome.
