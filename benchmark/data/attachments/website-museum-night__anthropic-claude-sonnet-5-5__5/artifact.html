ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night,Reimagined

One night. Seven hours. Every hall of The Old Gallery opens after dark — lit only by spotlights, projection and sound.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw Ages 18+
Get Tickets
The Concept

The galleries exhale after dark.

When the last daytime visitor leaves, The Old Gallery becomes something else. Corridors narrow into cinema, sculptures cast long shadows, and rooms closed to the public open their doors. ARTIFACT is an editorial, slow-paced night for those who like their culture quiet, curious and a little mysterious.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Permanent collections and three new installations, re-lit for the night with theatrical spotlighting and no crowds of daylight.

02

Live performances

Ambient composers, sound artists and projection sets performed inside the halls where the works live.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups follow curators through vaults, restoration studios and archives normally sealed to visitors.

Program

Hour by hour, into the night.

Eight moments between dusk and 02:00. Drift between them or stay in one room all evening.

Artists & Speakers

Voices in the dark.

Eight artists, curators and composers shape the night. Select a card for the full profile and session time.

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

A neoclassical former bank turned museum, with marble halls, a glass-roofed sculpture court and a hidden basement archive.

Address

The Old Gallery, ul. Długa 12, 00-238 Warsaw, Poland. Main entrance on the courtyard side, marked with gold lanterns.

Travel tips

The Old Town is a ten-minute walk from Warsaw Centralna. Taxis and ride-hailing can drop off at the corner of Długa and Nowomiejska. Expect queues around 19:00–19:30 — arriving at 20:00 is calmer.

Accessibility notes

Step-free entrance and lift to all exhibition floors. Accessible restrooms on every level, quiet room on the ground floor, and assistance dogs are welcome. Tell us in advance at access@artifact.example.

Parking & public transport

Metro M1 Ratusz Arsenał (6 min walk) and night buses N32, N44 stop nearby. Public car parks at Plac Zamkowy and Hala Targowa; there is no on-site parking. Bike racks at the courtyard gate.
