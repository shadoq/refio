Nocturnal Exhibition 2026 Warsaw, Poland
ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

Date 18 October 2026
Hours 19:00 – 02:00
Venue The Old Gallery, Warsaw
The Manifesto
When the daytime crowd departs and shadows stretch across marble colonnades, ARTIFACT awakens. A seven-hour multisensory passage uniting historical antiquity with radical sound, light, and private gallery sanctuaries.
01 / ARCHIVE

After-Hours Exhibitions

Roam vaulted galleries shrouded in twilight illumination. Curated masterpieces and hidden archaeological relics revealed under focused golden beams.

02 / RESONANCE

Live Performances

Site-specific ambient drones, contemporary string quintets, and spatial audio installations echoing through cavernous Renaissance rotundas.

03 / LABYRINTH

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Gain privileged keyholder clearance into subterranean archive crypts, restoration workshops, and historic private library vaults never opened to the public.

Chronology of the Evening

Program & Nocturnal Schedule

Follow the sequence of darkness as each chamber of The Old Gallery unlocks distinct sonic, visual, and architectural encounters.

19:00

Doors Open & Velvet Reception

Grand Atrium

Auditorium Maximum

The Location

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Krakowskie Przedmieście 26/28, 00-927 Warszawa

A neo-classical jewel founded in 1862, featuring vaulted brick crypts, 14-meter marble colonnades, and private inner courtyards insulated from the pulse of modern Warsaw.

Transit & Underground

Metro Line M2 (Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet Station) 300m walk. Bus lines 116, 178, 180, and 503 stop directly before the main iron gates.

Accessibility Access

Step-free elevator access is available at the North Portico. Tactile gallery floor guides and quiet respite chambers located on Level -1.

Nocturnal Parking

Valet service on Krakowskie Przedmieście or secure underground parking at Plac Teatralny (7-minute lit pedestrian route).

Frequently Asked Questions
