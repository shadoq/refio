The Location

The Old Gallery

A Historic Setting

Nestled in the heart of Warsaw's cultural district, The Old Gallery offers a unique blend of neoclassical architecture and modern art. Its soaring ceilings and marble columns provide the perfect backdrop for an evening of music and mystery.

Address: ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 15, Warsaw

Access: Metro Station "Stare Miasto" (2 min walk)

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code? +
We recommend smart casual or evening wear. The event takes place in a historic gallery, so we encourage guests to dress elegantly to match the atmosphere.
Can I bring my own drinks? +
No, outside food and beverages are not permitted. We have a selection of artisanal cocktails and non-alcoholic options available at our pop-up bars throughout the venue.
Is photography allowed? +
Yes, personal photography is encouraged! Please be respectful of other guests and the artworks. Flash photography is prohibited during performances.
What happens if it rains? +
The event is entirely indoors, so rain will not affect the experience. However, we recommend checking the weather forecast for your journey to and from the venue.
