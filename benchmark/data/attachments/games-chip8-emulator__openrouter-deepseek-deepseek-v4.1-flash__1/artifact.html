How to play: the CHIP-8 keypad is mapped to the physical keys shown on the right (1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V). Click or hold the on-screen keypad buttons too. Use Run, Pause, Step and Reset to control execution. The demo ROM is a bouncing ball — press CHIP-8 key 5 (physical W) to reverse it.
