The Concept

One night. Seven hours.
The collection awakens.

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living nocturne — a single ticketed evening where the permanent collection is re-staged under moving spotlights, live scores and slow cinema.

Wander freely between chambers. Each hour reveals a new movement: sound installations in the marble hall, performance pieces in the sculpture court, and a final procession through the vaulted print room.

No queues, no crowds, no daylight. Only the artifacts, the dark, and the people who came to see them differently.

01

Curated After Dark

A route designed by our curatorial team, revealing works rarely shown outside daylight hours.

02

Live & Immersive

Chamber musicians, projection artists and performers stationed throughout the galleries.

03

Members' Vault

Patron ticket holders gain access to the restricted print vault and its one-night exhibition.

Program

Seven hours, eight movements

The evening unfolds as a continuous score. Arrive at any hour — the galleries never pause.

19:00

Doors & First Light

Welcome aperitif in the atrium; spotlights rise across the main hall.

19:45

Opening Address

Chief curator introduces the night's route and the restored acquisitions.

20:30

String Nocturne

Live quartet performs among the marble sculptures of the west court.

21:30

Projection: Slow Cinema

Silent film loop mapped onto the vaulted ceiling of the print room.

22:30

Performance Piece

Choreographed movement through the long gallery — audience walks with the dancers.

23:30

Midnight Conversation

Artists in dialogue: collecting, memory and the ethics of display.

00:30

Vault Opening

Patrons' restricted print vault opens for a single hour.

01:15

Final Procession

Candle-lit walk through every chamber, closing at 02:00.

Before you arrive

Doors open at 19:00 and the last entry is 23:00. Arriving between 19:00 and 20:00 lets you catch the opening address and first light sequence.

No formal dress code — wear what lets you move comfortably through the galleries for several hours. The galleries are kept cool, so a layer is advisable.
