ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined.

Step through velvet darkness into gold-lit neoclassical salons. An intimate after-hours union of forgotten antiquities, live modular soundscapes, and subterranean vaults.

✦
Date 18 October 2026
◷
Hours 19:00 – 02:00
⦿
Location The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Curatorial Statement
“Artifacts don’t sleep when the sun sets. In the absence of daylight, their shadows confess secrets centuries in the making.”

ARTIFACT brings together visual artists, sonic architects, and museum curators to transform Warsaw's 19th-century gallery into an immersive, multi-sensory dreamscape. Wander past illuminated marble friezes, experience quadraphonic ambient soundscapes, and enter historical storage archives closed to the general public for over four decades.

01 / DISCOVERY

After-Hours Exhibitions

Experience permanent masterworks and private collections illuminated strictly by directional warm spotlights and responsive amber glows.

02 / SOUNDSCAPE

Live Performances

Spatial ambient synthesizer arrays, live cello improvisations, and poetic sound installations resonating across domed marble halls.

03 / INTIMACY

Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group curator access to subterranean restoration labs, archive cellars, and previously uncatalogued architectural relics.

Night Schedule

The Chronology

Eight carefully orchestrated chapters unfold from twilight dusk to the deep quiet of 02:00.

19:00

Doors Open & Ambient Prelude

Great Atrium

Arrival drinks in the Grand Foyer, accompanied by subtle quadraphonic tape loop installations echoing off the marble staircase.

19:30

Curator Welcome & Architectural Keynote

Lecture Rotunda

Chief Curator Helena Rostova introduces the nocturnal motif and historical significance of the Warsaw archive collection.

20:00

Hidden Collection Tour (First Departure)

Sub-Vault 04

Passage through underground masonry tunnels to inspect 16th-century ecclesiastical goldsmithing and raw canvas restorations.

21:00

Live Ambient Performance: Julian Karras

Central Hall

A deep-listening set combining analog modular synthesizers with processed viola beneath the 1888 painted fresco dome.

22:00

Sculpture Hall Projection: "Ephemeral Stone"

Marble Colonnade

Site-specific digital ray-marching projected onto Hellenistic plaster casts by generative artist Mikołaj Wójcik.

23:00

Artist Talk: Preserving Darkness

Library Saloon

Discussion exploring light pollution, sensory deprivation in exhibition spaces, and the visual vocabulary of shadow.

00:00

Midnight Gallery Walk (Silent Mode)

Full Wing

Total verbal silence observed across the second floor. Handheld brass lamps provided for individual contemplative exploration.

01:00

Closing Sound Session & Herbal Digestif

Glass Conservatory

Subtle drone frequencies by Lena Słowik accompanied by nocturnal warm botanical infusions and farewell discourse.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your evening at ARTIFACT.

We encourage dark elegance: velvet, dark tailoring, midnight hues, and comfortable shoes suitable for historic stone floors. While black tie is not required, athletic wear and casual streetwear are gently discouraged to maintain the atmospheric visual harmony.

Non-flash, silent mobile photography is welcomed in the main galleries. Flash photography, professional camera rigs, tripods, and filming during live musical performances and the Silent Midnight Gallery Walk (00:00–01:00) are strictly forbidden to honor the sensory experience.

Passes can be transferred to another guest or refunded up to 72 hours prior to the event (until 15 October 2026, 19:00 CET). After that window, tickets are strictly non-refundable due to strictly capped gallery capacities.

Yes. The Old Gallery features step-free ramp access at the west courtyard entrance, two elevators serving all three above-ground floors, and dedicated hydraulic lift access to Vault 04. Accessible restrooms are located on levels 0 and 2.

Curated bio-dynamic wines, herbal botanical digestifs, artisanal mocktails, and small savory canapés will be served in the Glass Conservatory and Mezzanine Lounge from 19:30 until 01:30. All drinks and appetizers are included in Curator and Patron Passes, and available à la carte for Night Pass holders.

Tours depart in cohorts of 15 guests every 30 minutes between 20:00 and 23:30. Curator Pass and Patron Pass holders are assigned a reserved departure time stamp on their digital ticket upon check-in, avoiding queueing and crowding.
