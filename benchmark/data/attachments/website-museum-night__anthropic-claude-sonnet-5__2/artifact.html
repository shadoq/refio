The Concept

An institution after dark

Once the public leaves and the galleries fall quiet, ARTIFACT opens the doors again — for one night only. Wander century-old halls lit only by candle and spotlight, encounter live performance between the sculptures, and step into rooms the museum rarely shows. This is not a tour. It is a night the museum keeps for itself, shared with a few hundred guests.

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection reframed in gold light, with pieces rarely seen in daylight hours.

Live performances

Ambient musicians and sound artists respond to the architecture, turning stone halls into instruments.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups are led into archives and storage rooms that never open to the public.

Location

The Old Gallery

The Old Gallery

ul. Muzealna 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Getting there: The gallery sits five minutes' walk from Ratusz Arsenał metro station. Arrive slightly early — entry is timed in small waves to keep the halls quiet.

Accessibility: The venue offers step-free access at the main entrance, accessible restrooms on the ground floor, and a wheelchair-friendly route through all public galleries.

Parking & transport: Limited street parking is available nearby; public transport is strongly recommended. Bus lines 106 and 175 stop directly outside the north entrance.
