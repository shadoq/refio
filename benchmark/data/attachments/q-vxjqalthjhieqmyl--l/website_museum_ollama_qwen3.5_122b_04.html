The Experience

Step into a world where art breathes in the silence of the night.

Velvet Darkness

We transform the historic halls of The Old Gallery into an immersive sanctuary. No harsh lights, no crowds—only soft spotlights guiding you through curated installations.

Live ambient performances by renowned electronic artists blend with the architecture, creating a soundscape that reacts to your movement.

Culinary Artistry

Taste the night with exclusive cocktails and small plates designed by Warsaw's top mixologists, served in our hidden speakeasy lounge.
