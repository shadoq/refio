The Concept

Art has always
lived in darkness.

ARTIFACT is not a museum visit. It is a nocturnal ritual — a curated journey through galleries transformed by light, sound, and performance into something altogether different from what you know.

For one night, the collections come alive. Artists, musicians, and performers inhabit the spaces between the masterworks, creating new dialogues across centuries of human expression.

This is the museum as you have never experienced it: intimate, electric, and alive with the particular magic that only darkness can conjure.

The Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw

Built in 1887, The Old Gallery is Warsaw's most storied cultural institution. Its neo-classical facades conceal seven decades of radical artistic transformation within.

For ARTIFACT, the entire building — from the vaulted basement to the rooftop terrace — becomes a stage. Every corridor, every alcove, every stairwell is part of the experience.
