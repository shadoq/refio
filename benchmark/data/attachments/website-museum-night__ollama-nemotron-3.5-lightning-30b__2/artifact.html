Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night
18 October 2026 19:00–02:00
The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Concept

A nocturnal journey through art, history, and mystery. Experience the museum like never before under the cover of darkness.

Velvet Darkness

Immerse yourself in the hushed atmosphere of the museum after hours, where each gallery reveals new dimensions of the collection.

Golden Spotlights

Curated pools of golden light guide your path, highlighting masterpieces and creating intimate moments of discovery.

Quiet Mystery

Experience art in contemplative silence, undisturbed by the daytime crowds, where every corner holds a secret.

Program

19:00 – Opening

Welcome reception with champagne and live classical music in the main hall.

19:30 – Guided Tours

Expert-led tours through the permanent collection and special installations.

20:30 – Performance

Site-specific contemporary dance performance responding to the museum space.

21:30 – Late Night Viewing

Unlimited access to all galleries with ambient electronic soundscape.

02:00 – Closing

Final moments together as the night draws to a close.

Metro: Rondo ONZ, 5-minute walk. Parking available underground.

Accessibility

Ground floor access, wheelchair accessible restrooms, hearing loop available.

FAQ

Doors open at 19:00 on 18 October 2026. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early for check-in.
Smart casual recommended. No sportswear or beachwear. Some areas may be cool, so a light jacket is advisable.
Tickets are available at the door, but we recommend pre-booking to guarantee entry. VIP and Premium tiers may sell out.
The event is recommended for ages 16+. Some content may be mature. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.
The event is primarily indoors. In case of extreme weather, ticket holders will be notified via email with any changes.
