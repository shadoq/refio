ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

One night a year, the galleries go quiet and the shadows come alive — a cinematic wander through exhibitions, sound, and hidden rooms rarely opened to the public.

18 October 2026 19:00 – 02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

The Concept

An Exhibition After Dark

When the last visitors leave and the galleries fall silent, ARTIFACT begins. For one night, The Old Gallery is reclaimed by artists, composers, and curators who transform its halls into something between an exhibition, a performance, and a séance. Wander at your own pace — every room holds a different mood, every hour a different discovery.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Full access to the permanent collection and a rotating cast of temporary installations, lit only by candle and spotlight.

Live Performances

Ambient composers and vocalists perform inside the galleries, responding live to the art surrounding them.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group tours into restoration studios and archives never normally open to visitors.

The Venue

Find Your Way In

The Old Gallery

ul. Świętojerska 14, 00-233 Warsaw, Poland

Arrive by 19:00 to catch the Curator Welcome, or come later and slip in quietly — doors stay open until 23:30. Coat check is available in the main atrium, and the courtyard bar remains open throughout the night.

Accessibility: The Old Gallery offers step-free access throughout all public routes, accessible restrooms on the ground and first floors, and assistive listening devices available at the coat check desk on request.

Getting there: The nearest metro stop, Ratusz Arsenał, is a four-minute walk. Limited street parking is available nearby; we recommend public transport or ride-share, as the courtyard cannot accommodate vehicle drop-off after 19:00.
