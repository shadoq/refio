Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Oct 18, 2026 | 19:00–02:00 | The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A Night of Mystery

Step into a world where art comes alive after dark. ARTIFACT transforms the museum into an immersive experience of sound, light, and shadow. Join us for an evening that blurs the lines between past and present.

The Evening Unfolds

19:00 — Arrival & Welcome

Cocktails and ambient sounds as you enter the transformed gallery.

20:00 — The Opening

A curated performance piece exploring the silence of history.

21:30 — Live Soundscapes

Sonic installations responding to the masterpieces on display.

23:00 — Midnight Reflection

A guided meditation through the darkest, most intimate corners of the museum.

01:00 — Afterparty

DJ sets and late-night conversations in the main hall.

02:00 — Departure

The lights come up, and the night ends.
