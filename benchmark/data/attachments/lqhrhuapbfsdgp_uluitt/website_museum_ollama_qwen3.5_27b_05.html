ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined.

OCTOBER 18, 2026  |  19:00 – 02:00

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Get Tickets

The Night Gallery

In the silence of the night, art speaks louder. ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living canvas where history meets avant-garde.

We invite you to step beyond the velvet rope and experience an evening curated for the senses—immersive soundscapes, exclusive artist talks, and rare exhibitions unveiled only under moonlight.

The Program

A curated journey through the night.

19:00 – 19:30

The Opening Bell & Champagne Reception

Gather in the Grand Foyer. A toast to begin the evening, followed by a guided tour of the "Shadows" installation.

19:45 – 20:30

Lecture: "Artifacts of the Future"

A keynote by Dr. Elena Vance on how digital preservation is reshaping our understanding of physical history.

21:00 – 22:30

Silent Disco & Jazz Quartet

An experimental soundscape performance in the Sculpture Garden. Headphones provided for an immersive audio experience.

23:00 – 01:00

The Midnight Auction & Afterparty

A curated selection of limited edition prints available for purchase, followed by an exclusive DJ set in the main hall.
