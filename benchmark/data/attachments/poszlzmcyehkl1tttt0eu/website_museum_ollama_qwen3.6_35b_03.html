The Concept

Where Art Meets
the Darkness

ARTIFACT transforms a historic museum into an immersive nocturnal experience — blending curated exhibitions, live performances, and atmospheric design into one unforgettable evening.

🏛️

After-Hours Access

Explore the museum's permanent collection in an entirely new light — guided by darkness, illuminated by art.

🎭

Live Performances

Contemporary dancers, ambient musicians, and spoken-word artists perform throughout the galleries.

🍸

Cocktail Lounge

A curated bar in the museum's courtyard featuring signature cocktails inspired by the artworks on display.

🎧

Spatial Audio

Immersive soundscapes composed specifically for the architecture of the building, experienced through wireless headphones.

📸

Photo Installations

Three exclusive photo installations designed for the evening, each telling a hidden story of the museum's collection.

🌙

Rooftop Terrace

End your night on the rooftop terrace with panoramic views of Warsaw's illuminated skyline and a curated DJ set.

The Program

Evening Timeline

A carefully orchestrated journey through sound, movement, and visual art across the museum's historic spaces.

19:00 – 19:30
Arrival & Welcome
Guests receive wireless audio headsets and a curated map. Welcome champagne in the grand foyer.
19:30 – 20:30
Gallery Walk: Shadows & Light
A guided nocturnal tour through the Renaissance and Baroque galleries, illuminated by custom spotlight installations.
20:30 – 21:30
Performance: "Echoes"
Contemporary dance performance in the main hall, choreographed to respond to the architecture and lighting.
21:30 – 22:30
Keynote: "The Art of Darkness"
Dr. Elena Voss discusses the role of darkness in contemporary art and museum curation.
22:30 – 00:00
Cocktail Hour & Open Galleries
The museum opens freely. Live jazz in the courtyard, cocktail service, and spontaneous gallery encounters.
00:00 – 02:00
Rooftop: "After Dark"
DJ set on the rooftop terrace with panoramic city views. The night closes with a collective reflection.
