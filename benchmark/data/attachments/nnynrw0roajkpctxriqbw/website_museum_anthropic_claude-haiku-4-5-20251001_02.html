Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in each ticket tier?
▼
Standard tickets include full event access with opening reception, live performances, and gallery access. Premium adds VIP lounge access, priority seating, exclusive merchandise, and artist meet & greet. VIP includes everything in Premium plus a private viewing room, champagne reception, exclusive dinner, and personal concierge service.
Can I bring a guest?
▼
Each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring additional guests, you'll need to purchase separate tickets for each person. Group discounts are available for parties of 10 or more—please contact our team for details.
What time should I arrive?
▼
The event begins at 19:00 with the opening reception. We recommend arriving 15-30 minutes early to check in and settle in. The venue will remain open until 02:00, so you can arrive anytime during the evening.
Is there a dress code?
▼
We recommend smart casual to formal attire. The event has an elegant, sophisticated atmosphere. Comfortable shoes are advised as you'll be exploring multiple gallery spaces throughout the evening.
Can I get a refund?
▼
Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person. If you need to cancel, please contact our support team at least 7 days before the event for transfer options.
