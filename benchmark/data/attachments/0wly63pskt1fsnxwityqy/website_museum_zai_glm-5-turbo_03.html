The Experience

Step into a museum transformed. When the doors close to the public, ARTIFACT begins — an immersive night where art, sound, and architecture converge in the quiet mystery of darkness. Wander through illuminated galleries, discover hidden collections, and lose yourself in performances that blur the boundary between observer and artwork.

◈

After-Hours Exhibitions

Explore the gallery's permanent collection under dramatic new lighting, revealing details invisible during daytime visits.

♫

Live Performances

Ambient compositions, violin in darkness, and soundscapes designed to resonate within the museum's unique acoustics.

◇

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Access restricted vaults, restoration workshops, and storerooms rarely opened to the public, led by expert curators.

The Venue

A historic space that becomes part of the art.

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Getting There

Located in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, 10 minutes on foot from the Old Town.

Accessibility

The venue is wheelchair accessible. Elevators serve all floors. Tactile guides available at reception.

Public Transport

  • Metro: Ratusz Arsenał (Line M1, 5 min walk)
  • Bus: Routes 100, 175, 222 stop at Plac Zamkowy

Parking

Limited street parking available. We recommend public transport.
