The Concept

The concept of ARTIFACT

A museum is loudest when it is empty. We invite you to listen. — Helena Voss, Chief Curator

ARTIFACT is a single night in which The Old Gallery sheds its daylight manners. Rooms are relit by hand, forgotten objects are brought up from storage, and the building itself becomes an instrument — for sound, for light, for conversation.

Move at your own pace through after-hours exhibitions, stop for live performances staged between the sculptures, and join small guided groups into rooms that are usually locked. It is part exhibition, part film set, part séance — and it happens only once.

  • I

    After-hours exhibitions

    Every wing open until 02:00, relit with low, theatrical light that reveals textures daylight hides.

  • II

    Live performances

    Ambient sets, projection pieces and movement performed among the collection — never on a stage.

  • III

    Guided hidden-room tours

    Small groups follow our curators into the vaults and archives normally closed to visitors.

Program

Seven hours, eight moments

The night unfolds room by room. Arrive at doors or drift in later — every session is timed so you can follow the whole arc or choose your own path.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    Arrive to a candle-lit foyer, a welcome glass and a soft score written for the building's acoustics.

    Grand Foyer
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    Helena Voss opens the night beneath the dome with the story of the collection's forgotten pieces.

    Rotunda
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    Small guided groups descend into storage rooms and archives sealed to the public for decades.

    Lower Vaults
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    A slow, immersive set that uses the hall's natural reverb as a second instrument.

    Hall of Echoes
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Light-mapped projection transforms marble figures into moving, breathing narratives.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    An intimate conversation on making work for the dark, followed by open questions from the audience.

    Auditorium
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    At the stroke of midnight, the East Wing is lit by hand-held lanterns only. Walk slowly.

    East Wing
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    Under the open sky, a final hour of warm, low-frequency sound to carry you back into the city.

    Courtyard

    Venue

    The Old Gallery

    ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 15

    Travel tips

    • Arrive 15 minutes early for the Curator Welcome.
    • A free cloakroom is available in the Grand Foyer.
    • Comfortable shoes — the vaults have stone stairs.

    Accessibility

    • Step-free entrance via the side courtyard.
    • Lifts to all floors except parts of the Lower Vaults.
    • Quiet room and seating on every floor.

    Public transport

    • Metro M2 — Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet, 6 min walk.
    • Buses 116, 128, 175, 180 — stop “Uniwersytet”.
    • Night buses run hourly after 23:00.

    Parking

    • Street parking is very limited.
    • Galeria Parking garage — 400 m away.
    • Bike racks at the courtyard entrance.
