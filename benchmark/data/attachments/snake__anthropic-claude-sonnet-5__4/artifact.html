SNAKE

1 Human vs Human — Arrows control Snake A, WASD controls Snake B
2 CPU vs Human — CPU controls Snake A, you control Snake B (Arrows or WASD)
3 CPU vs CPU — Sit back and watch two CPUs battle for food
Both snakes always share the board and compete for the same food.
Avoid walls, your own body, and the other snake — any collision ends the game!
