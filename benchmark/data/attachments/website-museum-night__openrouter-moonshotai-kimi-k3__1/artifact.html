Artifact: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

For one night only, the Old Gallery stays awake — velvet darkness, golden spotlights, and the quiet mystery of art after hours.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Get Tickets

01 — The Concept

One night, the museum stays awake

When the last visitor leaves and the city quiets down, the Old Gallery lights its golden spotlights and opens again. ARTIFACT: Museum Night is an after-hours journey through exhibitions, performances and hidden rooms — a slow, cinematic evening wrapped in velvet darkness and quiet mystery.

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection and a special nocturnal hang, seen under low golden light — the way paintings were meant to be seen.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection mapping and a silent lantern walk — performances composed for the building itself, unfolding between the paintings.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups, one lantern, doors that are usually locked. Our guides open the storage rooms and tell the stories the labels never mention.

04 — The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A nineteenth-century picture gallery in the heart of the old town — stone stairs, tall doors, and rooms that hold the quiet of a hundred years of looking.

The Old Gallery

ul. Złota 12, 00-019 Warsaw, Poland

Getting there

Metro M2 to Świętokrzyska, then a 6-minute walk along Złota street. Trams 4, 15 and 35 stop at Plac Zawiszy, two minutes from the side entrance.

Parking

Limited evening parking in the courtyard (enter from Krucza street). We recommend public transport — the night ends at 02:00, and the last metro runs at 02:30.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance from Złota street, lifts to all floors, accessible restrooms on the ground floor. The hidden-room tour includes narrow stairs — an alternative route is available on request.

Cloakroom & bar

Free cloakroom in the foyer. A quiet wine bar in the East Gallery serves until 01:00.
