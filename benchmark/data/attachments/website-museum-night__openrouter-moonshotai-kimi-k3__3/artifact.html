ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

For one night only, the Old Gallery keeps its lamps burning — seven hours of art, sound and quiet wonder behind closed velvet doors.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

The Concept

One night the museum stays awake

When the last visitor leaves and the city quiets down, the Old Gallery opens again — into velvet darkness, golden spotlights and quiet mystery. Wander the halls after hours, hear music breathe between the canvases, and step into rooms the daylight never shows.

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection and a special nocturnal hang, lit for the dark — paintings as they were meant to be seen, by lamplight.

Live performances

Ambient composers, projection artists and movers perform among the masters — sound and light in dialogue with centuries.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups descend into the vault rooms, where rarely shown works wait in the half-dark for their annual audience.

Good to know

  • Arrive 15 minutes early — doors open at 19:00 sharp.
  • Cloakroom available at the entrance, free of charge.
  • Last entry at 23:30; re-entry is not possible.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance on ul. Królewska.
  • Lifts to all floors and accessible restrooms.
  • A quiet room is available throughout the night.

Getting there

  • Metro M2 — Świętokrzyska station, 6 minutes on foot.
  • Buses 175 and 128 stop directly outside.
  • Paid parking behind the gallery, entrance from ul. Widok.
