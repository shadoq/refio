06 / Good to know

A few answers.

Everything you need to know before the doors open.

What time does the event start and end?

Doors open at 19:00 on Sunday, 18 October 2026. The evening concludes at 02:00 on Monday, 19 October.

Can I arrive after the event begins?

Yes. You can arrive later in the evening, though we recommend coming early to enjoy the full program.

Is the event accessible?

The illustrative venue plan marks a step-free entrance. Because this is a demo event, please confirm access arrangements with the organizer before making travel plans.

Are food and drinks available?

The evening includes a gallery bar in the concept program. A welcome drink is included with Curator Pass and Patron Circle; other refreshments would be separate.

Can I take photographs?

Personal, non-flash photography is welcome where indicated. Please respect any artwork-specific restrictions and other visitors' space.

Are tickets refundable or transferable?

No tickets are actually sold on this demo site. For a real event, refund and transfer policies would be provided by the ticketing organizer before purchase.
