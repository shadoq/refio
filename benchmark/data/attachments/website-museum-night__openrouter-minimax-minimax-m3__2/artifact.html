The Concept

An evening that breathes.

ARTIFACT is not a gala, not a party, not a lecture. It is a slow, intentional descent into the gallery after the doors close — when the rooms grow quieter, the light turns gold, and the works begin to speak.

Curated like a film. Lit like a confession. Served like a secret.

After-hours exhibitions

Eight curated rooms opened exclusively for ARTIFACT guests. No crowds, no queues — only the work and the silence around it.

Live performances

Chamber string quartets, ambient electronics, and a single closing set beneath the rotunda — programmed to follow the room.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group access to the archive vault, the conservation lab, and the rooftop terrace — places the public rarely sees.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

A 19th-century neoclassical hall, restored in 2019, with seven interconnecting rooms and a private rotunda. Originally a private collection, the building has been a working gallery since 1973.

  • Address ul. Krucza 16/22, 00-526 Warszawa, Poland
  • Getting there Metro: Centrum (Line 1) — 6 min walk. Tram: lines 7, 9, 22 stop at Hoża.
  • Accessibility Step-free access via the side entrance on Hoża. Accessible restroom on every floor. Service animals welcome.
  • Parking Limited street parking after 18:00. Public transport strongly recommended; a taxi rank sits at Plac Konstytucji.

FAQ

Before you arrive.

All tickets include entry to the eight after-hours galleries and the evening's full program. Curator and Patron passes add reserved seating, a hidden-room tour, and additional intimate sessions.
Doors open at 19:00. We recommend arriving between 19:00 and 19:30 to take in the foyer reception before the opening remarks at 19:30.
Yes. The side entrance on Hoża is step-free, and accessible restrooms are available on every floor. Please contact us in advance if you require any specific accommodation.
Non-flash photography is permitted in most rooms, but a few works are on restricted display. Look for the small camera-crossed icon on the wall label before photographing.
There is no enforced dress code. Most guests wear smart-casual evening attire — think dark, considered, and comfortable enough to stand and walk through eight rooms.
Tickets are refundable up to 14 days before the event. Within 14 days, we are happy to transfer your ticket to a friend — just let us know their name and email in advance.
A welcome drink is included with every ticket. A small selection of wine, non-alcoholic drinks, and light bites is available at the foyer bar throughout the evening.
Yes. Cloakroom is complimentary for all tiers. Patron Pass holders receive priority access and a complimentary late taxi service when the event ends at 02:00.
