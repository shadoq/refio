The Experience

ARTIFACT is more than an event — it is an immersion into the quiet mystery of a museum after dark. As daylight fades, the galleries transform: spotlights carve new paths through centuries of art, live performances echo through marble halls, and hidden rooms open their doors for one night only. This is culture reimagined — intimate, cinematic, and unforgettable.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Marszałkowska 3, 00-624 Warszawa, Poland

Travel Tips

Located in the heart of Warsaw's cultural district. The entrance faces the main courtyard.

Accessibility

The venue is wheelchair accessible with ramps at all entrances. Accessible restrooms available on each floor. An elevator services all exhibition levels.

Parking

Limited street parking available. Recommended: Parkowanie Centralna garage, 5 min walk.

Public Transport

Metro: Centrum station (3 min walk). Tram: lines 7, 9, 22, 24 — stop: Centrum. Bus: lines 100, 175, 222 — stop: Dworzec Centralny.
