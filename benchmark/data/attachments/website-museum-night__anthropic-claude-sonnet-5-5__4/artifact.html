Museum Night,Reimagined

One night. Seven hours. The doors of The Old Gallery stay open after dark — for light, sound, sculpture and the quiet mystery of art without the crowds.

The Concept

When the galleries exhale

After closing time, a museum becomes another place entirely. Corridors soften, spotlights narrow, and every canvas seems to hold its breath. ARTIFACT invites you inside for a single night — to wander freely through the collection, to hear music written for these very rooms, and to step behind doors that stay locked all year.

I

After-hours exhibitions

Four wings, lit by hand. See the permanent collection and three new installations in a hush that daytime never allows.

II

Live performances

Ambient composers, choreographers and projection artists transform marble halls into living, breathing stages.

III

Guided hidden-room tours

Curators lead small groups through archives, restoration studios and vaults rarely seen by the public.

Program

Seven hours, hour by hour

From the first opened door to the last note at 02:00 — a night composed like a film in eight scenes.

Venue

The Old Gallery

A neoclassical building from 1897, recently restored, with marble halls, a glass-roofed atrium and a sculpture hall that comes alive after dark.

Address

The Old Gallery, ul. Galeryjna 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Travel tips

Walk from Old Town in about 12 minutes. Taxis and ride-hailing can drop off directly at the main entrance on Galeryjna Street.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, lifts to all floors, accessible restrooms and quiet seating. Assistance available on request at the door.

Parking & public transport

Metro M1 “Galeria” — 4 min walk. Night buses N25 and N44 stop at “Stara Galeria”. Limited paid parking nearby; we recommend public transport.
