ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night,
Reimagined

For one night, the lights dim, the doors stay open, and the museum becomes something else entirely — exhibitions, performances and hidden rooms, from dusk until 2 a.m.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Get Tickets

01 — Concept

The Museum After Dark

When the final daytime visitor leaves and the gallery lights sink to a whisper, ARTIFACT begins. For seven hours, The Old Gallery opens its vaults, darkened halls and hidden rooms for a single curated night — a museum reimagined as a living artwork, moved through rather than looked at.

After-hours Exhibitions

Dimmed galleries, single-work spotlights and the vault collection shown by night — the museum at its quietest and most cinematic.

Live Performances

An ambient live score, projection mapping on stone and a wordless midnight walk — sound and light woven through the halls.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group tours into rooms the public never sees: the vault wing, the restoration studio and the curator's own archive.

04 — Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A 19th-century armoury turned gallery — five minutes from the river, one from the metro.

The Old Gallery · plac Komandorii 12, 00-101 Warsaw — main entrance on Komandorii St.

Getting Here

Metro line M2 to Komandoria, then a 4-minute walk. Trams 4, 15 and 20 stop at the door. The last M2 towards the centre leaves at 01:40 — conveniently mid-closing-set.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance on Komandorii St, lifts to every floor, accessible restrooms on each level, and quiet seating in the Rotunda. Guide dogs welcome; the midnight walk can be taken at a slower, seated pace.

Parking

On-site parking is limited to 40 spaces and must be pre-booked. We recommend public transport — night buses serve the square every 20 minutes until 3 a.m.
