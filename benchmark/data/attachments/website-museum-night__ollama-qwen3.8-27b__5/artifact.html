The Concept

A night where the museum breathes

When the last visitor leaves, the collection does not sleep. ARTIFACT: Museum Night opens the doors of The Old Gallery after dark — a single, cinematic evening of exhibitions, live performance, and quiet discovery. Move through halls usually closed to the public, hear original scores composed for the space, and share the night with the artists behind the work.

After-hours exhibitions

See the permanent collection under a different light, after the crowds have gone.

Live performances

Original scores for strings and electronics, composed for the halls themselves.

Guided hidden-room tours

Works rarely shown to the public, revealed by the curators who keep them.
