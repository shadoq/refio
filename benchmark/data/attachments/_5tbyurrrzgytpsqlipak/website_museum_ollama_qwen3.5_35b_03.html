The Night Collection

ARTIFACT transforms the museum into a living organism after hours. Walk through galleries bathed in golden spotlights, listen to live cello performances echoing off marble floors, and taste rare wines paired with historical narratives.

  • 🌑 Velvet darkness & golden spotlights
  • 🎻 Live acoustic performances in the halls
  • 🍷 Exclusive vintage wine tasting

The Night's Flow

A curated timeline of sensory experiences.

19:00 Arrival & Welcome Drink

Guests enter through the grand hall. A welcome glass of vintage sparkling wine is served as ambient sounds begin to fill the space.

19:45 Guided Shadow Walks

Small groups follow curators through the main galleries, exploring masterpieces in low-light conditions to reveal new textures.

21:00 Live Cello & Jazz Fusion

The sculpture garden transforms into a concert hall. A fusion of classical cello and modern jazz creates an immersive soundscape.

01:00 Midnight Toast & Closing

A final toast to the art of history. The event concludes with a guided exit through the illuminated gardens.
