Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night

18 October 2026
19:00–02:00
The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Concept

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms the traditional museum experience into a cinematic journey through art after dark. Explore hidden collections, encounter live performances, and wander through illuminated halls where sculpture, sound, and shadow converge.

After-hours Exhibitions

Experience the permanent collection under candlelight and spotlight, with curated narratives only revealed at night.

Live Performances

Immersive ambient music, spoken word, and dance performances respond to the artworks in real time.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Discover secret chambers and restored archives, accessible only during this exclusive event.

Travel Tips

Public transport is highly recommended. The gallery is a 5-minute walk from the Warsaw Metro 'Ratusz' station. Limited parking available.

Accessibility

The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with elevators to all levels. Sign language interpreters available upon request.

Parking & Transport

There is a paid underground parking garage 200m away. Bicycle racks are available at the entrance. A free shuttle bus runs every 20 minutes from Warsaw Central Station.

FAQ

Is payment processed on this page?
No, this is a demo landing page. No real payment is processed. Your reservation is held for 24 hours and requires confirmation via email.
Can I arrive late?
Yes, entry is allowed until 22:00. After that, access is restricted to ensure the flow of the evening’s program.
Are photos allowed?
Photography is permitted for personal use, but flash and tripods are prohibited. Some exhibits are marked "No Photography" — please respect these signs.
Is the venue accessible?
Yes, the venue is fully wheelchair accessible with elevators to all levels. Sign language interpreters are available upon request — please notify us when you book.
Can I change my ticket tier?
Yes, you can upgrade your ticket up to 48 hours before the event. Downgrades are not permitted. Contact us at info@artifactmuseumnight.com for assistance.
How do I save the event to my calendar?
Click the "Save to Calendar" button on the hero section. A .ics file will download automatically, which you can import into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
