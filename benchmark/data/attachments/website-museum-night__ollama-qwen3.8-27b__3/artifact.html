The Concept

A night composed for the collection

When the city falls quiet, the gallery becomes a stage. ARTIFACT is a single, slow evening — sound mapped to stone, light traced across bronze, and rooms opened that stay closed by day. Come for the art; stay for the silence between it.

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Aleje Jerozolimskie 42

00-024 Warsaw, Poland

  • Step-free access throughout, with accessible restrooms on every level.
  • A quiet room is available for guests who need a calmer space.
  • Nearest metro: Centrum (Line M2), a four-minute walk from the entrance.
  • Tram and bus stops line the avenue; the gallery sits directly on the route.
  • Secure parking is available in the adjacent courtyard, subject to availability.
  • Doors close at 01:00; the final set ends as the night dissolves.
