The Concept

One Night. Eight Hours. A Museum Transformed.

After dark, the Old Gallery becomes a cinematic labyrinth of velvet darkness and golden spotlights. Visitors move freely between rehung exhibitions, live performances and rooms that are usually locked. There is no fixed route and no closing bell — only a single ticket and one continuous night. Come early for the quiet, stay late for the sound.

After-Hours Exhibitions

The permanent collection rehung in darkness, lit only by curated spotlights. See familiar works as if for the first time.

Live Performances

Ambient sets, projection art and chamber music staged in the sculpture hall and the marble atrium.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group tours into the archive and restoration rooms that never open to the public.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, after dark

A nineteenth-century gallery in central Warsaw, reopened for one night with its lights low and its doors unlocked. Everything you need to find it, enter it and move through it is below.

The Old Gallery

ul. Hoża 42, 00-516 Warsaw, Poland

  • Address ul. Hoża 42, 00-516 Warsaw, Poland. Main entrance on Hoża; late-night entrance on the courtyard side.
  • Travel tips Metro Politechnika (M1) — 6 min walk. Tram 15, 18, 35 stop Hoża. Night bus N34 runs until 03:00.
  • Accessibility Step-free entrance on the Hoża side, lift to all floors, induction loops in Hall A, accessible WC on levels 0 and 2.
  • Parking Paid underground garage at Plac Konstytucji, 4 min walk. Limited street parking after 20:00.
  • Cloakroom Free, and mandatory for large bags and umbrellas.
