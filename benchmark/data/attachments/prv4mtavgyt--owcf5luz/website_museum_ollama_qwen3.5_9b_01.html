The Old Gallery

Nestled in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, The Old Gallery stands as a testament to architectural brilliance and artistic heritage. Originally built in 1892, this magnificent venue has been meticulously restored to preserve its grandeur while embracing contemporary design.

Our space features soaring ceilings, original ornate moldings, and state-of-the-art lighting systems that transform the gallery throughout the evening. The intimate yet expansive layout ensures an optimal viewing experience for all our exhibitions.

Accessibility features include wheelchair access, elevators, and designated parking. We're committed to making art accessible to everyone.

Address: ul. Artystyczna 15, 00-123 Warsaw, Poland

Capacity: Up to 300 guests
