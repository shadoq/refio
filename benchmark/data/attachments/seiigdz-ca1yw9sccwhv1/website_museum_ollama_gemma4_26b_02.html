The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw, is a sanctuary of history. For one night only, its stone corridors and vaulted ceilings will be transformed into a canvas of light and shadow.

Located in the heart of the historic district, the venue offers an atmosphere of unparalleled prestige and mystery.
