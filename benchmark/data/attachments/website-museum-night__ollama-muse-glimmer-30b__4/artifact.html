Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night
18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Velvet darkness. Golden light.

An after-hours immersion where art breathes without walls. Curated installations, live sound, and intimate encounters with the collection. Dress code: dark elegance.

ARTIFACT invites you to move through the museum as a living archive. Each room is a chapter, each artist a guide. No crowds, no glass — only presence.
