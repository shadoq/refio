The Concept

When the museum keeps its doors open after dark

For one night each autumn, the galleries of The Old Gallery set aside their daytime rhythm and welcome visitors into a different world. Lights are lowered, corridors become quiet stages, and objects that usually wait behind glass are suddenly part of a shared, nocturnal conversation.

ARTIFACT: Museum Night invites you to wander at your own pace, between exhibitions, performances, talks and tours, until the building falls silent again at dawn.

After-hours exhibitions

Main collections are opened in a new light, with extended rooms and quiet corners made for lingering. Objects are presented with fresh juxtapositions that reward slow looking.

Live performances

Musicians, media artists and performers share the halls throughout the night. Sound and movement shift from room to room as the evening unfolds.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups follow curators through rooms rarely open to the public. Each tour uncovers the stories behind objects that seldom leave storage.

Schedule

Program

Eight moments across the night, from the first welcome at dusk to a closing session under the stars.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    The galleries open to ticket holders and the evening begins with a quiet walk through the Main Hall.

    Main Hall
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    Lead curator Elena Marszałek introduces the night's themes and the hidden rooms waiting beyond the velvet ropes.

    East Wing
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    A guided walk through rooms rarely opened to the public, with stories behind each object.

    Gallery 3
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    Sound and light merge in an hour-long set that fills the building's stone halls.

    Atrium
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Large-scale projections animate the sculptures as the lights dim slowly around the marble.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    Artists discuss their work, their process and what it means to make art in the dark.

    Lecture Room
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    A silent, candlelit walk through the exhibition halls, guided by the museum's historians.

    Rotunda
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    The night winds down on the Garden Terrace with a low-key session beneath the open sky.

    Garden Terrace

Travel tips

  • Arrive before 20:00 to catch the Curator Welcome.
  • Coat check is available in the Atrium all night.
  • Late entry is open until 00:30.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance from the street.
  • Lift serving all exhibition floors.
  • Accessible restrooms on every level.
  • Assistance dogs welcome. Quiet hours available on request.

Parking & public transport

  • Metro station Centrum, about a 5 minute walk.
  • Tram and bus lines stop directly on Muzealna Street.
  • Paid parking is available in nearby garages.
