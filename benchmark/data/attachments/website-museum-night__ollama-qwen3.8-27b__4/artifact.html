The Concept

An evening where the museum breathes

When the last visitor leaves, the collection comes alive. ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a single, carefully choreographed evening — part exhibition, part performance, part quiet discovery — designed to be felt as much as seen.

After-hours exhibitions

Selected works presented in a new light, after the crowds have gone.

Live performances

Immersive soundscapes and projection performed inside the halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Step into wings and storerooms that rarely open to the public.

Program

The evening, hour by hour

A single night, eight movements. Arrive early, stay late — the program flows from welcome to a closing sound session in the courtyard.

Artists & Speakers

The voices of the night

Curators, composers, sculptors, and sound designers — the people shaping what you will see and hear.

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Plac Muzealny 3, 00-042 Warsaw, Poland

A 19th-century gallery building in the heart of the Old Town, restored for after-hours cultural events.

  • Public transport — Metro line M2 (Starówka) and tram 9 stop within a 3-minute walk.
  • Parking — Limited on-site; the Muzeum underground garage is 200m away.
  • Accessibility — Step-free access, accessible restrooms, and a quiet room on the ground floor.
  • Travel tip — Arrive by 19:00 for the welcome drinks; the courtyard is the best place to start.
