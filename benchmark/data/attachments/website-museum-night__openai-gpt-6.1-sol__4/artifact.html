Before you arrive

A few things
worth knowing.

A little clarity, so you can focus on the experience. This is a fictional event website, not an active ticket service.

Is there a real payment or ticket purchase?

No. This page is an interactive demonstration. Ticket selection produces an on-screen simulated reservation only. No card details are requested, no payment is collected, and no actual ticket or confirmation email is issued.

Can I arrive later in the evening?

In the event concept, entry begins at 19:00 and late arrivals are welcome before the final session. Scheduled tours and performances have specific start times. No actual admission policy is in effect because the event is fictional.

Can I take photographs?

The imagined experience welcomes discreet, non-flash photography in the main galleries, while performances and archive rooms would have their own guidance. These are conceptual policies; always follow the published rules of a real museum.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free entry, quiet seating, and accessible circulation are part of the design concept, but the venue is fictional and these facilities are not verified. The venue section explains the illustrative access notes. A real event should provide confirmed accessibility information before booking.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Choose any pass, then use the ticket-tier dropdown inside the demo reservation dialog. The total updates immediately. You can also close the dialog and select another tier; nothing is booked or persisted.

How does Save to Calendar work?

The button downloads an .ics file for 18 October 2026, 19:00, through 19 October, 02:00. The file uses floating local times, intended as Warsaw local time. Import it into your calendar and check its time-zone interpretation, especially if you are outside Warsaw.

What happens to the details I enter?

Your name and email are used only to validate the demo form in this browser tab. They are not sent anywhere or saved in browser storage. Closing the ticket dialog clears the form. The newsletter is also simulated and sends no data.
