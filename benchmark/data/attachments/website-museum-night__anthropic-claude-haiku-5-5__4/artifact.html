The Concept

When the galleries open after dark

For one night the museum belongs to those who stay past closing. Lights are lowered, rooms reveal their secrets, and every corridor holds a different kind of silence.

ARTIFACT: Museum Night turns the familiar halls of The Old Gallery into a cinematic journey. Expect exhibitions seen in new light, performances that drift between rooms, and conversations with the people who shaped the collection.

After-hours exhibitions

Main galleries stay open into the night, with lighting designed to bring out texture, depth and detail.

Live performances

Ambient sound, movement and projection sessions unfold across the halls throughout the evening.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups are led through rooms usually closed to the public, guided by curators and archivists.

Where

Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Przykładowa 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

The Old Gallery sits in a restored nineteenth-century building in the city centre. Enter through the main doors on the square and follow the signs to the Rotunda.

Travel tips

  • Arrive before 21:00 for the calmest entry.
  • Cloakroom available in the Rotunda; small bags only in the galleries.
  • Late entry is possible until midnight.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance from the square.
  • Accessible restrooms on every floor.
  • Quiet zones available in the Rotunda and Courtyard.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome.

Getting here

  • Metro: the Old Town station is a five-minute walk away.
  • Bus: stop “Gallery Square” is directly outside the venue.
  • Parking: paid parking is available in the nearby multi-storey car park.
