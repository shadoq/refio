ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night,
Reimagined

For one night only, the lights dim, the doors stay open, and the collection wakes. A cinematic journey through art after dark.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Get Tickets
The Concept

When the doors close at six, the museum falls silent. On 18 October, it doesn't.

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a single evening in which The Old Gallery remains open long after dark — galleries lit by candle-warm spotlights, storage rooms opened to the public, and sound drifting through the sculpture court. Wander freely, or follow the curated night program from the curator's welcome to the closing session at 2 AM.

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection rehung under low, cinematic light — works you've seen by day, transformed by night.

Live performances

Ambient sets, a midnight gallery walk, and a closing sound session composed for the building's acoustics.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group visits into the storage wing and conservation studio — rooms the public rarely enters.

Venue

The Old Gallery

A nineteenth-century gallery building in the heart of Warsaw, opened end to end for one night.

Address

Plac Malachowskiego 1, 00-076 Warsaw, Poland. Main entrance on the plaza side, steps from the fountain.

Getting Here

Metro line M1 to Świętokrzyska (7 min walk), trams 4/15/35 to Plac Małachowskiego. Night buses 601 and 605 run after midnight.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, elevator to all floors, accessible restrooms on level 0, and a quiet room available all night.

Parking

Limited street parking after 20:00. The underground parking at Plac Defilad is a 10-minute walk and open until 03:00.
