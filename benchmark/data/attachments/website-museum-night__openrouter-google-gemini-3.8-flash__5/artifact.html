“Where classical grandeur yields to velvety midnight reverie.”

The Concept

Velvet Darkness, Golden Spotlights

ARTIFACT dissolves the solemn boundaries of traditional museum spaces. For seven hours, marble corridors resonate with experimental synthesis, monolithic light sculptures, and meditative choreography.

🏛️

Classical Heritage

Over four hundred neoclassical artifacts, busts, and Renaissance arches cast into cinematic focus under warm amber beams.

⚡

Sonic Architecture

Quadraphonic audio arrays positioned throughout vaulted halls delivering custom ambient, drone, and rhythmic soundscapes.

🍷

Midnight Salon

Curated botanical elixirs, vintage wine selections, and conversational salon lounges tucked inside historical reading rooms.

The Nocturnal Schedule

Hour by Hour Descent

Follow the flow of the evening from early dusk reception through midnight crescendo and dawn meditation.

Grand Atrium

Dusk Reception & Glassware

21:45 — 23:15

23:30 — 00:45

01:00 — 02:00

Quiet Reverie & Afterglow

Subtle ambient frequencies, warm spiced tea infusions, and solitary viewing under Warsaw starlight.

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Constructed in 1888 along the Royal Route, The Old Gallery boasts monumental marble columns, coffered ceilings, and labyrinthine lower vaults rarely accessible to the general public.

Clarifications

Frequently Answered

Essential guidance regarding entry, dress expectations, and venue policies.

To preserve the atmospheric immersion and respect for performers, flash photography and tripods are strictly prohibited. Discrete, non-flash mobile captures are allowed except inside Vault 4B during dark sets.

We encourage dark evening wear, velvet textures, classical tailoring, or artistic monochrome black. Athletic attire, shorts, and flip-flops are not permitted within gallery rooms.

Yes. The Grand Atrium, Rotunda, and primary galleries are fully wheelchair accessible via elevator ramps. Vault 4B has dedicated lift assistance on site. Please alert staff upon arrival.

Tickets may be transferred to another individual up to 48 hours prior to the event date through your confirmation portal. Refunds are available until 14 days before 18 October 2026.

Patron and Benefactor ticket tiers include drink tokens and sommelier service. General pass holders can purchase drinks from our bespoke cocktail salon throughout the evening.

Doors remain open until 00:30 CET. However, during seated spatial sound concerts in Vault 4B, entry between movements will be staggered to prevent ambient disturbance.
