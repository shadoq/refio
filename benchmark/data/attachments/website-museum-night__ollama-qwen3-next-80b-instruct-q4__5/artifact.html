The Experience

ARTIFACT: Museum Night invites you into a transformed space where art breathes in the silence of darkness. This is not a typical night out—it’s a curated journey through hidden corridors, whispered stories, and luminous performances that turn the gallery into a living canvas. Each moment is designed to linger in memory long after the doors close.

After-hours Exhibitions

Experience curated collections illuminated only by soft spotlights, revealing new dimensions in every brushstroke and sculpture.

Live Performances

Immersive soundscapes, ambient music, and choreographed movement that respond to the space and the silence of the night.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Discover secret chambers and forgotten collections only accessible during this exclusive event.

Can I arrive late? ▼

Yes, you may arrive at any time during the event hours (19:00–02:00). However, some experiences, like the hidden tour, may have limited capacity and fill up early.

Are photos allowed? ▼

Non-flash photography is permitted for personal use. Tripods, professional equipment, and flash photography are prohibited to preserve the ambiance and protect artworks.

Is the venue accessible? ▼

Yes, the entire venue is wheelchair accessible. Elevators serve all floors. Assistive listening devices are available at the entrance upon request.

Can I change my ticket tier? ▼

Yes, you may upgrade your ticket at the entrance on the night of the event, subject to availability. Downgrades are not permitted.

Elena Vasquez

Visual Artist

Elena Vasquez transforms archival photographs into immersive light installations that explore memory and loss. Her work has been exhibited in the Tate Modern, MoMA, and the Venice Biennale. For this event, she will unveil a new series based on the museum’s 19th-century portrait collection, reimagined through dynamic projection mapping.

Session: Midnight Gallery Walk

Kaito Tanaka

Sound Composer

Kaito Tanaka creates ambient scores using resonant materials from the museum’s collection—bronze bells, wooden panels, and stone fragments—to produce soundscapes that respond to the architecture and the movement of visitors. His work blurs the line between sculpture and sound.

Session: Live Ambient Performance

Marina Dubois

Sculptor

Marina Dubois specializes in kinetic sculptures that respond to visitor movement through subtle magnetic and mechanical systems. Her installations invite contemplation through slow, almost imperceptible motion—echoing the quietude of the museum’s nighttime atmosphere.

Session: Sculpture Hall Projection

Dr. Rajiv Mehta

Curator

Dr. Rajiv Mehta led the curation of the museum’s most controversial exhibit, "Echoes of Empire," which sparked international debate. His insights into the political and social histories embedded in art make him one of the most compelling voices in contemporary curation.

Session: Curator Welcome

Lena Petrova

Installation Artist

Lena Petrova uses mirrors and fog to create infinite spatial illusions that challenge perception. Her installations in the Old Gallery will transform the main hall into a labyrinth of reflections, where visitors lose track of time and space.

Session: Midnight Gallery Walk

Javier Morales

Performance Director

Javier Morales choreographs silent, slow-motion performances in empty galleries. His performers move with deliberate, meditative grace, becoming living sculptures themselves. Each performance is unique, shaped by the quiet energy of the night.

Session: Live Ambient Performance

Aisha Nkosi

Historian

Aisha Nkosi reveals the forgotten stories behind 19th-century collectors—the private passions, clandestine acquisitions, and suppressed histories that shaped museum collections. Her research uncovers the hidden narratives behind the art we see today.

Session: Hidden Collection Tour

Oliver Chen

Light Designer

Oliver Chen designs the dynamic lighting system for the entire event, using custom LEDs and programmable spotlights to create a living atmosphere that evolves throughout the night. His work is invisible to the eye but essential to the emotional journey of the experience.

Session: All Events
