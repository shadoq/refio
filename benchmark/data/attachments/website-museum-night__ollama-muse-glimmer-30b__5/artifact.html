A night of quiet mystery

Step into a curated after-hours experience where architecture, sound and performance converge. Limited capacity, intimate rooms, and a single night only.

Curated Rooms

Eight immersive installations guided by light and scent. Each room tells a fragment of a larger story.

Live Sound

Original scores performed in situ. Acoustic sets, field recordings, and spatial audio designed for the gallery.

Artist Talks

Intimate conversations with creators between 22:00–00:30. No stages, just proximity.

Small bags allowed. Large bags must be stored in cloakroom.

Photography?

Non-flash photography allowed in designated areas only.
