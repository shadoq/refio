ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Step into a museum after dark. Seven hours of exhibitions, live performances, artist talks, and guided hidden-room tours — staged in velvet darkness and golden light.

Get Tickets
The Concept

A museum that only wakes at night

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living, breathing cinematic space. When the doors close to the public, ours open. Wander through dimly lit halls, encounter performances in unexpected corners, and let the collection reveal itself in a new light.

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection and special installations with the lights low and the crowds gone.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection art, and intimate sound sessions staged across the gallery halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group tours unlock the archive wing and rooms rarely seen by the public.

Getting there

  • Metro Politechnika — 6 min walk
  • Tram lines 4, 15, 18 — Marszałkowska stop
  • Bus lines 128, 175 — Plac Konstytucji

Parking

Paid parking is available at the underground garage on ul. Śniadeckich, a 3-minute walk from the entrance. Limited street parking after 20:00.

Accessibility

The venue is fully step-free. Wheelchair-accessible entrances, lifts, and restrooms are available on all floors. Companion tickets are free — contact us in advance.

Travel tips

Arrive between 19:00 and 20:00 to catch the curator welcome. The rooftop terrace can be cool at night — we recommend a light layer.
