Reimagining the Night

Elise Varga

Elise Varga is a groundbreaking cellist whose performances fuse classical technique with immersive light design. Her work has been featured at the Tate Modern, the Louvre, and the Museum of Contemporary Art in Los Angeles.

For ARTIFACT: Museum Night, she will perform a specially composed piece titled "Nocturne in D Minor," synchronized with projections of ancient manuscripts and celestial maps, creating a dialogue between sound and visual history.

Her album "Echoes in the Dark" received the 2025 Grammy for Best Contemporary Classical Composition.

Dr. Lena Petrova

Dr. Lena Petrova is Chief Curator of the National Museum of Modern Art and a leading voice in the study of Eastern European conceptual art. Her groundbreaking exhibition "The Silence of the Cold War" traveled to 12 international venues.

For ARTIFACT: Museum Night, Dr. Petrova will lead a curated walkthrough of the 'Echoes of Silence' exhibition, revealing hidden narratives in artworks that were censored during the 20th century. Her approach combines archival research with sensory storytelling, inviting visitors to experience art as a living archive.

She is the author of "Night Archives: Hidden Histories in Eastern European Art" (2024), a seminal text in contemporary curatorial studies.

Kaito Yamada

Kaito Yamada is a contemporary Japanese painter who bridges traditional sumi-e ink techniques with digital technology. His work has been exhibited at the Mori Art Museum and the Venice Biennale.

For ARTIFACT: Museum Night, Kaito will create a new piece titled "Midnight Ink" live on a 3m x 2m silk canvas under moonlight. The painting will respond to ambient sound levels and visitor movement, with ink patterns evolving in real-time through sensors embedded in the floor.

His technique, which he calls "Sonic Sumi," has been described as "the poetry of chance made visible."

Dr. Aris Thorne

Dr. Aris Thorne is Professor of Aesthetics at Oxford University and a leading philosopher of perception. His work explores how darkness and silence shape our experience of art.

In his lecture "The Philosophy of Night in Art," Dr. Thorne will argue that true aesthetic contemplation occurs not in daylight, but in the quiet, unlit spaces where the mind is free from distraction. He will draw on examples from Caravaggio, Rothko, and contemporary artists to demonstrate how darkness functions as a canvas for the imagination.

His book "The Silence Between Brushstrokes" (2023) is required reading in art theory programs worldwide.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Originally built in 1898 as a private art collection space, The Old Gallery was converted into a public museum in 1922. Its neoclassical architecture, vaulted ceilings, and original gaslight fixtures create an unparalleled atmosphere for an after-hours cultural experience.

Access is limited to ticket holders. The venue is fully accessible with elevators and tactile guides. Please note: photography is permitted only in designated areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this event suitable for children?

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is designed for adults 18+. The immersive nature of the event, including ambient soundscapes and late hours, makes it less suitable for children. We recommend our daytime family programs instead.

Can I take photographs?

Photography is permitted in designated areas only. Flash photography and tripods are strictly prohibited to preserve the integrity of the artworks and the experience of other guests. Please check signage at each gallery.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with elevators, accessible restrooms, and tactile guides. We also offer audio descriptions for visually impaired guests. Please contact us in advance to arrange accommodations.

What should I wear?

While there is no strict dress code, we encourage guests to dress in elegant evening attire. The venue is cool and dimly lit, so we recommend bringing a light jacket or shawl.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable but may be transferred to another person with 48 hours' notice. Please contact our support team at support@artifactmuseumnight.com for assistance.

Will there be food and drink?

Yes, complimentary cocktails and tea will be served throughout the evening. A limited selection of light bites will be available at the café area. Dietary restrictions can be accommodated with advance notice.
