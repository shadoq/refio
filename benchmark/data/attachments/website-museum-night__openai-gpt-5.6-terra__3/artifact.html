The proposition

The museum, unbuttoned.

ARTIFACT is a slow-burning evening designed for looking closer. The galleries become a living score of new commissions, concealed performances, and generous pauses.

01

After-hours access

Wander the permanent collection without the daylight rush, from the vaulted atrium to the archive below.

02

Art in motion

Eight artists activate the building with sound, moving image, choreography, and new site-specific work.

03

Room for wonder

Follow a suggested route or lose it completely. Every turn offers a different way into the night.

18 October · 19:00–02:00

The night unfolds.

Arrive when you wish; stay as long as the building keeps its secrets. All events are included with your ticket unless noted.

19:00

Doors & first light

Grand Atrium

Collect your night map, take in the opening installation, and begin at your own pace.

19:30

Velvet Frequencies

East Gallery

A live ambient set by Irena Sokołowska threads through a room of early modern paintings.

20:00

Curator’s provocation

Sculpture Hall

Dr. Marek Lis asks what changes when we meet a familiar object after dark.

20:45

Threshold

North Stair

A processional work by the Kinetic Assembly, performed among the marble landings.

21:30

Archive Nocturne

Reading Room

Rare books, whispered texts, and a continuous candlelit reading by Tomasz Nowak.

22:30

Midnight Cinema

Lecture Theatre

An expanded-film commission by Ada Zielińska with live 16mm projection.

00:00

Gold Room Session

West Salon

DJ Luma turns a collection of field recordings into an intimate dance-floor intervention.

01:30

Last illumination

Grand Atrium

A final collective sound work, then a quiet exit into the Warsaw night.
