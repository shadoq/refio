The Concept

Where art wakes up
after dark.

ARTIFACT is a single-night immersion into the museum after hours — a carefully curated collision of contemporary art, live performance, and nocturnal atmosphere.

Wander through galleries lit by golden spotlights. Encounter installations that respond to the night. Listen to artists speak from the rooms their work inhabits.

"The museum at night is a different creature — quieter, stranger, more honest."

One evening. One building. Twenty artists. An experience you won't forget.

Explore Tickets
The Experience

What awaits you

A night built around five pillars — each one designed to transform how you encounter art.

Golden Hour Galleries

Every gallery re-lit with warm golden spotlights. The familiar becomes unfamiliar — shadows deepen, colors shift, and each room feels like a private viewing.

Live Artist Encounters

Eight artists will be present throughout the night — available for conversation, demonstration, and impromptu performance in the galleries where their work lives.

Curated Soundscapes

A bespoke audio journey moves through the building — ambient compositions by invited composers, tuned to each gallery's atmosphere and artwork.

Late-Night Installations

Three site-specific installations open only after 23:00. Access is exclusive to ticket holders — these works exist only for this one night.

Café & Bar

A curated late-night menu by Warsaw's most sought-after underground bar. Signature cocktails named after artworks in the collection. Coffee until 01:30.

Collectible Program

Every ticket includes a limited-edition printed program — 24 pages, letterpress-printed, with essays by the artists and a map of the night's journey through the museum.

The Program

One night, carefully sequenced

From the first golden hour to the final installation at 02:00 — every moment has been composed.

19:00

Doors Open — Golden Hour

The museum opens its doors. Galleries are lit for the first time in their evening configuration. A welcome drink awaits in the central hall.

All guests
19:45

Opening Words

A short welcome from the curatorial team in the Rotunda. Introduction to the night's structure and the artists present.

Curatorial Team
20:30

First Artist Encounters

Three artists begin their live sessions in assigned galleries. Visitors can join, ask questions, and witness works in process.

K. Nowak · M. Lewandowska · T. Wójcik
22:00

Midnight Reading

A curated reading of new texts by participating writers, performed in the Sculpture Hall. Seating is limited — first come, first seated.

Z. Kamińska · P. Szymański
23:00

Late-Night Installations Open

Three exclusive installations unlock for ticket holders only. These works have no other public presentation — this night is their only existence.

Exclusive Access
01:00

Final Performance

A closing live performance in the Grand Gallery — a 40-minute piece responding to the building, the night, and the audience that has gathered.

A. Kowalski
02:00

Night Closes

The museum returns to silence. Guests depart with their printed program and a small artifact — a token of the night.

All guests

The Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw.
