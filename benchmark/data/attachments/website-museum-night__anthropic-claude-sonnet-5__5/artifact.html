The Concept

After Dark, the Museum Awakens

When the doors close to the public, ARTIFACT opens them to the few. Galleries dissolve into candlelit corridors, sculptures breathe under shifting gold light, and the halls fill with sound, performance, and quiet mystery. This is not a tour — it is a night suspended between art and dream.

◆

After-Hours Exhibitions

Wander galleries rearranged and lit exclusively for the night, revealing works rarely seen in daylight.

♫

Live Performances

Ambient composers and sound artists respond live to the architecture and art around them.

✦

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Step behind velvet ropes into restoration studios and archives never open to the public.

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Address: ul. Miodowa 14, 00-246 Warsaw, Poland

Getting There

Arrive early to enjoy the courtyard before doors open. The gallery is a short walk from the Old Town, with well-lit streets leading directly to the main entrance on ul. Miodowa.

Accessibility

The Old Gallery offers step-free access throughout, wheelchair-accessible entrances on the north side of the building, and accessible restrooms on the ground and first floors. Staff are available to assist at every entrance.

Parking & Transit

Nearest metro station: Ratusz Arsenał (M1), an 8-minute walk. Bus stop "Miodowa" is directly outside the venue. On-site parking is limited; taxi and rideshare drop-off is available at the main entrance on ul. Miodowa.
