06 / Good to knowBefore you arrive
A few practicalities

Questions, answered.

Make room for the unexpected. Here is what to know before planning your evening. Event arrangements on this demo page are illustrative.

Is payment processed on this page?

No. This is a demo landing page; selecting a tier does not take payment, issue a ticket, or create a reservation.

Can I arrive late?

Yes, you can join after doors open at 19:00, though timed sessions may have limited capacity. This is an illustrative event, not a real booking.

Are photos allowed?

Photography rules depend on the venue and artwork. Ask staff before photographing; please avoid flash during performances.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free access is intended in this concept. Since the venue is illustrative, confirm the real entrance, lift, seating and room access before making travel plans.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Close the demo ticket modal and select another tier; no reservation or charge is created by any selection.

How do I save the event to my calendar?

Use the Save to Calendar button in the hero to download an .ics file, then open or import it into your calendar app. The date and venue are part of this illustrative event.
