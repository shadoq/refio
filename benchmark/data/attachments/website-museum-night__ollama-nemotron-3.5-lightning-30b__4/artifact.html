Artist Details

The Old Gallery

Address: 12 Art Street, Warsaw, Poland

Travel tips: The venue is centrally located and easily accessible by public transport. Several bus and tram lines stop within walking distance.

Accessibility: The venue is wheelchair accessible with ramps at the main entrance. Accessible restrooms are available on the ground floor.

Parking / Public transport: Nearby parking garage available. Multiple bus lines (102, 150, 199) and metro station 'Świętokrzyska' within 10 minutes walk.

Can I arrive late? +

Yes, you can arrive anytime during the event hours (19:00–02:00). However, we recommend arriving at the start for the full experience.

Are photos allowed? +

Personal photography is allowed in most areas. Flash photography and professional equipment require prior permission from event staff.

Is the venue accessible? +

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms on all floors.

Can I change my ticket tier? +

Yes, ticket tier changes can be made up to 48 hours before the event. Contact us at info@artifactmuseumnight.com for assistance.
