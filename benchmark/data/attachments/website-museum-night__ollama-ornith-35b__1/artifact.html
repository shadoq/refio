ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Date 18 October 2026
Time 19:00 – 02:00
Venue The Old Gallery, Warsaw

The Evening

Program

A curated journey through sound, vision, and silence — from dusk until dawn.

19:00 – 20:00

Doors & Welcome

Arrive to a glass of Polish sparkling wine as the gallery transforms into a living installation.

20:00 – 21:00

Opening Ceremony

A cinematic prologue by director Kasia Nowak, projected across all four gallery walls.

21:00 – 22:30

Live Performances

Three artists perform in rotating rooms — ambient, classical, and electronic soundscapes.

22:30 – 23:00

Silent Gallery Walk

A guided, candlelit walkthrough of the permanent collection in complete silence.

23:00 – 01:00

DJ Set: After Dark

DJ Maja Wolska spins ambient house and downtempo beats among the sculptures.

01:00 – 02:00

Closing Ritual

A collective art-making session and final toast under the gallery's glass ceiling.

FAQ

All tickets include full access to the evening's performances, installations, and a welcome drink. VIP and Curator's Circle tickets include additional perks such as early entry, premium dining, and exclusive artist interactions.
We encourage smart evening wear. Think cocktail attire or creative black-tie. There's no strict dress code, but we ask that you dress respectfully for a museum setting.
Tickets can be refunded up to 14 days before the event. Within 14 days, tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person at no additional cost.
Yes. The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with elevator access to all floors. Please contact us in advance if you require any specific accommodations.
Photography is welcome in most areas, except during the Silent Gallery Walk and select performance segments. Please be mindful of other guests and avoid using flash.
The Old Gallery is located in central Warsaw, a 5-minute walk from the Trasa Łazienkowska metro station. Street parking is limited; we recommend using public transport or a rideshare service.
