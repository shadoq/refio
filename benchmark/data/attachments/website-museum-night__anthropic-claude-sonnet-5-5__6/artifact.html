The Concept

When the doors close, the collection awakens.

ARTIFACT turns The Old Gallery into a nocturnal stage. For seven hours, curators, composers and visual artists rewrite the rules of the museum visit: galleries dimmed to candle-glow, masterpieces reframed by light and sound, and rooms that remain sealed by day opened for a single night. Come to wander, listen, and lose track of time.

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent and special collections under dramatic night lighting, free from daytime crowds.

Live performances

Ambient soundscapes, chamber pieces and projections respond to the architecture in every hall.

Guided hidden-room tours

Follow a curator through archives, vaults and restoration studios that never open to the public.

Program

Seven hours, after dark

An hour-by-hour journey through the night — from the first opening of the doors to the last sound of the evening.

    Travel tips

    The gallery sits a five-minute walk from the Old Town. Late-night taxis and ride-share pick-ups wait on the eastern side, at the corner of Galeryjna and Nocna street.

    Accessibility

    Step-free entrance on the north side, lifts to all floors, accessible restrooms, and quiet rooms. Assistance dogs are welcome. Contact us for seating or guided support.

    Parking & public transport

    Metro M1 "Muzeum" is 4 minutes away on foot. Night buses N11 and N32 stop directly in front. Limited parking is available at the Galeryjna car park (2 min walk).
