The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Address: ul. Artystyczna 15, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Travel Tips: Located in the heart of Warsaw\'s cultural district, easily accessible by tram lines 17, 18, and 22.

Accessibility: The venue is fully accessible with elevators, ramps, and accessible restrooms throughout.

Parking: Limited street parking available. Public parking garage located at ul. Parkowa 5, 3 blocks away.

Public Transport: Metro Station: Żoliborz (Line 2), 5-minute walk. Bus stops: ul. Artystyczna (lines 17, 18, 22).
