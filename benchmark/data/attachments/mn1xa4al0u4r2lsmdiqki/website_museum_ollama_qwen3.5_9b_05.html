The Experience

Step into a world where art comes alive under the cover of darkness. ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms our galleries into an intimate sanctuary of culture, mystery, and discovery. Experience exclusive after-hours access to our finest collections, immerse yourself in live performances, and explore hidden rooms never before open to the public.

✨ After-Hours Exhibitions

Access our most prized collections when the world sleeps, experiencing art in a completely different light.

🎭 Live Performances

Immerse yourself in ambient soundscapes, live music, and theatrical performances throughout the night.

🗝️ Hidden Room Tours

Join our expert guides as they reveal secret passages and private collections usually kept locked away.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Address: ul. Sztuki 15, 00-123 Warsaw, Poland

Travel Tips:

By Metro: Get off at Centralna Station and walk 5 minutes north.

By Bus: Lines 17, 22, and 45 stop directly outside the venue.

Accessibility:

The venue is fully accessible with elevators to all floors, accessible restrooms, and wheelchair-friendly galleries. Assistive listening devices are available upon request.

Parking:

Public parking is available at the nearby parking garage (ul. Parkowa 8). Valet service is provided for ticket holders with Patron Pass.
