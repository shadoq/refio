Venue Highlights

Located in the heart of Warsaw’s cultural district, The Old Gallery blends historic architecture with contemporary art spaces. Our venue has been meticulously restored to offer a luxurious backdrop for ARTIFACT, featuring vaulted ceilings, marble floors, and state‑of‑the‑art audiovisual equipment.

Experience art in a setting where history meets innovation. The venue’s intimate spaces allow for immersive installations and personalized guest experiences.
