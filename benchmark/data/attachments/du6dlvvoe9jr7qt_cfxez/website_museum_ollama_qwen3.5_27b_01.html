The Experience

"When the lights dim, history wakes up. ARTIFACT is not just a night at the museum—it is an immersive journey through time, curated for those who seek more than silence."

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Built in 1924, The Old Gallery stands as a testament to neoclassical architecture and modern resilience. For one night only, the restricted wings will be opened to our guests. Expect velvet ropes removed, spotlights dimmed, and history brought alive by candlelight.

Frequently Asked Questions
