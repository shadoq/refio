Museum Night,

01 — Concept

After dark, the museum exhales.

One night each autumn, the collection steps out of the daylight. Velvet rooms glow under a lower, warmer light, the crowd thins to a whisper, and the building begins to tell the version of its story it keeps for the dark.

ARTIFACT is a slow procession through eight movements — projection, ambient sound, choreography and whispered tours staged between the marbles. Come early. Walk slowly. Let the museum exhale.

After-hours Exhibitions

The full collection, lit for the night alone — rooms you can finally hear.

Live Performances

Ambient sets and slow choreography staged between the sculptures.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Curators unlock vaults and storerooms closed to the public for decades.
