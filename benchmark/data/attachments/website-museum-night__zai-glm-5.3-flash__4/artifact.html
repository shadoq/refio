Museum Night, Reimagined

One night. Twelve rooms. A museum awake after dark.

For one night each October, The Old Gallery keeps its lights low and its doors open. Twelve rooms stay awake well past midnight — some lit for the first time in years. Curators, artists and musicians stage slow, cinematic encounters between visitors and the collection: a projection on marble, an ambient set drifting between canvases, a vault unlocked at 20:00. No queues, no crowds — just the building, the dark, and whatever the art becomes after hours.

After-hours exhibitions

Twelve rooms and two vault wings stay open until 02:00, lit low and slow, with wardens guiding the way through the dark.

Live performances

Ambient sets, a light-mapped projection piece and a midnight sound session — staged inside the halls themselves.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led through vault rooms and service corridors that have been closed to the public for years.
