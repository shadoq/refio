A night beyond the frame

The familiar,
seen differently.

When the city quiets, the gallery changes. ARTIFACT invites you into a world between art and atmosphere: a single night of rare access, unexpected encounters, and rooms that hold their own secrets.

Move at your own pace. Follow a sound into the dark, stay with a work a little longer, and find a new way to belong in a museum.
