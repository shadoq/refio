The Experience

ARTIFACT: Museum Night reimagines the traditional museum visit into a cinematic, after-hours journey through curated collections and immersive installations. Step into a world where art and storytelling converge under the glow of golden spotlights.

Yes, the venue is fully wheelchair accessible. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance if you require special accommodations.
