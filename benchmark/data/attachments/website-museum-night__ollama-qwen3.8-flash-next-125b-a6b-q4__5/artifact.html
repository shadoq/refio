The Concept

After Dark, the Gallery Speaks

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living, breathing nocturnal experience. Beyond the velvet ropes, past the closed doors — the collection comes alive through sound, light, and the people who guard it.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Wander the permanent collection under a different light. Curated spotlights reveal details hidden from the daytime crowd — brushstrokes, shadows, secrets.

Live Performances

Ambient musicians, VJs, and sound artists respond to the architecture itself. The Sculpture Hall becomes an instrument; the atrium, a stage.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend into the Level −1 Vaults. Small groups, led by curators, into storage rooms and conservation labs the public never sees.

The Program

Seven Hours of Nocturnal Art

A single continuous journey from dusk to dawn. Move freely between rooms — or follow the thread.

19:00

Doors Open

The atrium glows amber. Welcome drinks, a printed program, and the first ambient drone drifting from the east corridor.

Grand Atrium

19:30

Curator Welcome

Head curator Helena Voss introduces the night's route and the stories behind three works rarely shown together.

Atrium Stage

20:00

Hidden Collection Tour

Descend to the vaults. Bronze-age fragments, uncatalogued sketches, and the conservation lab — in small guided groups.

Level −1 Vaults

21:00

Live Ambient Performance

Sound artist Marek Dąbrowski performs a generative piece tuned to the resonant frequencies of the marble hall.

Sculpture Hall

22:00

Sculpture Hall Projection

VJ collective LUMEN maps light onto the marble figures. Statues appear to breathe, shift, and dissolve into shadow.

Sculpture Hall

23:00

Artist Talk

Performance artist Yara El-Sayed and art historian Dr. Tomas Berg discuss memory, material, and the ethics of display.

Gallery Cinema

00:00

Midnight Gallery Walk

A silent, candle-lit procession through the East Wing. No commentary — only footsteps, breath, and the paintings.

East Wing

01:00

Closing Sound Session

A final hour of low-frequency sound in the Velvet Room. Sit, listen, and let the night close around you.

Getting There

  • Metro Line M1 — Politechnika station, 4-minute walk north along ul. Piękna.
  • Tram 4, 15, 18 — stop "Piękna", directly opposite the east entrance.
  • Bus 131, 175 — stop "Wilcza / Piękna", 3-minute walk.
  • Paid underground parking at ul. Wilcza 69 (entrance from the north side).

Accessibility

  • Step-free access at all entrances; elevators serve every level including the vaults.
  • Wheelchairs available at the cloakroom — reserve in advance via the ticket form.
  • Hearing loop installed in the Atrium Stage and Gallery Cinema.
  • Quiet room available on Level 1 throughout the night.

Good to Know

  • Doors close at 02:00; the building empties by 02:30.
  • Coat check included with every ticket. No large bags.
  • Photography allowed without flash in all public galleries.
