ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined.

When the doors close to the day, they open to the night. Wander illuminated galleries after dark, hear music composed for stone and silence, and discover rooms the daylight never shows you.

The Concept

An After-Hours Ritual

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a nocturnal stage. Under low amber light, the permanent collection breathes differently — shadows lengthen across marble, and familiar paintings reveal secrets they keep from the afternoon crowds.

Curators become guides. Composers become storytellers. Every hour of the night is scored, staged, and lit with intention — a slow procession through art, sound, and architecture that ends only when the first grey light touches the Orangery glass.

This is not a party with art in the background. It is art, with the night as its audience.

After-hours Exhibitions

Full access to the permanent collection under bespoke night lighting, with select works opened to the public for the first time.

Live Performances

Ambient compositions and projection works performed live in the Sculpture Hall, written specifically for the acoustics of stone.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend into the Sublevel Archives with our curators — vaults, restoration rooms, and storerooms closed to visitors all year.
