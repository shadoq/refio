The Concept

Art After Dark

When the last visitor leaves and the lights dim, the museum transforms. ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a curated after-hours experience that reimagines the relationship between art, space, and the human senses.

Seven hours of live performances, immersive installations, guided nocturnal tours, and intimate encounters with works you've only seen in daylight — now revealed in a new, luminous darkness.

This is not a party. This is a pilgrimage.

The Location

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Built in 1887 and restored to its original grandeur in 2019, The Old Gallery is Warsaw's most storied cultural institution. Its vaulted ceilings, marble floors, and intimate alcoves make it the perfect stage for an event of this nature.

Three floors of gallery space, a sculpture garden, and a rooftop terrace will all be activated throughout the evening.
