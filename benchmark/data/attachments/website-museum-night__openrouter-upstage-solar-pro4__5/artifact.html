Questions

Frequently Asked

What time should I arrive?

+
Doors open at 18:30. We recommend arriving between 18:30 and 19:00 to enjoy the opening gala at its fullest. Late entry is welcome until 23:00, though some programmed elements may have already begun.

Is the venue accessible?

+
Yes. The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible, with elevator access to all floors and accessible restrooms on each level. Please let us know of any specific needs when booking, and we will do our best to accommodate.

Can I transfer or refund my ticket?

+
Tickets may be transferred to another person up to 48 hours before the event by contacting our box office. Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event date. After that, tickets may be exchanged for a future ARTIFACT event.

What is the dress code?

+
Smart evening attire is recommended. The event is a cultural gathering in a historic venue — think elegant, not formal. Comfortable shoes are advised, as the evening involves some walking between galleries.

Will photography be allowed?

+
Personal photography without flash is welcome in most areas. Certain installations and the Late-Night Salon have restricted photography — signage will be provided. Professional or commercial photography requires prior arrangement.

How do I get to The Old Gallery?

+
The gallery is a 5-minute walk from Rondo ONZ metro station (line M2). Limited paid parking is available in the adjacent courtyard. Detailed directions and a map will be sent with your ticket confirmation.
