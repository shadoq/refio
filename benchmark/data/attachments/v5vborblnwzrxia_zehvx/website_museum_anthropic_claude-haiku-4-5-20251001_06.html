Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is included in each ticket tier?

+

Gallery Access includes general admission to all exhibitions, opening reception, guided tour, and live performance. Premium Experience adds VIP lounge access, premium refreshments, artist meet & greet, priority seating, and exclusive merchandise. Platinum Patron includes everything plus private curator tour, champagne reception, private artist dinner, VIP parking, limited edition artwork, and lifetime membership.

Can I bring a guest?

+

Each ticket is valid for one person. If you'd like to bring a guest, you'll need to purchase an additional ticket. Group discounts are available for purchases of 10 or more tickets. Please contact our events team for group booking inquiries.

What is the dress code?

+

The dress code is smart casual to formal. We recommend elegant attire that reflects the premium nature of the event. Black tie is optional but encouraged for Platinum Patron ticket holders. Comfortable shoes are recommended as you'll be walking through multiple gallery spaces.

Is the event accessible for people with disabilities?

+

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with elevator access to all floors. We provide accessible restrooms and designated accessible parking spaces. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us in advance so we can ensure the best experience for you.

What is the cancellation policy?

+

Tickets can be refunded up to 14 days before the event for a full refund. Cancellations made between 7-14 days before the event will receive a 50% refund. No refunds are available for cancellations within 7 days of the event. Tickets are transferable to another person at no additional cost.

Will there be food and beverages available?

+

Yes, all ticket tiers include access to refreshments. Gallery Access includes complimentary soft drinks and light appetizers. Premium Experience includes premium beverages and gourmet catering. Platinum Patron includes champagne reception and a private artist dinner. Additional food and beverages are available for purchase throughout the event.
