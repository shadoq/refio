The Concept

An evening when the collection wakes

When the last daytime visitor leaves, The Old Gallery changes character. Spotlights narrow, voices lower, and the rooms become a stage for one extraordinary night of art, sound and discovery.

ARTIFACT invites you to wander through after-hours exhibitions, listen to live performances beneath vaulted ceilings, and descend into rooms usually closed to the public. It is a museum experienced the way it was always meant to be felt — slowly, intimately, in the dark.

— Jonas Wirth, Chief Curator

I

After-hours exhibitions

Three wings, reopened under dramatic light. Masterworks and contemporary pieces in conversation, curated exclusively for the night.

II

Live performances

Ambient sets, solo cello, and projection art staged in the Rotunda, the Sculpture Hall and the moonlit Courtyard.

III

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group descents into the Vault Level −1 archive — restoration studios and stored treasures rarely seen by the public.

The Program

Seven hours after dark

From doors to the final note, every hour unveils a new room, a new voice, a new way of seeing. Arrive early or drift in late — the night unfolds around you.

  1. 19:00Grand Foyer

    Doors Open

    Welcome glass of sparkling wine under the restored chandelier as the galleries dim to candlelight.

  2. 19:30Rotunda

    Curator Welcome

    Jonas Wirth opens the night with the story behind ARTIFACT and a first glimpse of tonight's rare loans.

  3. 20:00Vault Level -1

    Hidden Collection Tour

    A lantern-lit descent into the archive: restoration studios, sealed crates and works never exhibited.

  4. 21:00Rotunda

    Live Ambient Performance

    Lena Morawska builds a slow-moving soundscape from field recordings captured inside the museum itself.

  5. 22:00Sculpture Hall

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Marble figures become living screens as projection and light sculpture reshape the hall in real time.

  6. 23:00Auditorium

    Artist Talk

    An intimate conversation with the night's artists on darkness, memory and making work for a single evening.

  7. 00:00East Wing

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    A silent, torch-lit procession through the East Wing, guided by a whispered performance piece.

  8. 01:00Courtyard

    Closing Sound Session

    Solo cello and electronics under the open sky — a final hour of reverie before the doors close at 02:00.

The Artists

Voices of the night

Eight artists, curators and performers who shape the evening — each bringing a different way of listening to the museum in the dark.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

A neoclassical landmark on Warsaw's Royal Route, reopened for one night with every room — and a few secret ones — unlocked.

Address

ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 15,
00-071 Warsaw

Main entrance on Krakowskie Przedmieście. Box office and cloakroom in the Grand Foyer.

Travel tips

  • Arrive before 20:00 to join the first Hidden Collection Tour.
  • The Royal Route is pedestrian-friendly — expect lively crowds on weekend nights.
  • Bring a light layer: the Courtyard session is open-air.

Accessibility

Step-free access via the side ramp on the left of the main steps. Lifts serve all levels including Vault Level −1. Accessible toilets on the ground floor, induction loops in the Auditorium, and quiet rooms in the East Wing. Companions of visitors with disabilities enter free.

Transport & parking

Metro: M2 Nowy Świat–Uniwersytet, 6 min walk. Bus: lines 116, 180, 503 to Uniwersytet. Night buses run from Centrum after 23:30.

Parking: Limited street parking; nearest garage at Plac Piłsudskiego (5 min walk). Taxis and ride-shares can drop off at the main entrance.
