The Concept

Where Art Meets Darkness

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms Warsaw's most iconic galleries into a luminous after-hours experience. Walk through candlelit halls, encounter sculptures bathed in golden light, and discover artworks in an atmosphere that feels both ancient and utterly new.

This is not a typical museum visit. It is a cinematic journey through centuries of human creativity, curated for the evening hours when art reveals its deepest secrets.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit.

We recommend smart casual or cocktail attire. The event takes place in climate-controlled galleries, but the evening walk to the venue may be cool. Think elegant comfort — you'll be walking through art, not a dance floor.
Personal photography without flash is welcome in designated areas. Professional equipment (tripods, external lighting) requires prior approval. We ask that you respect the artworks and other guests at all times.
Yes. The Old Gallery has full wheelchair accessibility including ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms. Please contact us in advance if you require any specific accommodations and we'll ensure everything is prepared.
Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the event. Between 14 and 7 days, a 50% refund applies. Within 7 days, tickets can be transferred to another person but cannot be refunded. VIP tickets have a 30-day cancellation window.
Yes! Premium and VIP tickets include a cocktail package. Standard ticket holders can purchase drinks and artisan snacks at our bars throughout the venue. We also have a dedicated dietary requirements service — please note any needs during checkout.
ARTIFACT: Museum Night is designed as an adult-oriented experience (18+). The atmospheric lighting, late hours, and curated content are intended for mature audiences. We appreciate your understanding.
