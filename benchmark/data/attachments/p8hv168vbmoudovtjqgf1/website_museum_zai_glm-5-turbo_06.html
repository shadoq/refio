ARTIFACT: Museum Night
18 October 2026 19:00 – 02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
The Concept

Where Art Comes Alive After Dark

ARTIFACT is more than an event — it is a transformation. When the doors close and the lights dim, the museum becomes a living canvas where centuries of creativity meet contemporary expression. Wander through halls illuminated by golden spotlights, where classical sculptures share space with digital installations, and where every room holds a new discovery.

◎

After-Hours Exhibitions

Explore rarely seen collections and specially curated installations in the quiet intimacy of the museum after dark.

♪

Live Performances

From ambient soundscapes to contemporary dance, experience performances staged among masterpieces in extraordinary settings.

✦

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Discover secret chambers and restricted galleries with expert art historians, illuminated only by your golden flashlight.
