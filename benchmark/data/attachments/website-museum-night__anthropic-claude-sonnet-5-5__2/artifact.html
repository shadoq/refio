The Concept

When the crowds leave, the art speaks.

ARTIFACT turns a century-old gallery into a nocturnal stage. Masterworks are lit by single beams, sculpture halls become cinemas, and rooms closed to the public open their doors for one evening only.

Curated by a collective of artists, sound designers and historians, the night is paced like a film — slow openings, quiet crescendos, and a final scene after midnight.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection and new commissions in low light, with the galleries almost entirely to yourself.

02

Live performances

Ambient music, spoken word and projection-mapped sets performed among the exhibits, all through the night.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Follow a curator through archives, restoration studios and vaults that are normally sealed from view.

Program

Seven hours, hour by hour

A slow-burning itinerary from the opening doors to the last note.

    Travel tips

    The old town is a 10-minute walk away. Taxis and ride-hailing can drop off directly at the main entrance on the north side. Arrive by 19:30 for the smoothest entry.

    Accessibility

    Step-free entrance, lifts to every floor, accessible restrooms and quiet seating. Guide dogs welcome. Tell us in advance and we will assign a host.

    Parking & public transport

    Metro M1 "Muzeum" is a 4-minute walk; buses 105 and 175 stop at the door. Limited paid parking on Nowa Street, plus a public car park 300 m away.
