ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

A cinematic after-hours journey through exhibitions, live performances, artist talks, and guided hidden-room tours — where the gallery becomes a stage and the night becomes the exhibit.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Scroll

When the lights go down, the collection wakes up.

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a one-night-only cultural event that transforms The Old Gallery into a living, breathing artwork. From the moment the doors open at 19:00 until the final sound session at 01:00, every corridor, hall, and hidden room becomes part of a single continuous experience.

Curated by the ARTIFACT Foundation, the evening brings together artists, musicians, curators, and thinkers for a program that moves between the intimate and the monumental — inviting you to slow down, look closer, and stay late.

After-hours exhibitions

Wander the main collection and temporary shows with the gallery entirely to yourself — no crowds, no rush, just the art and the dark.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection art, and intimate stage moments staged across the atrium, sculpture hall, and rooftop terrace.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group tours unlock the archive, the conservation studio, and rooms rarely opened to the public.

Getting there

The gallery sits in the heart of Warsaw's Wola district, a short walk from the Rondo Daszyńskiego transport hub. Follow the illuminated ARTIFACT signs from the main entrance.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, passenger lifts to all floors, accessible restrooms, and assistance dogs are welcome throughout the venue. Please contact us in advance for any specific requirements.

Parking & transport

Metro line M2 (Rondo Daszyńskiego) is a 7-minute walk. Tram and bus lines stop nearby. Paid street parking is available, and secure bike racks are located at the main entrance.
