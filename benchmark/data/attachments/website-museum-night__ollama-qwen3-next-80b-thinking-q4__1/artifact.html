Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Located in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, The Old Gallery is a restored 19th-century building that has been transformed into a cutting-edge cultural space. Its grand halls and intimate chambers provide the perfect backdrop for ARTIFACT's immersive experience.

Address: ul. Długa 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Public transport: Tram lines 1, 3, 5; Metro M1 to Centrum station

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does the event start and end?

The event begins at 19:00 with the opening ceremony and concludes at 02:00. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled activities.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, we recommend elegant evening attire. Black tie optional, but we encourage guests to embrace the museum's sophisticated atmosphere.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable but may be transferred to another person. Please contact us at least 48 hours before the event for transfer requests.

Can I bring a guest to the dinner?

Dinner seating is limited to ticket holders only. Additional guests may be accommodated for an extra fee, subject to availability. Please inquire when booking.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible. Please contact us in advance if you require special accommodations.
