CHIP-8 Arcade

A tiny computer, ready to explore.

64 × 32 / CLASSIC

Sprites wrap around both edges of the display. Pixels are XOR-drawn.

Controls

10 / frame

Try the built-in demo

Press Run. The box moves with CHIP-8 keys 2 (up), 8 (down), 4 (left), and 6 (right). On your keyboard, those are 2, s, q, and e; you can also use the on-screen keys.

This uses classic CHIP-8 behavior: shifts read Vy, and Fx55/Fx65 advance I by x + 1. Timers tick at 60 Hz while running, including while waiting for a key; they stop when paused.

Keypad

Keyboard rows: 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V. Keys can also be held with a pointer.
