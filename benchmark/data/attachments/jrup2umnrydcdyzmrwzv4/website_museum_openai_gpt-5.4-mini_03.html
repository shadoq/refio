Program

An evening choreographed in chapters

A precise rhythm of arrival, immersion, and closing ritual — designed for lingering, not rushing.

01 / Arrival

Threshold and light

Guests enter through a candlelit foyer with welcome drinks, sound cues, and the first spotlighted object of the night.

02 / Encounters

Talks and performances

Artists, curators, and performers activate galleries with short-form talks, live interventions, and intimate sets.

03 / After-hours

Late viewings

Slow, quiet gallery access with a midnight room, ambient listening lounge, and final departure under gold light.

Concept

Museum night with luxury atmosphere

Quiet mystery, editorial scale

ARTIFACT treats the museum like a nocturnal set: velvet shadows, precision lighting, and intentional pauses between moments of spectacle.

Premium but approachable
