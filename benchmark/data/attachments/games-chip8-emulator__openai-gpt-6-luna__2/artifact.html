Interpreter notes

Classic CHIP-8 profile: sprites wrap at display edges. Shift instructions use Vx as their source; Bnnn jumps to nnn + V0. Fx55/Fx65 leave I unchanged. 0nnn (SYS) is a harmless no-op.

Timers advance at 60 Hz while running, independently of CPU speed. The sound timer is shown as an indicator; no audio permission or assets are needed.
