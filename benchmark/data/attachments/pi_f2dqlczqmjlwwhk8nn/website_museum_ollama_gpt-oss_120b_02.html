Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

Elegant casual – think velvet, silk and dark tones.

Are tickets refundable?

No refunds; however you may transfer your ticket to another adult.

Is there wheelchair access?

Yes, all main areas are fully accessible.

Can I bring a camera?

Professional equipment is prohibited; smartphones are welcome.

Will food be available?

Light refreshments will be served at the foyer.

How long does the event last?

From 19:00 until approximately 02:00.
