Museum Night,
Reimagined

One night. One gallery. The doors close to the daylight world — and open to something older, quieter, and far more alive.

The Concept

An After-Hours Ritual for the Curious

When the last visitor leaves and the guards make their rounds, the gallery becomes a different creature. ARTIFACT invites you inside that hour — a curated night of hidden collections, ambient sound, and encounters you cannot have in daylight.

After-hours Exhibitions

Wander the permanent collection under low amber light, with interpretive soundscapes composed for the empty halls. No crowds. No queues. Just you and the works.

Live Performances

Ambient composers, a soprano, and projection artists transform the Sculpture Hall into a living instrument throughout the night.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend into the Level −1 vaults with our curators. Works in storage, conservation studies, and pieces never shown to the public.

The Program

Seven Hours, One Unbroken Night

The evening moves like a slow tide — from the bustle of the atrium to the hush of the East Wing at midnight. Arrive whenever you like; the night holds its shape until 02:00.

19:00

Doors Open

The Grand Atrium welcomes you with warm light, a welcome drink, and the first ambient set drifting from the upper galleries.

Grand Atrium

19:30

Curator Welcome

Elena Marchetti opens the night with a short address on what the hidden collection means — and what it hides.

Atrium Stage

20:00

Hidden Collection Tour

A guided descent into the Level −1 vaults. Limited groups, lantern-lit corridors, works unseen since 1998.

Level −1 Vaults

21:00

Live Ambient Performance

Jonas Weber performs an evolving ambient set among the marble figures, with Clara Nowak's voice threading through the hall.

Sculpture Hall

22:00

Sculpture Hall Projection

Kenji Mori maps slow-moving video onto the collection's largest sculptures, turning stone into light.

Sculpture Hall

23:00

Artist Talk

Amara Diallo and Viktor Petrov in conversation on installation, darkness, and the ethics of looking.

The Lantern Room

00:00

Midnight Gallery Walk

A silent, self-guided walk through the East Wing at the stroke of midnight. Candles only. No talking.

East Wing

01:00

Closing Sound Session

Tomas Ferreira closes the night with a long-form sound bath in the Orangery as the museum exhales.

Orangery

Artists & Speakers

The People Behind the Night

Eight artists, curators, and performers shape the evening's arc — from the first note in the atrium to the last resonance in the Orangery.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

A neoclassical gallery on ul. Piękna, closed to the public after dark — except once a year, for us.

Getting There

  • Metro Politechnika — 4 minutes on foot via ul. Piękna
  • Tram 4 / 15 / 18 — stop "Piękna", 2 minutes away
  • Underground parking beneath the gallery (limited, first come)
  • City bikes available at the rack beside the east façade

Accessibility

  • Step-free access at all entrances and throughout the route
  • Elevators to every level, including the Level −1 vaults
  • Hearing loop in the Atrium Stage and Lantern Room
  • Assistance dogs welcome; quiet room available on request

Address

ul. Piękna 12, 00-543 Warsaw
