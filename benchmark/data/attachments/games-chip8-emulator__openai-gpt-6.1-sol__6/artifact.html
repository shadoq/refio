Compatibility & machine notes

Classic CHIP-8, all 35 instructions. Programs start at 0x200; the 80-byte hexadecimal font lives at 0x050. Legacy 0nnn SYS calls are ignored. SCHIP and XO-CHIP extensions are not supported.

Modern quirks: shifts use VX; logic preserves VF unless VF is the destination. Bnnn uses V0. Fx55/Fx65 leave I unchanged. Fx1E wraps I to 16 bits without changing VF. Sprites wrap both edges; drawing uses XOR and reports collisions in VF.

Fx0A requires a fresh key press, not a key already held. Timers continue at 60 Hz while waiting, but freeze when paused. Pause and Reset release all keys and silence sound. ROMs stay local to your browser; no uploads or external resources.
