Step into a world where art comes alive after dark. ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms the gallery into an immersive experience — guided tours, live performances, curated installations, and exclusive access to collections you won't see during the day.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

Nestled in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, The Old Gallery is a former 19th-century textile warehouse transformed into one of Poland's most atmospheric art spaces. Its soaring ceilings, original ironwork, and play of light and shadow create a perfect setting for evening events.

  • Address: ul. Starego Galerii 12, 00-001 Warsaw
  • Capacity: Up to 300 guests
  • Accessibility: Elevator access to all floors
  • Parking: Valet service available
  • Nearest Metro: Nowy Świat (3 min walk)

FAQ

What should I wear?

We recommend smart-casual attire. Think dark tones, elegant fabrics — the kind of outfit you'd wear to a gallery opening. No strict dress code, but we'd love for you to feel part of the occasion.

Is photography allowed?

Photography is permitted in most areas, but please respect the artists' wishes and avoid flash photography in the main exhibition hall. Some installations may have specific restrictions — staff will guide you.

Can I bring guests?

Tickets are non-transferable and personal. However, you may bring one guest who will need their own ticket. Please note this at the time of purchase.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the event are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a 25% service fee. Contact us at events@artifact.gallery for assistance.

Is there parking available?

Yes, valet parking is available at the venue. For Premium ticket holders, valet service is complimentary. Standard and Group ticket holders can use the paid valet service (PLN 30). Street parking is also available nearby.
