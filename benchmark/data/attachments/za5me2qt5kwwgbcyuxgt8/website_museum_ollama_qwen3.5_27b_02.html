LOCATION

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Nestled in the heart of the historic district, The Old Gallery offers a hauntingly beautiful backdrop for our evening event. With its vaulted ceilings and century-old stone walls, it is the perfect canvas for modern art to come alive after dark.

  • Krakowskie Przedmieście 26/4
  • Metro: Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet (5 min walk)
