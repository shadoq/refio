The Boundary Between Art & Night

ARTIFACT is not just an exhibition. It is a temporal displacement. For one night, The Old Gallery surrenders its silence to light, sound, and movement.

We invite you to step into a curated universe where contemporary performance meets the weight of history, creating a dialogue across centuries.
