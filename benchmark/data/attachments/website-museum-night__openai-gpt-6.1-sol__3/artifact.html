06 /A few things to know

Before the
doors open.

This is an imagined event and interactive design demo, not an announcement of a real museum night.

Is payment processed on this page?

No. The checkout is a demo only. It never asks for card details, charges money, or creates a real booking. Personal information is not sent or stored, and no confirmation email is sent.

Can I arrive late?

In the imagined program, doors remain open after 19:00 and the evening ends at 02:00. Timed sessions would begin as listed. For a real event, confirm last admission and re-entry rules directly with its organizer.

Are photos allowed?

The proposed experience would welcome non-flash personal photography where permitted, with no tripods and respect for performers and other visitors. These are illustrative guidelines; actual collection and photography restrictions would need confirmation.

Is the venue accessible?

No physical venue or accessibility facilities have been verified. The demo imagines step-free access, but lifts, routes, accessible toilets, seating, and sensory accommodations must be confirmed with a real venue before making plans.

Can I change ticket tier?

Yes. Open any pass and use the tier selector in the demo checkout. The total updates immediately. You can also select between 1 and 10 tickets; no purchase is made.

How do I save the event to my calendar?

Use “Save to Calendar” in the hero to download an .ics file, then import or open it in your calendar app. It runs from 19:00 on 18 October to 02:00 on 19 October 2026 in Europe/Warsaw time. The saved event is fictional.

What does each ticket include?

Night Pass covers general entry, the main exhibition, and the general program. Curator Pass adds the hidden collection tour and reserved talk seating. Patron Pass also includes an imagined VIP lounge, signed print, and priority entrance. These benefits are illustrative, not deliverable purchases.

Are the artists and location real?

The eight artist identities, program, venue name, and address are fictional for this demo. The gallery photography is illustrative and does not identify the speakers or depict a verified event venue.
