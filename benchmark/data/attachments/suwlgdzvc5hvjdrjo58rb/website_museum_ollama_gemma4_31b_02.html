The Artifact Experience

Step beyond the velvet rope. ARTIFACT is not a tour, but an immersive descent into the subconscious of art history. For one night only, The Old Gallery transforms into a living gallery where shadows speak and gold glimmers in the dark.

Experience curated silence, cinematic lighting, and exclusive access to archives never before seen by the public eye.

The Old Gallery
