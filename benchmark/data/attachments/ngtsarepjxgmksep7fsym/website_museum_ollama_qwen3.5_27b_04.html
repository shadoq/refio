📍 The Old Gallery, Warsaw ⏰ 19:00 – 02:00

The Experience

Step into the velvet darkness where art comes alive.

ARTIFACT is not just an exhibition; it is a nocturnal journey through the corridors of history and imagination.

We invite you to experience our curated collection under the soft glow of candlelight, accompanied by live ambient performances and guided tours that reveal secrets hidden in plain sight during daylight hours.

"Art is the only way to run away without leaving home." — Twain, reinterpreted for the night wanderer.

The Evening

19:00 – 19:30

Arrival & Welcome Drink

Gather in the Grand Hall. Enjoy a signature "Midnight Velvet" cocktail as you settle into the atmosphere.

19:30 – 20:30

Guided Tour: Shadows & Light

A curator-led exploration of the permanent collection, focusing on works rarely seen after dark.

20:45 – 21:30

Live Performance: Cello & Electronics

An immersive soundscape performance by local artists, blending classical strings with modern synthesis.

21:45 – 02:00

Free Roam & Afterparty

Explore the galleries at your own pace. DJ sets begin in the sculpture garden.
