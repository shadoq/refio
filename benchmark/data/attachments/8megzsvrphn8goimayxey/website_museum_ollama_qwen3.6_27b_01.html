The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A historic landmark reimagined for contemporary culture. Located in the heart of the city, this venue offers a unique atmosphere blending classical architecture with modern art installations.

  • Address: ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 7, 00-011 Warsaw
  • Accessibility: Full wheelchair access via main entrance and elevators.
  • Parking: Limited street parking. Recommended to use public transport.

Metro: Marszałkowska (Line M2) - 5 min walk
