The Concept

When the lights go down,
the art wakes up.

An evening of quiet wonder

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a single evening, held after closing, when the Old Gallery opens its doors to a small audience for an immersive night of exhibitions, live sound, conversation, and guided passage through rooms normally kept in silence. Step inside after dark.

⏳

After-hours exhibitions

Every gallery re-lit for one night only. Works ordinarily tucked away are brought into a single, continuous viewing.

♫

Live performances

Ambient compositions and a closing sound session performed inside the Sculpture Hall, in response to the rooms themselves.

🗝

Guided hidden-room tours

Curators lead small groups through storage and archive spaces rarely seen by the public — the museum behind the museum.

Getting here

The Old Gallery

✦ ul. Staromiejska 14, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland
🚇

Public transport

Metro Line M2, Stare Miasto station — 4 minutes walk. Trams 4, 13, 21 stop two blocks away.

🅿

Parking

Secure underground parking at the gallery (limited spaces, reservation recommended). Street parking on Staromiejska and parallel streets after 18:00.

♿

Accessibility

Step-free entry throughout the ground floor galleries. A lift serves the upper exhibition level. Assistance available on request at check-in.

🗺

Travel tip

Arrive 15 minutes early to collect your map and welcome drink. Latecomers are admitted between segments at the next natural break.
