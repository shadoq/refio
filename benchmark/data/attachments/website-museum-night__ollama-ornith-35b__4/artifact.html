The Concept

Where Art
Meets the Night

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a one-night-only immersive cultural event that transforms a historic Warsaw gallery into a living canvas of sound, light, and movement.

For one evening, the walls breathe. The art awakens. Guests move through installations designed to blur the line between observer and participant.

The Creators

Artists

Eight voices shaping the night — each bringing a distinct world into the gallery.

Marek Nowak
Visual Artist
Known for large-scale installations exploring memory and decay. His work has been exhibited across Europe and the Americas.
Ewa Kowalska
Choreographer
A pioneer of site-specific dance, Ewa creates performances that respond directly to architectural space and history.
Tomasz Zieliński
Sound Artist
Works at the intersection of field recording, modular synthesis, and spatial audio design.
Lena Moreau
Photographer
Specializes in long-exposure night photography and darkroom alchemy. Her prints capture the liminal between day and night.
Jakub Wiśniewski
Filmmaker
Creates experimental short films exploring urban isolation and the poetry of everyday objects.
Sofia Andersson
Textile Artist
Weaves light and thread into immersive environments. Her work blurs the boundary between craft and fine art.
Adrian Voicu
Sculptor
Works with reclaimed materials and industrial waste, transforming them into meditative forms.
Nina Petrova
Poet & Performer
Her spoken-word performances merge Slavic folklore with contemporary urban narratives.
