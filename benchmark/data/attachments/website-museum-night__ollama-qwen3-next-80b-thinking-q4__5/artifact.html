Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night

18 October 2026 | 19:00–02:00 | The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Experience the Extraordinary

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a unique after-hours event that transforms the museum into a living canvas of art, music, and immersive experiences. Explore curated exhibits, live performances, and exclusive access to hidden collections under the glow of golden spotlights.

Join us for an evening where history meets the future, and every corner holds a new discovery.

Program Timeline

19:00 - Opening Ceremony

Welcome to ARTIFACT. Experience the grand opening with a special performance by the Warsaw Philharmonic.

20:30 - Art Installation

Interactive digital art exhibit by renowned artist Elena Voss. Explore the fusion of technology and classical art.

22:00 - Live Jazz Session

Under the stars in the museum courtyard, enjoy a live jazz performance by the Warsaw Jazz Collective.

00:00 - Midnight Gallery Tour

Guided tour of the museum's most exclusive collections, available only during this event.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does the event start and end?
The event begins at 19:00 and concludes at 02:00. Doors open at 18:30 for early arrivals.
Is there a dress code?
We recommend elegant evening attire. Black tie optional, but we encourage guests to embrace the museum's sophisticated atmosphere.
Are children allowed?
This event is for guests aged 18 and above. Please plan accordingly.
Can I bring my own food and drinks?
No outside food or beverages are permitted. Complimentary drinks will be available throughout the event.
