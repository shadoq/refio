The Concept

Art wakes up when the crowds go home.

ARTIFACT invites you into the museum after dark, when galleries fall quiet and the works begin to speak in a different register.

Across one long night, rooms are reimagined with light, sound and movement. Curators lead you through halls that are usually closed to the public, while artists and performers respond to the collection in real time.

Arrive as a visitor, leave as a witness to something rare.

After-hours exhibitions

Galleries reopened for one night, lit with warm spotlights and unhurried silence.

Live performances

Ambient sets, sculptural movement and projections that answer the works on the walls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups descend into storage halls and private rooms with a curator as guide.

Artists & Speakers

The voices of the night.

Eight makers, curators and performers, each with a session inside the program.

Elena Marchetti

Curator & Installation Artist

Shapes memory into rooms that seem to breathe after dark.

Tomasz Wieczorek

Light Artist

Working with beam, shadow and the slow drift of colour.

Amara Okafor

Sculptor

Monumental forms carved from reclaimed stone and bronze.

Lukas Brandt

Sound Designer

Low frequencies and long silences, composed for stone rooms.

Joanna Kowalska

Media Artist

Projection work that turns architecture into a moving archive.

Marek Lindqvist

Stage Director

Choreographs how bodies, light and objects share a stage.

Sofia Ruiz

Performance Artist

Durational work that asks what it means to stay in a room.

Piotr Nowak

Art Historian & Speaker

Reads the collection against the stories that were left out.

Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Muzealna 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

A nineteenth-century exhibition hall, reopened for one night in October with its lights lowered and its doors held open late.

Getting here

  • Metro line M1, station Centrum, about 5 minutes on foot
  • Bus 175 and 518 stop directly outside the building
  • Limited paid parking on Muzealna street, booked in advance

Travel tips

  • Arrive after 19:00 for the full program
  • Late arrivals are welcome until 00:30, subject to capacity
  • Coat check is available in the Grand Hall

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance and lift to all floors
  • Accessible restrooms on every level
  • Assistance dogs are welcome throughout the venue
  • A quiet room is available near the atrium
