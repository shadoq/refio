The Concept

One night. Seven hours.
The museum after dark.

For a single autumn night, The Old Gallery opens its doors long after closing time. Wander spotlight-lit halls, hear ambient scores drift through the rotunda, and step into hidden rooms that daylight visitors never see.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection and a special nocturnal installation, lit only by golden spotlights and shadow.

02

Live performances

Ambient composers, projection artists and performers take over the halls with works made for the dark.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Follow our curators into the archive rooms and sealed corridors where the secret collection sleeps.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

A nineteenth-century picture gallery in the heart of Warsaw — and for one night, entirely yours.

Address

The Old Gallery
ul. Królewska 12, 00-001 Warsaw

Getting there

Metro M2, Świétokrzyska station — 8 minutes on foot. Buses 175 and 128 stop at Królewska, directly outside. Trams 4 and 15 stop at Królewska 05.

Paid parking is available at Plac Piślsudskiego, a 5-minute walk away. We recommend arriving by public transport — the night ends late.

Accessibility

Step-free access throughout, elevators to all floors, and accessible restrooms on every level. A quiet room is available all night — ask any steward.

Good to know

The cloakroom is free. Last entry is 23:30. The courtyard bar serves wine and zero-proof cocktails until 01:30.
