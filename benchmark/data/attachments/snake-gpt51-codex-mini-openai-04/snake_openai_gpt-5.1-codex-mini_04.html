Snake Duel

Choose a battle mode and guide your snake across a 30×30 field. Arrow keys control player 1, WASD controls player 2. Press Enter to restart once the match ends.

Each snake grows after eating food. The game ends when any snake crashes into the wall, itself, or the opponent.
