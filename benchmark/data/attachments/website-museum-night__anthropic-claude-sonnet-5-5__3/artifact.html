The Concept

When the doors close, the art wakes.

For one night, The Old Gallery hands itself over to shadow, sound and stillness. Corridors normally crowded with daylight become theatres of light; sculptures hold their breath under single golden beams.

ARTIFACT is an invitation to see a museum the way its curators do — alone, after hours, and with a little mystery. Seven hours, eight rooms, one unforgettable night in the heart of Warsaw.

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection and three new installations, lit only for tonight.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection mapping and sound sessions performed among the artworks.

Guided hidden-room tours

Follow our curators into restoration studios and vaults that never open to the public.

Program

Seven hours, hour by hour

A slow-burning night that begins in candlelight and ends in resonance.

    Travel tips

    Enter through the Garden Gate on ul. Wystawowa. A 6-minute walk from the Old Town; taxis and rideshare drop off at the marked bay.

    Accessibility

    Step-free entrance, lifts to every floor, accessible restrooms, quiet room and seating throughout. Assistance dogs welcome.

    Parking & public transport

    Metro M2 “Muzeum” (4 min walk) and buses 175, 222 and N32. Limited paid parking on-site; city car park two blocks north.
