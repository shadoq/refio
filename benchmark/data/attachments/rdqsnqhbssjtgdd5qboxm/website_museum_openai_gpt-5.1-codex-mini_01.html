Concept

ARTIFACT is a premium evening performance where the museum becomes a living narrative. Velvet darkness, golden spotlights, and choreographed light journeys highlight contemporary works across painting, sculpture, and installation.

Curated Atmospheres

Guided wanderings through galleries with theatrical lighting and calibrated sonic cues.

Nocturnal Cinema

Immersive video projections and intimate screenings, a dialogue between image and silence.

Velvet Ritual

Limited ticket flows, signature cocktails, and a hush of sophistication curated for late-night connoisseurs.
