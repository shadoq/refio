The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Located in the heart of the city, this neoclassical masterpiece houses centuries of cultural heritage. For one night, it becomes a stage for the extraordinary.

  • Address: Krakowskie Przedmieście 6, Warsaw
  • Transit: Metro: Marszałkowska (5 min walk)
  • Parking: Limited street parking; recommend public transit.
  • Accessibility: Full wheelchair access via main entrance.
