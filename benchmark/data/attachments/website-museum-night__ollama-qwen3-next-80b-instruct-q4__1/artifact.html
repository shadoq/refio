Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night
18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Reimagining the Night

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms the traditional museum experience into an immersive, sensory journey through the darkness. As the city sleeps, the gallery awakens with curated light installations, live performances, and interactive exhibits that reveal hidden narratives within the collection.

Each artifact tells a story not just through its form, but through the atmosphere it inhabits. Our team of artists, historians, and technologists have collaborated to create an experience where the boundaries between past and present, object and observer, dissolve in the velvet night.

Expect unexpected encounters: a Renaissance painting that responds to your breath, a medieval manuscript that whispers its secrets when touched, and sculptures that cast shadows telling stories of their own.

An evening where art breathes

Program

19:00
Opening

Evening Reception

Guests are welcomed with a curated selection of artisanal cocktails and canapés inspired by the museum's collection. Ambient soundscapes composed from historical artifacts' resonant frequencies set the tone for the evening.

19:45
Performance

Whispers of the Archive

A live performance by the Warsaw Chamber Ensemble, interpreting historical documents through experimental music. Instruments are played with objects from the museum's collection, creating a sonic dialogue between past and present.

20:30
Exhibit

Light Sculptures: Echoes

Interactive light installations respond to visitor movement, revealing hidden patterns in the museum's architecture. Each sculpture is a physical manifestation of data from the museum's digitized archives.

21:15
Performance

Shadow Play: The Forgotten

A shadow theater performance using historical artifacts as puppets, telling the stories of those whose voices were lost to history. The performance is projected onto the museum's original 19th-century walls.

22:00
Exhibit

Neural Night: AI Interpretations

Artificial intelligence algorithms analyze the museum's collection and generate new interpretations displayed as digital projections. Visitors can interact with the AI to explore alternative historical narratives.

23:00
Performance

Midnight Sonata

A solo pianist performs a composition created from the frequencies of museum artifacts. The piano is placed in the center of the main hall, surrounded by visitors who can move freely through the space.

00:30
Exhibit

Reflections: The Future

Visitors are invited to contribute their own interpretations of the artifacts through digital tablets. These contributions are displayed as a collective mosaic on the museum's central wall.

01:30
Closing

Final Reflection

A quiet moment of contemplation as the final light installations fade. Guests are invited to write a note to the future and place it in the museum's time capsule.

Venue

The Old Gallery, originally constructed in 1892 as a private collection space for the wealthy industrialist family of Władysław Kowalski, has been meticulously restored to its original grandeur while incorporating state-of-the-art lighting and climate control systems.

Its 12,000 square feet of exhibition space features original marble floors, gilded moldings, and stained-glass skylights that have been preserved through careful conservation. The building's unique architecture creates natural acoustics perfect for the evening's performances.

Located in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, the venue is accessible by public transportation and offers limited valet parking. The surrounding area features several historic cafés and bookshops that remain open late for post-event gatherings.

As a protected cultural monument, the venue maintains strict environmental controls to preserve the artifacts on display, ensuring an optimal experience for both the art and its visitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this event suitable for children?

While children are welcome, we recommend this experience for guests aged 12 and above due to the immersive nature of the installations and the late hour. Some exhibits contain dim lighting and soundscapes that may be overwhelming for younger children.

Are there accessibility accommodations?

Yes, the venue is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms. We offer audio descriptions for visually impaired guests and sign language interpretation upon request. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance to arrange accommodations.

Can I take photographs?

Photography is permitted for personal use in most areas, but flash photography and tripods are prohibited to preserve the integrity of the artworks and the experience of other guests. Some interactive installations may have specific photography restrictions, which will be clearly indicated.

What should I wear?

While there is no strict dress code, we encourage guests to embrace the evening's atmosphere with elegant evening attire. The venue is climate-controlled, but some areas may be cooler than others, so a light jacket or shawl is recommended.

Are food and drinks available?

Yes, complimentary cocktails and canapés are included with all ticket tiers. The café will remain open throughout the evening with additional beverages and light snacks available for purchase. All beverages are non-alcoholic options available upon request.

Can I purchase tickets at the door?

Tickets are available for purchase online only. Due to the limited capacity and curated nature of the experience, we do not sell tickets at the door. We recommend purchasing in advance to guarantee your spot.

Is parking available?

Limited valet parking is available for VIP ticket holders. Public parking is available nearby at the Warsaw City Parking Garage, a 5-minute walk from the venue. We strongly encourage the use of public transportation due to limited parking availability.
