Exclusive Nocturnal Exhibition

Museum Night,
Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night opens the sealed vaults and monumental chambers of The Old Gallery for a single luminous autumn evening. Experience timeless masterworks submerged in velvet shadow, sonic minimalism, and golden spotlights.

Date 18 October 2026
Hours 19:00 – 02:00
Location The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Get Tickets
Curated Experience

8 chambers, subterranean archive viewings, and responsive architectural illumination.

“
“When daylight recedes from marble hallways, art ceases to be a historical artifact and transforms into an intimate nocturnal sanctuary.”
— Dr. Elena Vane, Chief Curator
01

After-Hours Exhibitions

Venture through the Renaissance and Antiquities wings after regular operating hours. Specially positioned warm spotlights isolate brushstrokes, textures, and silhouettes in total contemplative silence.

02

Live Performances

Neoclassical acoustics activated by modular synthesizer ensembles, live ambient cellists, and spatial sound installations composed exclusively for the acoustics of the Grand Rotunda.

03

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend into subterranean preservation vaults and archival restoration labs normally off-limits to the public, led by the chief conservators of Warsaw’s historic collection.

The Evening Schedule

Hour by Hour Nocturne

A continuous progression of visual art, sound ceremonies, and intimate dialogues unfolding across seven architectural zones.

19:00

Doors Open & Ambient Prelude

Grand Foyer

Arrival champagne reception accompanied by low-frequency acoustic soundscapes and introductory gallery lighting choreography.

19:30

Curator Welcome & Opening Remarks

Main Rotunda

An editorial introduction to the night’s theme of darkness and preservation by Dr. Elena Vane beneath the dome.

20:00

Hidden Collection Tour: The Vaults

Vault Gallery & Archive

Small-group guided traversal into the climate-controlled vaults to view 17th-century baroque studies under conservation-grade illumination.

21:00

Live Ambient Performance

Neoclassical Atrium

Julian Thorne pairs tape loops and prepared grand piano with spatial delay, harmonizing with the 20-meter column arcade.

22:00

Sculpture Hall Light Projection

Marble Hall of Antiquities

Site-responsive ray-traced laser mapping by Maya Lin-Hoffman casting kinetic geometric shadows against Roman marble torsos.

23:00

Artist Talk: Shadows in Conservation

North Salon

Panel on how light and darkness dictate the survival of pigment, featuring visiting artists and senior European restorers.

00:00

Midnight Gallery Walk

Curated Darkness Chambers

Total dimming of overhead fixtures. Visitors explore the permanent collection illuminated solely by handheld amber beam guides.

01:00

Closing Sub-Bass Sound Session

Subterranean Crypt

Deep meditative frequency immersion by sound architect Kieran Sato to ease visitors into the night’s conclusion.

The Setting

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Krakowskie Przedmieście 44, Warsaw, Poland

Erected in the late 19th century with Italian neoclassical limestone colonnades, The Old Gallery stands as a cathedral of fine art. For ARTIFACT, the building’s monumental skylights will be dimmed, giving way to amber beam spotlights, private archive corridors, and vaulted stone cellars rarely unveiled to patrons.

Public Transit & Metro

Directly accessible via Metro Line M2 (Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet Station, 350m) and Tram lines 4, 18, 26.

Patron Parking

Valet service at North Gate. Underground parking garage available via Karowa Street with 24/7 attendant.

Accessibility

Fully step-free access across all 3 levels. Dedicated gallery elevators and tactile guidance paths.

Check-in & Cloakroom

Expedited guest verification at Portico South. Secure complimentary coat and bag check available.

Historical Center District Coordinates: 52°14'20.4"N 21°01'03.0"E
