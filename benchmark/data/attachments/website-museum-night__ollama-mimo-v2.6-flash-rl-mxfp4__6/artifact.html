The Concept

After the doors close,
the collection wakes.

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a living nocturne — seven hours where the boundary between visitor and artwork dissolves. Spotlights drift. Sound bleeds between rooms. Curators open doors that stay sealed the rest of the year.

This is not a late opening. It is a choreographed night of after-hours exhibitions, live performances, and guided hidden-room tours, staged for those who believe museums are most alive in the dark.

After-hours exhibitions

Eight galleries lit exclusively for the night, with works rarely shown in daylight hours.

Live performances

Ambient sets, choreography, and projection pieces staged between the sculpture halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led into the archive, restoration studio, and sealed collection rooms.

Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Kredytowa 4, 00-062 Warsaw, Poland

A 19th-century exhibition hall on the Cultural Axis, steps from the Vistula embankments. Marble staircases, cast-iron glass roof, and eight interconnected galleries.

Getting there

  • Metro: Centrum station (Line M1 / M2), 6-minute walk via Nowy Świat.
  • Bus & tram: stops "Stare Miasto" and "Kredytowa", served by lines 175, 180, and tram 4, 15, 18.
  • From Warsaw Chopin Airport: approx. 25 minutes by taxi or metro + transfer.

Parking

Limited street parking on ul. Kredytowa (paid, 19:00–06:00). Recommended: underground car park at Plac Powstańców Warszawy, 4 minutes on foot.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance via the west ramp; lift access to all public floors.
  • Accessible restrooms on ground and first floor.
  • Assistance dogs welcome. Large-print program available at the foyer desk.
  • Induction loop installed in the Reading Room for the artist talk.
