The Concept

Art lives after
the lights go out

ARTIFACT is not a museum visit. It is an encounter — with art, with darkness, with the quiet electricity of a gallery after hours. We transform The Old Gallery into a living, breathing nocturnal world.

Eight artists. Seven rooms. One night. Guided by candlelight and curated sound, you move through installations that exist only in darkness, performances that begin at midnight, and conversations that last until dawn.

🕯

Nocturnal Installations

Site-specific works created exclusively for darkness. Art that cannot exist in daylight.

🎭

Live Performances

Intimate performances woven through the gallery spaces — music, movement, spoken word.

🥂

Curated Hospitality

Champagne, rare spirits, and artisanal bites served in the candlelit vaults below.

🎧

Soundscapes

Original compositions by resident sound artists fill each room with immersive audio.

📖

Guided Encounters

Intimate curator-led tours for Patron ticket holders. Maximum 12 guests per session.

🌙

Until 2am

Seven hours of uninterrupted immersion. The night is yours to explore at your own pace.

Evening Program

The Night Unfolds

A carefully orchestrated sequence of experiences, each building on the last.

19:00
Opening

Doors Open & Welcome Reception

Guests arrive to a candlelit welcome in the Grand Hall. Champagne, introductions, and the first glimpse of the installations.

19:45
Performance

Opening Ceremony — "Threshold"

A 20-minute immersive performance by Marta Kowalska marking the transition from day to night. The gallery transforms before your eyes.

20:30
Exhibition

Gallery Rooms Open — Free Exploration

All seven rooms open simultaneously. Move freely through the installations. Guided tours depart every 30 minutes for Patron guests.

21:30
Music

Live Set — Aleksander Voss

An hour-long ambient performance in the East Wing. Voss plays original compositions on prepared piano and electronics.

22:30
Dining

Midnight Supper in the Vaults

A seated supper in the candlelit vaults beneath the gallery. Seasonal menu by Chef Zofia Wierzbicka. Included for Patron guests.

00:00
Midnight

Midnight Ritual — "The Unveiling"

A secret installation revealed only at midnight. All guests gather in the Central Hall for a collective, unrepeatable moment.

00:30
Late Night

Late Night Lounge & Bar

The gallery becomes a late-night salon. Rare spirits, conversation, and the installations remain open until closing.

02:00
Closing

Doors Close

The night ends. The art remains, but only in memory.

The Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw

Built in 1887, The Old Gallery is one of Warsaw's most storied cultural spaces. Its vaulted ceilings, marble floors, and labyrinthine basement corridors make it the perfect host for a night of mystery and art.

The building has been carefully restored while preserving its original character — the creak of the floorboards, the smell of old stone, the way sound moves differently in each room.
