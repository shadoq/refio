The Concept

One Night, After Dark

For one night, The Old Gallery stays open long after dark. The house lights go down, the spotlights come up, and the collection is shown the way it is rarely seen — slowly, quietly, in gold. Curated performances unfold between the frames, doors to hidden rooms stand open, and the museum becomes a cinematic after-hours world of its own in the heart of Warsaw.

◆

After-hours Exhibitions

Full access to the main galleries, re-lit for the night. Golden spotlights rest on single canvases while the rooms around them settle into velvet darkness.

✦

Live Performances

Ambient sound, projection and movement pieces staged inside the collection — brief, quiet works that meet the art exactly where it hangs.

◈

Hidden-Room Tours

Small guided groups step behind the scenes, through the storage and restoration rooms normally closed to visitors.

Artists & Speakers

Voices of the Night

Eight artists, performers and curators shape the programme of ARTIFACT: Museum Night — from ambient composition and light projection to contemporary sculpture and dance. Select a card to meet them.

Marta Zielińska

Curator of the Night

The head curator behind the whole night, who re-hangs the collection around a single thread of gold and silence.

Tomasz Wójcik

Light Artist

He paints with projectors instead of brushes, mapping slow-moving light onto stone and marble.

Anna Kowalczyk

Sculptor

Her cast-glass and bronze forms meet the unrestored works of the storage wing, unlocked for one night only.

Jakub Lewandowski

Ambient Composer

Long, patient drones built from field recordings and a restored harmonium, composed for the Grand Hall itself.

Ewa Szymańska

Installation Artist

She builds rooms inside rooms — and has hidden seven small interventions along the midnight walk.

Piotr Adamczyk

Performance Artist

Famously slow movement performances staged beside the old masters; look for him standing very still in Gallery II.

Karolina Nowak

Art Historian

The art historian who opens the doors at 19:00 and moves through the galleries with micro-talks on single paintings.

Michał Kaczmarek

Sound Designer

He tunes the sound of every room in the building, then closes the night with an open-air set in the courtyard.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A restored 19th-century gallery in the heart of Warsaw, The Old Gallery keeps its marble staircases, brass fittings and skylit main hall — and for this one night opens every corridor, storage wing and courtyard to its guests.

  • ⌂Address — ul. Artystów 12, 00-048 Warsaw, Poland
  • ⓜGetting there — Metro line M1 to Świętokrzyska, then 6 minutes on foot; trams 4, 15 and 18 stop at the door
  • ♿Accessibility — Step-free entrance, lift to all floors and accessible restrooms; guide dogs welcome; a quiet room is available on request
  • ⓅParking — Limited street parking after 18:00; the Plaza Garage on ul. Kredytowa is a 4-minute walk and offers a flat evening rate
  • ➤Travel tip — Arrive by 18:45 for the shortest entry queue; a priority lane opens for Patron Pass holders at 18:30
CloakroomFree coat and bag check in the foyer, open all night from 19:00 until 02:00.
Late EntryDoors stay open all night — the last recommended entry for the full programme is 23:00.
