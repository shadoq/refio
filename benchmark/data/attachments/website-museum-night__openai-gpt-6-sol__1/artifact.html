06 / Good to know

Before the lights go down.

A few useful details to help you make the most of your evening.

What is included in my ticket?

Every pass includes admission from 19:00 to 02:00, the after-hours exhibitions, live performances and artist talks. Curator and Patron passes add the experiences listed on their respective cards.

Do I need to book a guided tour in advance?

Guided hidden-room tours are included with Curator and Patron passes. At a real event, limited tour places would be assigned ahead of time; this demonstration does not make bookings.

Can I arrive at any time?

Yes. Doors open at 19:00 and the galleries close at 02:00. Arriving early gives you the widest choice of performances and conversations.

Is the venue accessible?

The fictional event is planned with step-free entry and accessible facilities. For specific requirements, please check directly with a real event organiser before travelling.

Are tickets refundable or transferable?

This is a demo, so no tickets are issued or charged. For a real event, refund and transfer terms would be shared clearly before checkout.

How do I add the event to my calendar?

Use the Save to Calendar link to download an .ics file with the Warsaw event times. Calendar export works locally; it does not register or reserve a place.
