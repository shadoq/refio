01 Program & Concept

ARTIFACT: Museum Night opens the sanctified halls of The Old Gallery to an evening of curated experiments, sensory cinema, and conversations with contemporary practitioners. Guests move through illuminated corridors, encountering suspended sculptures, projections, and velvet-draped acoustic lounges.

After-hours exhibitions

Bespoke installations from Warsaw's leading creatives, unveiled beneath soft golden lights.

Live performances

Guided hidden-room tours

03 Venue

The Old Gallery

Plac Defilad 1, Warsaw

Travel Tips: Arrive via Nowy Świat or Świętokrzyska metro and follow the golden banners into the museum courtyard.

Accessibility & Transport

Wheelchair access points at the main entrance. Elevators available through the Corinthian foyer. Limited VIP parking available with prior request; bike stands provided.

Parking & Public Transport

Parking gates at Aleje Jerozolimskie; park-and-ride metered spots. Shuttle buses run from Central Station every 15 minutes from 18:00. Suggested routes highlight minimal street crossings.
