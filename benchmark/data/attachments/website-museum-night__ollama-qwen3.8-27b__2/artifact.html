ARTIFACT: Museum Night

After Hours Live Performances Hidden Rooms Limited Seats
The Concept

An evening where the museum speaks quietly

When the last visitor leaves, the collection begins to breathe. ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a single, slow evening inside The Old Gallery — exhibitions left open, sound moving through marble halls, and rooms that are usually sealed finally unlocked.

It is not a party. It is an invitation to see the night differently — to listen, to wander, and to let the art do the talking.

✦

After-hours exhibitions

Every gallery stays open until 02:00, lit low and unhurried, so the works can be seen the way they were meant to be felt.

✦

Live performances

Ambient music and spatial sound sessions composed for the architecture, moving slowly through the halls.

✦

Guided hidden-room tours

Curator-led tours into the closed storage wing, where works never shown to the public finally meet the light.
