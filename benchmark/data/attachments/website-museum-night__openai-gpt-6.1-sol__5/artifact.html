06 / Before you arrive

A few things
to know.

The mystery belongs in the galleries, not in the details. Here are the answers to your most likely questions.

Is payment processed on this page?

No. This is a demo landing page with a simulated checkout. No payment details are requested, no money is collected, and confirming does not create a reservation or a valid ticket.

Can I arrive late?

In this illustrative program, arrival is welcome after 19:00 and until the 02:00 close. Timed sessions begin as listed, so arriving late could mean missing a session. There is no real event admission offered here.

Are photos allowed?

The imagined event allows personal, non-flash photography in the main exhibition, unless an artwork is marked otherwise. Flash, tripods, and recording performances would require permission. For any real museum visit, follow that venue’s actual policy.

Is the venue accessible?

The illustrative venue plan includes step-free access, lifts, accessible facilities, and quiet seating. These features are fictional and have not been verified at a real Warsaw venue. Always confirm access needs with the actual venue before a visit.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Before confirming the demo, use the ticket tier selector in the checkout dialog; your total updates immediately. After confirmation, you can reopen any tier to try again. No purchase or reservation exists to amend.

How do I save the event to my calendar?

Choose “Save to Calendar” to download an .ics file, then open it with your calendar app. It lists 18 October, 19:00, through 19 October, 02:00. Times are floating local times intended for Warsaw; set or verify your calendar’s timezone accordingly. The listing is for a demo event.

Who are the artists on this page?

All eight artist identities, biographies, and sessions were invented for this demonstration. The photographs are illustrative art and venue imagery, not portraits of those fictional artists.
