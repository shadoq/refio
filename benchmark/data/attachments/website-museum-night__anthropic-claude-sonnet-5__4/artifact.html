The Concept

An Institution After Dark

When the last visitor leaves and the galleries fall silent, ARTIFACT begins. For one night, The Old Gallery opens its hidden collections, dims its halls to candlelight and gold, and becomes a stage for sound, movement, and quiet revelation. This is not a tour — it is a passage through the building's memory, guided by artists who treat the museum itself as a living instrument.

◆

After-Hours Exhibitions

Rarely-seen works and archival pieces displayed under low, directional light designed to reveal texture and shadow.

♪

Live Performances

Ambient composers and sound artists respond in real time to the architecture of the galleries and sculpture halls.

✦

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group access to restoration rooms and archive spaces normally closed to the public, led by museum curators.

Location

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

ul. Świętokrzyska 14, 00-050 Warsaw, Poland

Arrive via the main entrance on Świętokrzyska street. Cloakroom and welcome drinks are located just inside the entrance hall — please allow a few minutes for check-in during peak arrival between 19:00 and 20:00.

The venue is fully wheelchair accessible, with ramped entrances, accessible restrooms on the ground and first floors, and an induction hearing loop installed in the main theatre for the Artist Talk.

Limited public parking is available on nearby streets after 19:00. We recommend the M1 metro line to Świętokrzyska station (4 minute walk) or bus lines 111 and 222, both stopping directly outside the gallery.
