Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night

What is ARTIFACT: Museum Night? ▼

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a premium evening event at The Old Gallery in Warsaw. Join us for an unforgettable night of art, culture, and discovery with exclusive access, live performances, and curated experiences.

When does the event take place? ▼

The event runs from 19:00 to 02:00 on October 18, 2026. Doors open at 19:00 with the first guided tour at 19:30.

Is there a dress code? ▼

We suggest smart casual attire. For VIP tickets, we recommend semi-formal wear to match the ambiance of the historic venue.

Can I bring guests? ▼

Tickets are non-transferable and cannot be shared. Each ticket is for one person only. Please purchase separate tickets for each attendee.

Is parking available? ▼

Street parking is available nearby. We also recommend using public transportation — Tram 17 stops at Stare Miasto, just 2 minutes from the gallery.

What happens after the event? ▼

The gallery reopens normally the following morning. All purchased items and drinks can be taken with you. Thank you for being part of our special evening!
