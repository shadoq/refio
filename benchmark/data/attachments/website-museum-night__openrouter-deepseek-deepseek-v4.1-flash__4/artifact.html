The Concept

When the museum becomes the night

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms The Old Gallery into a living, breathing cultural experience. Wander through exhibitions after dark, encounter live performances between the sculptures, and follow a guide into rooms rarely opened to the public.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Explore the permanent collection and a special late-night installation, lit for the dark and designed to be seen slowly.

02

Live performances

Ambient composers, projection artists and performers weave sound and light through the halls across the night.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group tours unlock the vault level and private rooms, guided by curators who know every secret.

Venue

The Old Gallery

Getting there

The Old Gallery sits in the heart of the city, a short walk from the river and the old town. Follow the golden lights along Muzealna Street.

Accessibility

Step-free access throughout, lifts to all floors, a hearing loop in the auditorium, and assistance dogs are welcome. Accessible restrooms on every level.

Parking & public transport

Metro M1 (Muzeum Station, 4 min walk), tram lines 4, 15, 18 and bus 128 stop nearby. A covered car park is available on Muzealna 14.
