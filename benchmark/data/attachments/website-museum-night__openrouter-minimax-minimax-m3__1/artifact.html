The Concept

An evening where the museum
stays awake.

ARTIFACT is a one-night-only cultural happening that transforms The Old Gallery into a velvet-dark stage for art, sound, and conversation. The lights dim, the doors close to the public, and a new kind of audience steps inside.

Expect intimate encounters with rarely seen works, ambient performances in the sculpture court, and conversations with the curators who brought the collection to life. No rush. No crowds. Just the museum, after dark.

01 /

After-hours exhibitions

Wander the main halls in near-silence, with curated lighting and no daytime crowds.

02 /

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection pieces, and a closing sound session in the atrium.

03 /

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group access to the West Wing's rarely opened storage rooms.

The Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw.

The Old Gallery

14 Krakowskie Przedmieście, Warsaw

  • Getting there Metro line M1 — stop "Krakowskie Przedmieście" (3 min walk). Trams 4, 14, 18 stop on the square.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is payment processed on this page?+
No. This is a demo landing page. The reservation flow simulates a confirmation but does not charge any card or process real payment.
Can I arrive late?+
Yes. Doors stay open throughout the night. Please note that some guided tours (Hidden Collection, Artist Talk) have fixed start times and may not be joinable after they begin.
Are photos allowed?+
Non-flash photography is welcome in the main halls. Some rooms and performances are marked as no-photo — please respect the signs and the artists.
Is the venue accessible?+
Yes. The Old Gallery has a step-free side entrance, accessible restrooms on every floor, and reserved seating in the lecture room. Contact us in advance if you need specific accommodations.
Can I change my ticket tier?+
In this demo, tier changes are not processed. In a real event, you would receive a confirmation email with a link to upgrade or modify your reservation up to 48 hours before doors open.
How do I save the event to my calendar?+
Click the "Save to Calendar" button in the hero section. It will download an .ics file you can open with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or any other calendar app.
