ARTIFACT: Museum Night

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

The Experience

Step into a world where art comes alive under the cover of darkness. ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms our galleries into an immersive journey through creativity, mystery, and culture. Experience exclusive access to hidden collections, live performances, and intimate conversations with renowned artists in an atmosphere of quiet luxury.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Discover artworks in a completely different light. Our evening illumination reveals textures and details you've never seen before.

Live Performances

Immerse yourself in ambient soundscapes and visual projections that transform the gallery spaces into living art installations.

Hidden Room Tours

Join our curators for exclusive access to private collections and rarely opened spaces that remain closed to the general public.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

Address: ulica Artystów 15, 00-123 Warsaw, Poland

Travel Tips: Located in the historic district, easily accessible by tram lines 17 and 22. The nearest metro station is Powiśle (Line 2), approximately 10 minutes walk away.

Accessibility: The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps at all entrances. Restrooms on each floor are equipped for accessibility needs.

Parking: Limited street parking available. Public parking garage located at ulica Marszałkowska 45, 5 minutes walk.

Public Transport: Tram 17, 22; Bus 138, 142; Metro Line 2 (Powiśle)
