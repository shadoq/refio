01 / The experience

07

Hours outside the ordinary

Not just a visit.
A different way to see.

Leave the daylight at the door. ARTIFACT imagines a museum transformed: corridors become listening rooms, familiar works reveal unfamiliar details, and every turn holds a quiet discovery.

Move at your own pace between exhibitions, intimate performances, and conversations with the people who shape them. Less spectacle. More presence.

I.

After-hours exhibitions

Encounter paintings and sculptural works in a softer light, with space to pause and look a little longer.

II.

Live performances

Ambient sound, moving light, and intimate live sets composed for the architecture around you.

III.

Guided hidden-room tours

Step beyond the public galleries into imagined archives and collections, guided by a curator’s eye.

06 / Before you arrive

A few things
to know.

A little clarity, before the mystery. Everything here describes a fictional event concept.

Is payment processed on this page?

No. This is a demo landing page. Prices are illustrative, the reservation form runs only in your browser, and no payment information is requested. No actual ticket, booking, or email is issued.

Can I arrive late?

In this imagined program, entry is available after 19:00 until the final session begins at 01:00. You might miss a timed tour or performance if you arrive later. This is an illustrative policy, not a real admission guarantee.

Are photos allowed?

The concept welcomes personal, non-flash photography in the main galleries, with no tripods or filming during performances. Individual works could have restrictions at a real event; always follow the actual venue’s guidance.

Is the venue accessible?

The fictional venue concept includes a step-free entrance, lifts, accessible toilets, and quiet seating. These facilities have not been verified and do not refer to a real location. For a real event, confirm your access needs with its organizer before traveling.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Open any demo ticket option, then use the ticket-tier selector before confirming. The illustrative total updates immediately. There is no stored reservation to amend afterward; you can simply try the demo again.

How do I save the event to my calendar?

Choose “Save to Calendar” in the hero to download artifact-museum-night.ics, then import it into your calendar app. It covers 18 October 2026 at 19:00 through 19 October at 02:00. The file uses floating local times without a timezone; set or interpret the event in Warsaw local time, especially if your calendar is in another timezone. It is a fictional event reminder, not a booking.
