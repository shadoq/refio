The Concept

A museum that only exists at night

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into a candle-lit labyrinth of art, sound and story — for a single night each year.

When the last visitor leaves and the doors are locked, the museum begins its second life. For one night only, ARTIFACT reopens The Old Gallery with spotlit masterpieces, live sound and rooms that usually stay locked — a slow, cinematic wander through the collection after dark.

After-hours exhibitions

Twelve galleries stay open past midnight, re-lit by candle-warm spotlights and slow, cinematic soundscapes tuned to each room.

Live performances

Ambient sets, string quartets and sound artists perform inside the collection — each piece composed for the room it inhabits.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group walks through the vaults, restoration studios and staff corridors that the public never sees — limited slots each hour.

The Artists

Voices of the night

Eight voices shape the night — curators, composers and conservators, each assigned to a single room and a single hour.

Dr. Ewa Marszałek

Head Curator

Opens the vaults she has guarded for two decades and tells the story the daylight labels never tell.

Nikolai Brandt

Sound Artist

Composes slow, unfolding drones from field recordings captured inside the gallery after closing time.

Camille Okafor

Installation Artist

Weaves suspended rooms of cloth and low light that appear only once your eyes adjust to the dark.

Tomasz Wierzbicki

Light Designer

Tunes candle-warm spotlights that drift through the collection like patient, searching hands.

Mira Solvang

Performance Artist

Performs a silent, hours-long procession that drifts through the wings as part of the exhibition itself.

Prof. Ada Lindqvist

Art Historian

Reads the night’s route like a score, tracing gold, shadow and quiet theft across four centuries.

Jonas Ferreira

Projection Artist

Maps generative light onto marble until the sculpture hall breathes in slow gold and navy.

Zofia Kieślak

Conservator

Keeper of the vaults; tonight she opens the restoration studio and each painting’s secret history.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, after hours

A 19th-century landmark in the heart of the Old Town — marble halls, a glass rotunda and vaults that stay locked by day.

The Old Gallery

12 Świętojańska Street · Old Town · Warsaw

Getting There

Trams 4, 6 and 13 stop at “Zamkowy”, two minutes from the gates. Take metro line M1 to Świętokrzyska, then a twelve-minute walk through the Old Town. Night buses N11 and N21 run back to the centre after the event.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance and lifts to all floors, a quiet room on level −1, and an induction loop in the lecture room. Please allow a little extra time for the cobbled street outside the main gate.

Parking

No on-site parking — surrounding streets close for the Old Town night zone. Paid lots at Puławska and Chmielna sit a short ride away, so we warmly recommend arriving by public transport.
