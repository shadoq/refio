06 Before you arrive

A few
good questions.

A little clarity, so you can leave room for curiosity.

Will I be charged for a reservation?

No. This is a fictional event and a demonstration website. The reservation form simulates a confirmation entirely in your browser. No payment is taken, no ticket is issued, and your personal details are not stored or transmitted.

Can I arrive later in the evening?

In the imagined program, entry begins at 19:00 and the night ends at 02:00 on 19 October. Later arrival is part of the concept, but timed sessions start as listed. For a real event, always check the organizer's last-entry policy.

Can I take photographs?

The proposed approach is personal photography without flash, while respecting performers, other visitors, and artwork restrictions. This is not a verified venue policy; a real museum's posted rules take precedence.

Is the event accessible?

The concept is designed around step-free entry, rest seating, and a quiet space. Performances may include low light, amplified sound, and projections. No real facilities have been verified. Contact an actual organizer to confirm your specific access requirements before booking or travelling.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes, within this demo: change the tier in the reservation dialog before confirming. The total updates immediately. To explore another option after confirmation, close the dialog and start a new demo reservation. Nothing is saved or purchased.

How does the calendar download work?

“Save to Calendar” downloads an .ics file you can import into your calendar. It starts at 19:00 on 18 October 2026 and ends at 02:00 on 19 October. Times are floating local times intended for Warsaw; check the imported event if your calendar uses another time zone. The download is not a ticket or booking.

What does program access include?

The Night Pass concept includes the exhibitions and general program sessions. The hidden collection tour is reserved for Curator and Patron tiers. Reserved artist talk seating is also a Curator and Patron benefit; the Patron concept adds lounge access, a print, and priority entrance.
