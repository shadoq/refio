Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night

What are the exact opening hours?

+

The event runs from 19:00 to 02:00, giving you 7 hours of immersive cultural experiences. Doors open at 18:30 for early arrivals.

Is photography allowed?

+

Photography is allowed in common areas and during featured presentations. Please refrain from photographing private works or during intimate performances.

What should I wear?

+

We recommend elegant evening attire. The venue maintains a sophisticated atmosphere, and comfortable shoes are advised for walking through the galleries.

Are there parking facilities?

+

Yes, we have a dedicated valet parking service. Additional information will be provided with your ticket confirmation.

What payment methods are accepted?

+

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for ticket purchases. No real payment processing is involved - this is a reservation system.

Can I cancel my reservation?

+

Yes, you can cancel up to 48 hours before the event for a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours are subject to a 50% administration fee.
