Execution controls

Files stay on your device. Loading a ROM pauses and resets the machine.

Compatibility quirks

Quirks apply immediately. Defaults use VX for shifts, leave I unchanged on transfers, and clip sprites at the display edges.

Start here

Press Run to try the built-in sprite ROM. Move with W / A / S / D (CHIP-8 keys 5 / 7 / 8 / 9). Hold Q (key 4) to beep. Touch the keypad to play on mobile.

The demo is real CHIP-8 bytecode: it XOR-erases and redraws its sprite, moves at about 20 Hz using the delay timer, and calls a sound subroutine.

Fonts live at 0x050; programs start at 0x200. Timers tick at 60 Hz independently of CPU speed, including during a key wait. Pausing freezes timers; single step executes one opcode without ticking timers.

Fx0A accepts a fresh key press, not a key already held. It completes on press, including while paused. Switching tabs or losing window focus releases all keys and pauses. SYS (0nnn) is a no-op: native COSMAC calls are unsupported. This is classic CHIP-8, not SUPER-CHIP; Dxy0 draws zero rows.
