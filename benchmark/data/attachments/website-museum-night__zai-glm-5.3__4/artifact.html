The Concept

One Night. Every Gallery. After Dark.

When the last daytime visitor leaves, the Old Gallery belongs to the night. Lights dim to a low amber hum, hidden doors unlock, and the collection is seen the way few ever see it — slowly, quietly, under golden spotlights.

After-hours exhibitions

Wander halls where the works are lit only by curated spotlight. Each room is scored for slow looking — no crowds, no glare, no hurry.

Live performances

Sound artists, projection mappers and dancers reshape the galleries hour by hour. The building itself becomes an instrument after midnight.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups slip behind the scenes into storage and conservation rooms, where works kept from public view return for a single night.

The Program

Seven Hours, Hour by Hour

A single continuous night, composed like an exhibition. Arrive at seven; leave when the lights return.

    Doors Open

    Pick up your pass, follow the candle-lit corridor into the foyer and collect your night map.

    Main Foyer
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    Isabelle Marchand opens the night with a short address on light, silence and slow looking.

    Auditorium
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    A rare walkthrough of works normally kept in storage, led by the gallery's conservation team.

    Hidden Rooms
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    Elara Voss and Kenji Mori unfold a slow-moving score of sound and light across the main hall.

    Grand Hall
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Marble and bronze are reimagined in a forty-minute projection mapping sequence.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    Dr. Henrik Alvarsson and guests discuss what museums lose — and gain — when the lights go down.

    Auditorium

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    The Long Gallery opens in near darkness; walk it slowly, guided only by low amber light.

    The Long Gallery
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    Nikolai Brandt closes the night with a quietly monumental set of sub-bass and sparse piano.

    Grand Hall

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A century-old gallery between the Old Town and the river — all four floors open for one night only.

The Old Gallery

Mariensztat 12, 00-042 Warsaw, Poland

Getting Here

  • Metro M2 — Nowy Świat station, 8 minutes on foot; M1 — Centrum, 12 minutes.
  • Trams along Wybrzeże Kościuszkowskie stop two streets away, running all night on weekends.
  • Evening parking is limited — the gallery car park holds 30 cars and fills before 19:30.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance from Mariensztat street.
  • Elevator to all exhibition floors, including the hidden rooms.
  • Accessible restrooms on the ground floor.
  • A quiet room with soft seating, available all night.
