06 / Before you arrive

Good to know.

A few details to make your evening effortless.

Is payment processed on this page?

No. This is a demo: no actual payment is processed, and submitting the form does not create a booking or a real ticket.

Can I arrive late?

Yes. Doors open at 19:00, and you can arrive later. Check the program for scheduled tours, talks and performances you do not want to miss.

Are photos allowed?

Personal photography without flash is welcome in most areas. Follow on-site signage and staff guidance where restrictions apply.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free entry and accessible facilities are planned. Contact the venue in advance to confirm arrangements for your individual needs.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Close the demo reservation form and select another tier from the ticket cards. No booking or payment is made.

How do I save the event to my calendar?

Click the “Save to Calendar” button in the hero section to download an .ics calendar file for the event.
