The Concept

Where Art Meets
the Witching Hour

ARTIFACT transforms Warsaw's most storied gallery into an immersive nocturnal experience. After the last visitor leaves, the museum awakens — bathed in golden light, alive with performance, music, and mystery.

Wander through candlelit corridors, witness live art being created, and lose yourself in sound installations that breathe new life into centuries-old masterpieces.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

The Old Gallery

ul. Królewska 12, Warsaw, Poland

Built in 1887, The Old Gallery is Warsaw's most celebrated cultural institution. Its neo-classical halls, vaulted ceilings, and intimate courtyards create the perfect stage for an evening that blurs the line between observer and participant.

The building has hosted over 200 years of artistic legacy — and on the night of October 18th, it becomes something entirely new.

Questions

Frequently Asked

ARTIFACT is an annual premium after-dark museum experience held at The Old Gallery in Warsaw. The event transforms the museum into an immersive nocturnal world of live art, performance, music, and mystery — open exclusively to ticketed guests from 19:00 to 02:00.
ARTIFACT is an 18+ event. Valid photo ID will be required at the door. Guests under 18 will not be admitted, regardless of ticket status.
We encourage smart-casual to formal attire. Think: gallery opening meets late-night salon. Dark tones, elegant fabrics, and personal expression are all welcome. Sportswear and casual streetwear are discouraged.
Yes — ticket upgrades are available subject to availability. Contact us at tickets@artifact-night.pl with your booking reference and desired tier. Upgrades are processed within 48 hours.
The Midnight Salon is an intimate gathering of up to 30 guests with selected artists. Held in the museum's private reading room, it features wine, conversation, and an exclusive preview of unreleased works. Access is included with Premium and Patron tickets.
Personal photography is welcome throughout the event. Professional photography equipment (tripods, external flash, lenses over 50mm) requires prior accreditation. Please be respectful of artists and fellow guests when photographing.
The Old Gallery is located at ul. Królewska 12, Warsaw. The nearest metro station is Centrum (M1/M2 lines), a 5-minute walk. Tram lines 7, 9, and 22 stop directly outside. Limited parking is available on ul. Marszałkowska. We recommend public transport.
Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. 50% refunds are available between 30 and 14 days prior. No refunds are issued within 14 days of the event. Tickets may be transferred to another person at any time — contact us to arrange this.
