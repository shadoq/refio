06   /   Good to know

A few things to know.

A little clarity before you step into the dark.

Explore passes ↗
Is this a real event?

No. ARTIFACT: Museum Night is a fictional demonstration website. The venue, program, prices, artists, and reservations are illustrative; please do not make travel plans based on this page.

What does a Night Pass include?

The example Night Pass includes entry after 19:00, the main exhibitions, and general program access. The hidden collection tour is reserved for Curator and Patron passes.

Can I arrive after the doors open?

In this example program, doors open at 19:00 and the evening continues until 02:00. Arriving later would mean missing earlier scheduled activities.

Are the hidden-room tours included?

The 20:00 Hidden Collection Tour is listed as part of the Curator Pass and Patron Pass. It is not included in the Night Pass.

Is the venue accessible?

The fictional event plan describes a step-free entrance and lift access. This is not verified venue information; always contact a real venue directly before visiting.

Do you send a ticket after confirmation?

No. The checkout is an on-page demonstration only. It does not collect payment, transmit your information, create a booking, or send a ticket.

Can I get a refund or change my pass?

No real purchase takes place, so there is nothing to refund or change. The passes shown here exist solely to demonstrate the ticket-selection experience.
