The Concept

The museum after dark

When the last visitor leaves and the alarms hush, the museum changes. For one night in October, The Old Gallery opens its doors after dark — velvet darkness, golden spotlights, and the quiet mystery of rooms that usually sleep. Wander the collection by lamplight, follow sound through the sculpture hall, and step into hidden rooms the public never sees. This is the museum as art-house cinema: one night where the collection breathes.

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection and a special nocturne exhibition remain open until 02:00, lit for the night.

Live performances

Ambient sets, projection art and sound sessions unfold through the night, room by room.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups step into the storage rooms and the closed cabinet wing, keys in hand.

Good to know

  • Arrive 15 minutes early — doors open at 19:00 sharp.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance on the north side.

  • Accessible restrooms on every level.
