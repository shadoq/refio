Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What should I wear? +
We recommend smart-casual or evening attire. The event takes place indoors in a historic building, so comfortable shoes are advised for walking and standing.
Can I bring food? +
Outside food is not permitted. However, we will have a selection of artisanal snacks and beverages available for purchase throughout the night.
Is photography allowed? +
Yes, personal photography is encouraged! Please refrain from using flash during performances. Some specific installations may have no-photography zones, which will be clearly marked.
What if I need to cancel? +
Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person up to 48 hours before the event. Please contact us with your booking details if you need assistance.
Is there wheelchair accessibility? +
Yes, The Old Gallery is fully accessible. Elevators and ramps are available to all areas of the venue. Please contact us in advance if you require specific accommodations.
