Keypad

CHIP-8 key → keyboard. Press and release are both tracked. Hold keys 5/6/7/8 in the demo to move the sprite.

About

Complete CHIP-8 interpreter: 4 KB RAM, font at 0x050, programs at 0x200, all 35 opcodes, 60 Hz delay/sound timers, XOR sprites with VF collision flag. The demo ROM is assembled inline — no downloads needed.
