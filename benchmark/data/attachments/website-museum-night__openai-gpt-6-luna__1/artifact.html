A different kind of looking

The museum,
after its last light.

At night, the gallery changes its rhythm. Familiar rooms become cinematic; quiet corners begin to speak. ARTIFACT invites you to wander without hurry through a living collection shaped by artists, curators, and the city around us.

Getting here

Świętokrzyska metro is a short walk away. City buses stop nearby at Zachęta. Plan for a 7-minute walk from the metro.

By car

Limited paid parking is available on surrounding streets and at nearby public car parks. Public transport is recommended.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, lift access to public galleries, accessible toilets, and seating throughout. Contact the venue ahead for assistance.

Arrival

Doors open at 19:00. Please have your reservation confirmation ready at the main entrance.
