The Experience

A Night of Art & Mystery

Step into a world where art comes alive under the cover of darkness. ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms The Old Gallery into an immersive journey through centuries of creativity.

Guided by expert curators, you'll explore hidden corners, discover rare artifacts, and witness installations that exist only after hours. This is not merely a visit — it's an experience.

Join us for an evening of wonder, where every shadow tells a story and every light reveals a masterpiece.

The Space

The Old Gallery

Located in the heart of Warsaw's historic district, The Old Gallery has been carefully restored to its former glory while embracing modern art presentation.

With its soaring ceilings, original architectural details, and state-of-the-art lighting systems, the venue provides the perfect canvas for our evening experience.

The gallery features three main halls, each designed for different types of artistic expression — from intimate installations to grand exhibitions.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this event suitable for children?

+

While the event is designed for adults, children over 12 may attend with a paying adult. We recommend checking with our team for specific age restrictions on certain installations.

Can I bring a guest?

+

Yes! You may bring one guest at no additional cost. Simply mention it when purchasing your ticket, and we'll note it on your reservation.

What if I need to cancel?

+

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the event are fully refundable. Cancellations within 48 hours receive a 50% refund. No-shows are non-refundable.

Is parking available?

+

Yes, there is a nearby parking garage on ul. Marszałkowska that offers reserved spots for ticket holders. Premium and Platinum ticket holders receive a complimentary parking pass.

What should I wear?

+

We suggest smart casual attire. The venue has a relaxed dress code, but we encourage you to dress comfortably for an evening of exploration and socializing.

Is there a dress code?

+

No formal dress code, but we recommend smart casual attire. The venue has a relaxed dress code, but we encourage you to dress comfortably for an evening of exploration and socializing.
