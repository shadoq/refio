The Experience

A Cinematic Journey Through Art & Sound

We transform the city's cultural landmarks into a single, immersive stage. As night falls, spotlights replace daylight, and classical music blends with ambient soundscapes.

This is not just an exhibition; it is a living narrative where history meets modernity in a dance of shadows and light.

The Night's Flow

The Program

19:00 – 20:30

Welcome Reception & Opening Keynote

Guests are welcomed with a signature cocktail. The opening speech by the museum director sets the tone for the evening.

21:00 – 22:30

The Symphony of Shadows

A live string quartet performs classical pieces in the main hall, accompanied by a light projection show.

23:00 – 01:00

Late Night Jazz & Art Talks

The atmosphere shifts to a lounge setting with jazz music and intimate talks by contemporary artists.

01:30 – 02:00

Farewell & Midnight Toast

A final collective moment of reflection before the event concludes.
