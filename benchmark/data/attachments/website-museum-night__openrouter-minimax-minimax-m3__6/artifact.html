The Concept

An evening where the gallery becomes the stage.

For one night only, the museum sheds its daytime stillness and opens its doors to a different kind of visitor — the curious, the nocturnal, the seekers of atmosphere. ARTIFACT is not a viewing. It is a slow, sensory passage through eight hours of curated sound, light, and conversation.

No crowds. No rush. Just rooms that breathe differently after dark.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A 19th-century neoclassical building on the western edge of the city, originally a private collection for the aristocracy and converted into a public museum in 1932. Twelve halls. Two courtyards. One hidden cellar.
