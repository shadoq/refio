The Concept

In the quiet hours after closing, when the crowds have dispersed and the lights dim to a golden glow, the museum transforms. ARTIFACT is not just an exhibition; it is an immersion.

We invite you to walk through history as if time has paused for your arrival alone. Guided by ambient soundscapes and curated lighting, discover the hidden details of masterpieces usually lost in daylight noise.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

Built in 1895, this architectural masterpiece combines neoclassical grandeur with modern exhibition spaces. Tonight, the main hall and the underground archives will be open exclusively to ticket holders.
