The Concept

When the Gallery Closes,
the Real Exhibition Begins

An after-hours cultural experience where art, sound, and silence collide under velvet darkness.

ARTIFACT: Museum Night is not a typical gallery opening. It is a carefully orchestrated evening where the boundaries between performance, installation, and contemplation dissolve. The galleries become stages. The artworks become witnesses. The visitors become participants.

Across six interconnected spaces, curated artists respond to the architecture, the history, and the silence of The Old Gallery. Each room offers a different frequency of experience — from immersive soundscapes to intimate live readings, from projected light to tactile installations.

This is a night designed to be felt, not just seen. Come alone. Come with someone. But come with attention.

The Program

Seven Hours of
Unfolding Experience

A flowing timeline of performances, installations, and moments of stillness.

19:00

Doors Open — Arrival & Silence

Guests enter through the main hall. No announcements. No music. Only the sound of footsteps on marble and the weight of anticipation.

Welcome
19:45

Opening Ritual — "First Light"

A single spotlight activates. A spoken-word artist performs an original piece written for this building, this night, this moment.

Performance
20:30

Room 1 — "Echoes in Marble"

Immersive sound installation. Spatial audio responds to movement. Visitors wear provided headphones that transform the sculpture garden into a living instrument.

Installation
21:15

Room 2 — "Velvet Archive"

An interactive archive where guests contribute their own artifacts — objects, stories, photographs — to a temporary collective memory.

Interactive
22:00

Room 3 — "Frequency"

Live electronic music performed in the rotunda. Modular synthesizers, field recordings, and ambient textures create a continuous soundscape.

Music
23:00

Room 4 — "Shadow Play"

Projection mapping transforms the east wall into a canvas of moving light. Collaborative work between a visual artist and a choreographer.

Visual
00:15

Room 5 — "The Last Gallery"

A single painting illuminated. A violinist plays. No one speaks. This is the moment the event was built toward.

Climax
01:00

Closing — "Farewell in Amber"

Guests receive a small amber glass with a handwritten note. The lights slowly rise. The gallery returns to silence.

The Artists

Twelve Voices,
One Night

Curated performers and creators responding to the architecture of The Old Gallery.

Marek Nowak

Spoken Word

Warsaw-based poet and performer. His piece "First Light" was written exclusively for this venue.

Elena Voss

Sound Artist

Berlin-based sound designer known for immersive spatial audio installations in historic spaces.

Kai Tanaka

Electronic Music

Tokyo-born, Warsaw-based musician working with modular synthesis and field recordings.

Sofia Reyes

Visual Artist

Mexican-Lithuanian artist specializing in projection mapping and light-based installations.

Jan Kowalski

Violinist

Classically trained, spiritually driven. Performs in galleries, churches, and abandoned spaces.

Ava Lindström

Choreographer

Stockholm-based choreographer exploring the relationship between movement and architecture.
