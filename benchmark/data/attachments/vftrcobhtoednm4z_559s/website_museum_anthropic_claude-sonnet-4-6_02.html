The Concept

Where Art Breathes
After Dark

ARTIFACT is not a museum visit. It is an immersion — a curated journey through living art, sound, and light that transforms The Old Gallery into something entirely new after the sun sets.

Eight international artists. Seven gallery rooms. One extraordinary night. From immersive sound installations to live performance art, every corner holds a discovery.

The Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw

Built in 1887 and restored to its original grandeur in 2019, The Old Gallery is Warsaw's most storied cultural space. Seven interconnected rooms, each with its own character and history.

On the night of ARTIFACT, the gallery's 3,200 square metres become a living canvas — every corridor, alcove, and stairwell part of the experience.
