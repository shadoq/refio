The Concept

Where Art Comes Alive After Dark

For one extraordinary night, The Old Gallery opens its doors to reveal hidden treasures, immersive performances, and encounters with art that transcend the ordinary. ARTIFACT is not an exhibition — it is an experience.

🌙

After-Hours Exhibitions

Walk through centuries of art under the glow of curated spotlights, with no crowds, no rush.

🎭

Live Performances

Ambient soundscapes, spoken word, and projection art transform gallery halls into living canvases.

🔦

Hidden-Room Tours

Guided access to vaults, restoration studios, and spaces normally closed to the public.

🚇 Public Transport

The gallery is a 5-minute walk from Metro Świętokrzyska. Tram lines 18, 35 stop at Królewska.

♿ Accessibility

Full wheelchair access. Elevator to all floors. Accessible restrooms on each level. Audio description devices available at reception.

🚗 Parking

Limited on-site parking (reservation recommended). Public garage at Plac Zamkowy, 200m.
