The Concept

Where Art Meets the Night

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms Warsaw's most iconic cultural institution into a living canvas. For one extraordinary evening, the museum comes alive with immersive installations, live performances, and curated experiences that blur the boundaries between art, architecture, and atmosphere.

5 Hours
12 Artists
3 Galleries
The Experience

A Night of Wonder

Five hours of immersive art, live performances, and curated experiences across the museum's most extraordinary spaces.

19:00 – 19:30

Grand Opening

Enter through the illuminated courtyard with a welcome champagne toast and live string quartet performance.

📍 Courtyard Entrance

19:30 – 20:30

After-Hours Exhibitions

Exclusive access to normally restricted galleries, lit by dramatic spotlight installations.

📍 Main Galleries A & B

20:30 – 21:30

Live Performances

Contemporary dance, ambient soundscapes, and spoken word poetry performed throughout the gallery spaces.

📍 Grand Hall

21:00 – 22:30

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group journeys through secret passages and hidden chambers with resident curators.

📍 West Wing Corridors

22:00 – 23:00

Artisan Workshops

Hands-on sessions with resident artists — marble casting, fresco techniques, and contemporary printmaking.

📍 Studio Wing

22:30 – 23:30

Rooftop Cinema

An open-air screening of rare art-house films projected against the museum's neoclassical façade under the stars.

📍 Rooftop Terrace

23:30 – 01:00

Midnight Gala

Close the night with a candlelit gathering in the Grand Hall, featuring live jazz and artisan cocktails.

📍 Grand Hall

01:00 – 02:00

Farewell & Departure

A final toast and commemorative gift as guests depart under the museum's illuminated dome.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

Housed in a meticulously restored 19th-century neoclassical building in the heart of Warsaw, The Old Gallery has been a cultural landmark for over 140 years.

Address

ul. Marszałkowska 12, 00-014 Warsaw, Poland

Getting There

5 min walk from Politechnika Metro Station. Parking available at ul. Nowowiejska.

Accessibility

Fully wheelchair accessible. Elevator access to all floors. Assistance available upon request.

Parking

Underground parking at ul. Nowowiejska (100m). Valet service available on event night.

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about the event.

The event begins at 19:00 with the Grand Opening and concludes at 02:00 with the Midnight Gala. Doors open at 18:30 for early entry. We recommend arriving 15 minutes before your scheduled tour time if you have one.

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible. All floors are connected by elevators, and there are designated accessible viewing areas throughout the venue. Please contact us in advance if you require specific accommodations.

Tickets can be refunded up to 14 days before the event (October 4, 2026). Between October 4–17, a 50% refund is available. No refunds are issued within 72 hours of the event. Tickets can be transferred to another person at any time.

We recommend smart elegant attire. Think cocktail dress, suit, or creative formal wear. The atmosphere is sophisticated but not rigidly formal. Patron ticket holders will receive a dress code guide upon purchase.

Yes! All ticket types include a welcome drink. Curator and Patron ticket holders receive premium cocktails throughout the evening. Artisan canapés will be available for purchase at various stations. A full gala dinner is included with Patron tickets.

Personal photography without flash is welcome in most areas. Professional photography equipment (DSLR, tripods, lighting) requires prior approval. Flash photography is prohibited in all gallery spaces to protect the artworks.

Yes! Groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount. Contact us at groups@artifactnight.com for custom group packages, private tours, and corporate event options.
