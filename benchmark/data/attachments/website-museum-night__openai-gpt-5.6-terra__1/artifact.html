The invitation

When the gallery exhales.

ARTIFACT is an after-dark encounter with the museum at its most intimate. Wander through illuminated collections, pause for live interventions, and follow curators beyond the familiar route.

Designed for attentive looking rather than hurried viewing, this is a single night of art held in a slower, stranger light.

Find the entrance

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Plac Sztuki 12
00-123 Warsaw

Enter through the historic east doors on Plac Sztuki. Doors open at 19:00; please allow a little time for cloakroom and security.

  • Public transport: Metro M2, Rondo ONZ station, is an 8-minute walk. Buses 107 and 160 stop at Plac Sztuki.
  • Parking: Limited paid street parking is available after 18:00; the Centrum underground car park is 350 metres away.
  • Accessibility: Step-free entry, lifts to every level, accessible toilets, seating in all galleries, and assistance animals are welcome. Please contact us in advance for a companion ticket or tailored assistance.
  • Travel tip: Bring a light layer—the sculpture hall is beautifully cool after dark.
