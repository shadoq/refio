The Concept

One night, after the doors close

A single evening in which the museum belongs to the night — and to those curious enough to stay.

When the last visitor leaves and the doors are locked, the gallery begins its second life. Under low golden light, the paintings seem to breathe, and the marble corridors hum with quiet soundscapes composed for a single night. ARTIFACT invites you to wander freely — through halls rehung for darkness, into rooms the public never sees, and along routes guided only by your own curiosity.

Every edition of ARTIFACT is built once, for one night only — and dismantled by sunrise.

After-hours exhibitions

Three floors rehung under curated spotlight, with works lit, staged and sequenced for the dark.

Live performances

String quartets, ambient electronic sets and movement pieces performed inside the galleries themselves.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group walks through the vaults, the restoration studio and the gallery's sealed east wing.

The Program

One night, hour by hour

From first entry to the final echo — the evening unfolds in eight movements.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    Velvet ropes drop. Collect your pass at the gilded desk and step into the dimmed grand hall for a welcome pour.

    Grand Hall
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    Head curator Dr. Irena Volkova opens the night with a short talk on light, darkness and the art of looking slowly.

    Main Rotunda
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    Small guided groups slip into the vaults and the restoration studio — rooms never shown in daylight hours.

    Vaults & Studio
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    Elena Vasquez performs a candle-lit ambient set beneath the atrium skylight, composed for the museum's acoustics.

    Atrium
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Marc Delacroix wraps the marble hall in slow-moving projection art — light drawings that climb the statues.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    An intimate on-stage conversation with the featured artists on making work for a single night. Reserved seating for pass holders.

    Auditorium
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    The lights drop to embers. Wander all three floors in near-darkness, guided by candle-lines and sound.

    All Floors
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    Jonas Revald closes the night with a quiet vinyl-and-cello set in the courtyard until the last guest drifts home.

    Courtyard

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A 19th-century gallery of marble halls and candlelit corridors — opened after dark for one night only.

Address

The Old Gallery
Senatorska Street 12, 00-075 Warsaw, Poland

Getting here

Metro M1 to Ratusz Arsenał (8-minute walk), or trams 4, 15 and 35 to Senatorska. Evening services run until 01:30; night buses replace them after 02:00.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance on Senatorska, elevator access to all three floors, accessible restrooms on the ground floor, and a quiet room off the atrium. Guide dogs are welcome.

Parking & transport

The gallery has no on-site parking. The nearest garage is at Plac Bankowy, a 6-minute walk away. We recommend arriving by public transport.
