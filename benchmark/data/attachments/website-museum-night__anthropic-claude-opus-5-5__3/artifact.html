The Concept

A museum that only exists after dark

For one night, The Old Gallery closes its doors to daylight and opens them to something quieter, stranger and far more intimate. ARTIFACT turns a two-hundred-year-old collection into a living stage — galleries lit only by spotlight, sculptures washed in projected light, and sound drifting through halls that are usually silent.

Wander at your own pace, follow a curator into rooms never shown to the public, or sit in the rotunda as ambient composers score the architecture itself. There is no right way to experience the night — only your own.

“Every object in a museum was once alive in someone’s hands. At night, you can almost hear them remember.” — Iréne Valesca, Chief Curator

Hall IV · 23:40

  • i.

    After-hours exhibitions

    Three floors of permanent and temporary collections, re-lit by stage designers for the night — every gallery open until 02:00.

  • ii.

    Live performances

    Ambient sound sessions, light projections on marble and a midnight performance that moves through the galleries with you.

  • iii.

    Guided hidden-room tours

    Small groups follow our archivists into the Vault Wing and restoration studios — rooms that have never been open to visitors.

The Program

Seven hours, one long exhale

From the first doors to the final chord — a night designed to unfold slowly. Arrive when you like, stay as long as the dark allows.

      The Venue

      The Old Gallery, Warsaw

      A neoclassical gallery on the Royal Route — marble halls, a domed rotunda and a vault wing sealed for decades.

      Address

      Travel tips

      • Doors open at 19:00 — the first hour is the quietest.
      • Bring your ticket QR on your phone; a cloakroom is available.
      • October nights are cold — the courtyard lounge is outdoors.

      Accessibility

      • Step-free entrance via the Courtyard Gate with lifts to all floors.
      • Seating in every gallery; quiet room on the ground floor.
      • Companion entry is free — mention it when reserving.

      Parking & public transport

      • Metro M2 Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet — 4 minutes on foot.
      • Buses 116, 128, 180 stop at Uniwersytet; night buses after 23:30.
      • Limited parking at Plac Piłsudskiego garage — we recommend transit.
