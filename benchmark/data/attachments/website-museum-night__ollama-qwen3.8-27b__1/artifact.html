The Concept

A night built around the quiet

When the galleries close to the public, the real story begins. ARTIFACT: Museum Night opens the doors of The Old Gallery after dark, turning a single evening into a slow, cinematic journey through art, sound, and space.

There is no rush, no queue, no daylight. Just spotlit works, ambient performance, and conversations with the artists and curators who shaped the collection — all under one roof, from dusk to the small hours.

Bring your curiosity. Leave with a different way of seeing.

After-hours exhibitions

See the permanent collection re-lit for night, with works you rarely notice in daylight.

Live performances

Ambient and experimental sets composed for the architecture of the halls.

Guided hidden-room tours

Curators lead small groups through storage, archives, and rooms closed to the public.

Getting there

Five minutes on foot from the Miodowa metro stop. The nearest bus stops are on ul. Miodowa and ul. Nowy Świat.

Accessibility

Step-free access to all main halls, accessible restrooms, and a quiet room available on request. Service animals welcome.

Parking

Public parking is available on ul. Nowy Świat and in the Miodowa underground garage. Evening rates apply after 19:00.
