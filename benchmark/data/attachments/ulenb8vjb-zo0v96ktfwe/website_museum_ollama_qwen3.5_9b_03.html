The Venue

The Old Gallery

Address: ulica Sztuki 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Travel Tips: Located in the heart of Warsaw\'s cultural district. Easily accessible by metro (Station: Nowy Świat) or tram (Lines: 17, 20).

Accessibility: The venue is fully accessible with elevators, ramps, and accessible restrooms. Audio guides are available in multiple languages.

Parking: Public parking available at nearby garages. Valet service available for ticket holders.
