Museum Night, Reimagined

ARTIFACT: Museum Night

18 Oct 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

Step into a world where art transcends time, where the night breathes stories and the museum becomes a living canvas. ARTIFACT invites you to experience curated exhibitions, live performances, and hidden-room tours that will leave you spellbound.

After‑Hours Exhibitions

Explore masterpieces beyond the usual hours.

Live Performances

Immerse in music and theater under the gallery lights.

Hidden‑Room Tours

Discover secret chambers with guided narratives.
