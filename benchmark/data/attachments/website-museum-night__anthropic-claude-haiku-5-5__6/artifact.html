The night

A museum after dark

An after-hours night of exhibitions, performances and hidden rooms, unfolding under low gold light as the galleries slowly reveal what daylight keeps quiet.

After-hours exhibitions

Galleries open their doors long after closing time, with lighting designed for quiet, close looking.

Live performances

Ambient sets, sound sessions and staged moments that respond to the architecture around them.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups follow our curators through rooms that are rarely opened to the public.

Schedule

Program

Eight moments across the night. Arrive early, wander freely, and let the evening find its own rhythm.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    Welcome into the Main Hall as the lights dim and the collection comes alive.

    Main Hall
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    A brief introduction to the themes and hidden stories behind this year's selection.

    Grand Foyer
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    A guided walk through rooms and works rarely shown to visitors.

    Hidden Rooms
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    A layered sound piece composed for the atrium's acoustic space.

    Atrium
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Light and shadow mapped onto marble, bronze and stone after hours.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    An open conversation about process, memory and the making of new work.

    Lecture Room
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    A slow, unhurried walk through the gallery wing with candlelit stops.

    Gallery Wing
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    The night closes softly in the atrium with a final set of low, resonant sound.

    Atrium

Where

The Old Gallery

Travel tips

  • Arrive before 20:00 for the shortest entry queue.
  • Coat check is available in the Grand Foyer.
  • Cloakroom and water stations are located near the entrance.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance and lift access to all public floors.
  • Accessible restrooms on every level.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome throughout the venue.

Parking & public transport

  • Metro: a short walk from the central metro station on line M1.
  • Bus: stop Muzealna, served by several city routes.
  • Limited paid parking is available on nearby streets.
