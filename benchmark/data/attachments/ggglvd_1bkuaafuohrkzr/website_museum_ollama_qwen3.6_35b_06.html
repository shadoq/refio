The Concept

Where Art Meets
the Darkness

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms the historic walls of The Old Gallery into an immersive nocturnal experience. For one extraordinary evening, masterworks come alive under golden spotlights, while contemporary soundscapes weave through centuries of human creativity.

This is not a conventional gallery visit. It is a cinematic journey — part performance, part exhibition, part ritual — designed for those who believe art should be felt as much as seen.

The Program

An Evening Unfolds

From the first golden hour to the last whisper of midnight, every moment has been choreographed.
