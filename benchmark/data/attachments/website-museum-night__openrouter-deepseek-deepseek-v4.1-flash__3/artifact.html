The Concept

One night. Every gallery. After dark.

When the last visitors leave, The Old Gallery stays awake. For seven hours, its halls, vaults and rooftops become a living stage — exhibitions lit for the night, performances unfolding between sculptures, and doors that usually stay closed opening just for you.

After-hours exhibitions

Wander the permanent collection and a special nocturnal hang, lit by warm spotlights and silence.

Live performances

Ambient sets, durational performance and projection art staged in the Sculpture Hall and atrium.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group tours descend to the vault level and open rooms the public never sees by day.
