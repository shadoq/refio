Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night

What are the dress code requirements?

▼

Dress code is smart casual to formal evening wear. We recommend elegant attire that complements the museum's atmosphere.

Can I bring food and drinks?

▼

Food and drinks are included in ticket prices. We have a full bar service and can accommodate dietary restrictions upon request.

What happens if I miss the event?

▼

All tickets are non-refundable but transferable. You can give your ticket to someone else or save it for future ARTIFACT events.

Is parking available?

▼

Yes, underground parking is available with a surcharge. Please mention during ticket purchase to reserve a spot.

Can I take photographs?

▼

Photography is allowed in common areas with flash disabled. Some installations may have restrictions - our staff will guide you.

What payment methods are accepted?

▼

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. No real payment processing - this is a mock event.
