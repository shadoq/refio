The Experience

Where Art Meets the Night

ARTIFACT transforms The Old Gallery into an immersive nocturnal experience. Wander through illuminated halls, encounter live performances among classical sculptures, and discover hidden spaces rarely opened to the public. This is not a museum visit — it is a passage into art after dark.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Explore the gallery's permanent and temporary collections under dramatic, specially designed lighting that reveals hidden details invisible during daytime visits.

Live Performances

Experience ambient music, sound installations, and projection mapping woven into the architectural fabric of the museum, creating an atmosphere unlike any conventional concert.

Hidden-Room Tours

Gain guided access to restricted archives, restoration workshops, and underground storage vaults — spaces the public rarely sees, illuminated by torchlight.

Getting There
The Old Gallery is centrally located on Warsaw's historic Royal Route, easily accessible by public transport. We recommend arriving by metro or tram to avoid limited parking in the area.

Accessibility
The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with elevator access to all floors, accessible restrooms on each level, and dedicated seating areas in performance spaces. Please contact us in advance for any specific requirements.

Public Transport
Nearest metro: Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet (Line M2), 4-minute walk. Tram stops: Nowy Świat (lines 18, 35), 2-minute walk.

Parking
Limited street parking available on Krakowskie Przedmieście. Nearest paid parking garage: Parking Powiśle, 800m away.
