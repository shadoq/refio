The Concept

One night.
Every hall awake.

When the doors lock and the day crowds drift home, the museum changes state. The light drops, the marble begins to hold sound, and rooms you thought you knew reveal second lives.

ARTIFACT is a single night built around that transformation — eight stops across the halls, from vault tours to projection art mapped on stone, scored slowly and lit like film.

After-hours exhibitions

Every gallery stays open past midnight, re-lit and re-sounded for a slower, closer look at the collection.

Live performances

Ambient sets, drone and strings — slow spatial music performed among the sculptures, not on a stage.

Guided hidden-room tours

Vaults, restoration rooms, and corridors closed to the public — unlocked for one night with a curator.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

A nineteenth-century exhibition house in the heart of Warsaw — vaulted, marble, and mostly unlit until now.

The Old Gallery

ul. Nowogrodzka 18, 00-511 Warsaw, Poland

Getting there

Arrive before 21:00 for the quietest entry. The gallery is a 5-minute walk from Metro Świętokrzyska; trams 10 and 17 stop at "Nowogrodzka" directly outside.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance and elevators to all floors. A quiet room is available on level 1, and large-print programs can be collected at the welcome desk.

Parking & transport

There is no on-site parking — the nearest garage is on ul. Emilii Plater, 200 m away. Taxis can drop off at the main gate, and night buses R60 and W70 run after midnight.
