Gouraud Cube

A little geometry. A moving light. Every pixel computed.

Gouraud shading 12 triangles Running
Keyboard shortcuts work anywhere outside text fields.

Light is calculated at each corner using a moving point light and Lambert’s cosine law. Each face keeps its own outward normal, then splits into two triangles. Perspective-correct RGB interpolation shades their pixels, while a depth buffer resolves visibility. Toggle wireframe to reveal the triangles; pause freezes both the cube and the light. No WebGL, libraries, or image assets.
