The concept

A night in the museum, after the lights should have dimmed

When the last visitors leave and the day’s crowds fall silent, the galleries begin to breathe differently. ARTIFACT: Museum Night invites you into that quieter world — rooms reopened after hours, collections seen in low gold light, and performances that unfold between the sculptures.

Expect a slow, cinematic evening: wander at your own pace, linger in the hidden rooms, and let the program carry you from the first welcome to the last sound before dawn.

After-hours exhibitions

Permanent and rotating collections opened to you in a quieter, more intimate light.

Live performances

Ambient sound, theatre and light works staged among the artworks through the night.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led by curators into rooms that are rarely opened to the public.

The evening

Program

Eight moments across seven hours, moving from the first doors to the closing sound session.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    Step out of the evening and into the museum. Coat check, welcome drinks and the first gallery rooms await.

    Main Hall
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    A short spoken opening from the curatorial team on the themes and objects shaping the night.

    East Wing
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    A guided walk through storerooms and rooms usually closed to visitors, with stories behind each object.

    Gallery 3
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    A slow, layered performance that moves through the atrium, blending voice, percussion and resonant space.

    Atrium
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Light and projection transform the marble figures into shifting, luminous forms across the hall.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    An open conversation with the night’s artists about process, memory and what it means to show work after dark.

    Lecture Room
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    As the clock turns, a silent walk through the galleries with lanterns and the archive’s most fragile pieces.

    Rotunda
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    A final session in the courtyard and rotunda, drifting gently toward the last hour of the night.

    Courtyard

Getting there

  • Metro: Świętokrzyska station, about a 5-minute walk.
  • Tram and bus stops are located close to the venue on Krakowskie Przedmieście.
  • Parking is limited and paid. We recommend public transport or ride-hailing.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance and wheelchair-friendly routes throughout the galleries.
  • Accessible toilets on the ground floor.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome.
  • A quiet room is available for anyone who needs a break from the crowds.

Travel tips

  • Doors open at 19:00. Arriving early gives you the calmest start.
  • Dress warmly; some courtyard sessions take place outdoors.
