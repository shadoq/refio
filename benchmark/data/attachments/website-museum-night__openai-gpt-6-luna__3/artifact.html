A few things to know

Good to
know.

A little practical magic to help your night go smoothly.

Doors open at 19:00 and the night runs until 02:00. Your ticket is valid for entry at any time during the event; we recommend arriving early if you'd like to catch the opening welcome.

The Old Gallery has step-free access to the main galleries, accessible toilets, and seating areas throughout. Please contact the venue team before your visit if you have a specific access request.

Tickets are limited, so advance booking is recommended. If any remain, a small number may be available at the door. This demo page does not process real bookings or payments.

Come as you are. There is no dress code; choose something comfortable for wandering through the galleries and enjoying a long night out.

Re-entry is available on the night. Please keep your ticket confirmation with you and check in with the door team when you leave and return.

The event is designed for adults and older teens, and all guests under 16 must attend with an adult. Some performances include low lighting and amplified sound.
