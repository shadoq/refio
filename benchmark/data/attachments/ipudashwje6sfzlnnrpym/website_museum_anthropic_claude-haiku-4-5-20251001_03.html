Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night

What is the dress code? +
We recommend smart casual to elegant attire. This is a premium cultural event, so please dress to impress. Comfortable shoes are recommended as you'll be exploring multiple gallery spaces.
Can I bring a guest? +
Each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring guests, please purchase additional tickets. Group discounts are available for parties of 10 or more—contact our team for details.
Is there a refund policy? +
Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person. Transfers must be requested at least 7 days before the event. In case of event cancellation, full refunds will be issued.
What time should I arrive? +
Doors open at 19:00. We recommend arriving 15-30 minutes early to check in and enjoy the opening reception. The venue will remain open until 02:00.
Are cameras allowed? +
Photography is encouraged in designated areas. Please be respectful of other guests and artists. Professional photography equipment may require prior approval.
Is the venue accessible? +
Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with accessible restrooms and elevators. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us in advance so we can ensure your comfort.
