The night belongs to art

When the doors close, the stories open.

Step into a museum transformed. For one night, The Old Gallery leaves the familiar behind: paintings glow in the dark, music finds its way through grand halls, and new encounters unfold around every corner.

ARTIFACT is an invitation to look closer, listen differently, and lose track of the hour. A meeting place for curious minds, made for the beautifully unexpected.

A few useful details

Good to know.

Everything you need to make the most of your night at the museum.

What is ARTIFACT: Museum Night?

ARTIFACT is a one-night cultural event at The Old Gallery, bringing together after-hours access to the collection, live performances, conversations, and music. The event runs from 19:00 on 18 October until 02:00 on 19 October 2026.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 19:00. Your ticket is valid for the full event, and you are welcome to arrive at any time during the evening. Patron Circle ticket holders may arrive from 18:30.

Are tickets refundable or transferable?

For this demonstration website, ticket selection is a local, non-binding interaction and no real purchase takes place. Please check the event organizer’s official terms for real-world ticket policies.

Can I attend with children?

The event is designed as a late-night experience for adults and older teens. Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Some performances may contain low lighting or loud sound.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free entry is available at the side entrance, and accessible facilities are located on the main event level. Please contact the venue ahead of time if you have specific access questions or need assistance.

Can I leave and come back?

Re-entry is available on the night with your event wristband and ticket confirmation. Please keep both with you when you leave the building.

Will food and drinks be available?

A small selection of refreshments will be available to purchase in the foyer. Food and drink are not permitted inside the galleries.

How do I get to The Old Gallery?

The gallery is at Plac Małachowskiego 3 in central Warsaw. Świętokrzyska metro station is about a 7-minute walk away, with bus and tram stops nearby. Limited paid parking is available in the area.
