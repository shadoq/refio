The Concept

One night.
A thousand years of quiet.

For a single night, The Old Gallery unlocks its doors after dark. Wing by wing, the museum turns into a stage — vaults open, marble breathes with projection, and a cello answers the old masters. Slow down, wander, and let the silence do the talking.

6Wings open
40+Artists & guides
7Hours of night

After-hours exhibitions

Every gallery stays open past midnight, hung for low light and long looks. Rediscover familiar works in total quiet.

Live performances

Cello, electronics and voice woven between the paintings — sound staged for stone, not speakers.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups descend into the vault level and storerooms that never open by day. Capacity is limited; curiosity is not.

Venue

The Old Gallery

A nineteenth-century palace of art, unlit and unguarded — for one night only.

Getting in, getting there

ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 13, 00-071 Warsaw

  • Public transport: Metro M2 to Świętokrzyska, then a 7-minute walk. Buses 128 and 175 stop directly at "Krakowskie Przedmieście".
  • Parking: Limited street parking after 20:00. We recommend public transport or a taxi drop-off at the main entrance.
  • Accessibility: Step-free entrance on Krakowskie street, elevators to all public floors, and accessible restrooms on every level.
  • Comforts: Cloakroom and a quiet room on the first floor; assistance dogs are welcome throughout the night.
  • Entrance: The gold-lit arch on Krakowskie — look for the diamond marker on the map.
