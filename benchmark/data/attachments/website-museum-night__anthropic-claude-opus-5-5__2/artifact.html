The Concept

An evening where the collection wakes

For one night each autumn, The Old Gallery surrenders its daylight rules. The fluorescent hum is switched off, the ropes are lowered, and the halls are handed to curators, composers and light-makers who know how to make old things feel dangerous again.

Walk through rooms that have been sealed for decades. Listen to a cellist answer the echo of the Rotunda. Watch marble figures breathe under projected light. Every hour, a new layer of the building reveals itself — and by midnight, the museum belongs to those who stayed.

ARTIFACT is not a party in a museum. It is a museum, finally allowed to perform.

Helena MarczakChief Curator, The Old Gallery

Three ways in
I

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection re-lit for night: low amber pools of light, quiet galleries, and three rooms opened exclusively for this evening.

II

Live performances

Ambient sets, movement pieces and spoken word staged between sculptures — performances composed for the acoustics of the building itself.

III

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups follow curators through the Vault Wing, restoration studios and archive corridors normally closed to the public.

The Program

Seven hours, eight moments

The evening unfolds room by room. Arrive early for the full arc, or drift in and follow the light.

  1. 19:00

    Doors Open

    Arrive under the colonnade. Candlelit welcome, cloakroom and a glass of something amber in the foyer.

    Grand Foyer
  2. 19:30

    Curator Welcome

    Helena Marczak opens the night with a short address beneath the dome and a first glimpse of what's hidden.

    Rotunda
  3. 20:00

    Hidden Collection Tour

    Small-group descents into storage rooms and sealed archives, led by curators and historians.

    Vault Wing
  4. 21:00

    Live Ambient Performance

    A slow-building set of cello, tape loops and field recordings from the building's own corridors.

    Atrium
  5. 22:00

    Sculpture Hall Projection

    Classical marble mapped with light and shadow — a twenty-minute cycle that repeats until midnight.

    Sculpture Hall
  6. 23:00

    Artist Talk

    An intimate conversation on memory, darkness and what museums owe the future. Reserved seating for Curator Pass.

    Lecture Salon
  7. 00:00

    Midnight Gallery Walk

    Lights drop to a whisper. Torch-lit walk through the Old Masters with a poet reading between frames.

    East Galleries
  8. 01:00

    Closing Sound Session

    A final hour of deep, resonant sound under the dome. Lie back on velvet cushions and let the night end slowly.

    Rotunda

The Venue

The Old Gallery

A neoclassical hall on Warsaw's Royal Route, home to three centuries of collected objects — and, for one night, to you.

Address

The Old Gallery
ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 15
00-071 Warsaw

Main entrance on Krakowskie Przedmieście, beneath the central colonnade. Look for the gold lanterns.

Travel tips

  • Doors open at 19:00 — the first hour is the quietest time to explore.
  • Layers recommended: the Vault Wing stays cool all year.
  • Cloakroom is free; large bags must be checked.

Accessibility

  • Step-free access via the north ramp and two passenger lifts.
  • Accessible restrooms on every floor; quiet room beside the foyer.
  • Companions of guests with disabilities enter free — just ask at the door.

Parking & public transport

  • Metro M2 · Nowy Świat–Uniwersytet, 6 minutes on foot.
  • Buses 116, 128, 180 · stop "Uniwersytet", 2 minutes away.
  • Night buses run after 23:15; taxi rank on Królewska.
  • Limited paid parking under Plac Piłsudskiego (open 24h).
