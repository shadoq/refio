The Program

19:00 — Arrival & Welcome

Glass of vintage wine and a guided entry into the silent halls. The museum transforms as the city lights fade.

20:15 — Live Performance

A contemporary string quartet performs a reimagined classical suite in the main rotunda.

21:45 — The Curator's Tour

An exclusive, after-hours tour of the "Hidden Collection" usually reserved for scholars.

23:00 — Midnight Jazz & Art

The courtyard opens up. Live jazz, interactive light installations, and late-night refreshments.

02:00 — Closing

A final toast as the museum doors close for another year. Goodnight.

Frequently Asked Questions

ARTIFACT is designed as a sophisticated evening event. While children are welcome, the atmosphere and late hours (until 02:00) make it most suitable for adults or families with older teenagers.

Each ticket is assigned to one individual. If you wish to attend with someone, please purchase two separate tickets.

We encourage "Museum Chic" or smart casual attire. While there's no strict black tie requirement, guests typically dress elegantly for the occasion.
