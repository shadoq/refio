Venue

The Old Gallery

The Old Gallery in Warsaw, steeped in eighteenth-century grandeur, receives ARTIFACT Night in its candlelit halls. Marble floors reflect golden spotlights while velvet drapes cradle slow-moving projections.

Expect quiet courtyards, hidden libraries, and midnight cafes opening to whisper talk. Every corner offers a hushed narrative waiting to be discovered.
