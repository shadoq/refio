The Concept

An Evening Between
the Frames

After the last visitor leaves and the lights dim to a whisper, the gallery becomes something else entirely. ARTIFACT: Museum Night opens the doors to a nocturnal edition of The Old Gallery — where curated darkness reveals what daylight conceals, and every corridor hosts a new encounter.

01

After-hours Exhibitions

Rooms normally closed to the public open for one night only, lit by hand-placed spotlights and candlelight.

02

Live Performances

Ambient composers, light artists and performers respond to the collection in real time, hall by hall.

03

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small-group descents into the vault levels with curators who guard the stories behind the storerooms.
