ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night,
Reimagined

The gallery shifts after dark. Follow light through forgotten rooms, meet artists at work, and experience the collection in a different state of mind.

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Get Tickets
“An invitation to see the familiar collection by a more mysterious light.”

The proposition

Stay after the last bell.

ARTIFACT is a slow, sensory passage through art after hours—when the halls are quieter, the shadows are longer, and every encounter feels unexpectedly close.

01 / EXHIBITIONS

After-hours exhibitions

Move freely through the permanent collection and site-specific interventions, open until late.

02 / PERFORMANCE

Live performances

Sound, movement, and light reshape the gallery architecture throughout the evening.

03 / DISCOVERY

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups enter spaces and stories usually kept beyond the public route.
