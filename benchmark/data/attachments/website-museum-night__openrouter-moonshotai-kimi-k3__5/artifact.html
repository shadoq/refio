The Concept

Where the museum exhales

When the last daylight visitor leaves, the museum does not sleep — it exhales. For one night in October, the Old Gallery opens its doors after dark: the permanent collection lit by golden spotlights, velvet darkness gathering in the corners, and a one-night-only ARTIFACT exhibition at its heart. Composers, performers and projection artists move quietly among the works. And behind locked doors, the vaulted storage rooms wait for the few who hold a Curator Pass.

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection and a one-night-only ARTIFACT exhibition, lit for darkness and open until two in the morning.

Live performances

Ambient composers, movement artists and projection mappers performing among the works throughout the night.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led into the vaulted storage rooms normally closed to the public.

Finding Us

The Old Gallery

Getting there

Metro M2 to Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet, then a 6-minute walk. Buses 116 and 180 stop at Krakowskie Przedmieście, directly outside.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance on the north side, elevators to all floors, accessible restrooms on every level. Wheelchairs available free of charge at the cloakroom.

Parking

Limited paid parking at Plac Piłsudskiego (5 min). We strongly recommend public transport — the night ends at 02:00.

Good to know

Cloakroom is free and mandatory for large bags. The museum café serves drinks and small plates until 01:00.
