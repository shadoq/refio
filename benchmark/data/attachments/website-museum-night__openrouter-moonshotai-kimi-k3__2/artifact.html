The Concept

One night the museum
stays awake

For one night in October, The Old Gallery refuses to sleep. The daytime crowds dissolve, the lights drop to a golden hush, and the collection breathes in the dark. Wander the halls past midnight, hear music drift between the marble, and step into rooms that have been locked for decades. This is the museum as it was always meant to be felt — slowly, quietly, after dark.

After-hours exhibitions

The galleries stay open past midnight — no daytime crowds, no queues, just you and the collection in golden half-light.

Live performances

Ambient sets and deep listening sessions unfold between the halls, scored to the building's own acoustics.

Guided hidden-room tours

Curators unlock rooms closed to the public for decades — vaults, archives and corridors few have ever seen.
