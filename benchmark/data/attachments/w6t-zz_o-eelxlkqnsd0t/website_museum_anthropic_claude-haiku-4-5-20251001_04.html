ARTIFACT: Museum Night

The Concept

An immersive nocturnal experience blending contemporary art, live performance, and architectural discovery. Museum Night transforms familiar spaces into venues for wonder.

🎨

Curated Installations

Discover site-specific artworks created exclusively for this event, transforming every corner of the gallery into a canvas for creative expression.

🎭

Live Performances

Experience world-class musicians, dancers, and performers in intimate settings throughout the venue, creating moments of unexpected beauty.

✨

Nocturnal Atmosphere

Specially designed lighting and soundscapes create an otherworldly ambiance that reveals the museum in an entirely new dimension.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night.

What is included in each ticket tier?

▼

Standard tickets include full access to all performances, installations, and the opening reception. Premium tickets add VIP lounge access, complimentary drinks, and a meet & greet with featured artists. Exclusive tickets include everything in Premium plus a private guided tour, exclusive dinner, behind-the-scenes access, and signed merchandise.

What time should I arrive?

▼

The event runs from 19:00 to 02:00. We recommend arriving by 18:45 to allow time for check-in and to experience the opening reception. Premium and Exclusive ticket holders can arrive from 18:30 for early access to the VIP lounge.

Is the venue accessible for people with disabilities?

▼

Yes, The Old Gallery is fully wheelchair accessible with accessible restrooms and designated parking. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us at info@artifactmuseum.com and we'll ensure your comfort throughout the event.

Can I bring a guest?

▼

Absolutely! Each ticket is for one person. Simply purchase additional tickets for your guests. Group discounts are available for parties of 10 or more—contact us for details.

What should I wear?

▼

Smart casual to formal attire is recommended. The venue maintains a sophisticated atmosphere. Comfortable shoes are advised as you'll be exploring multiple spaces throughout the evening. The temperature is climate-controlled.

Is photography allowed?

▼

Personal photography is permitted in designated areas. However, professional photography and videography require prior permission. Please respect artists' requests regarding specific installations. Flash photography is not permitted in certain areas to preserve the ambiance.
