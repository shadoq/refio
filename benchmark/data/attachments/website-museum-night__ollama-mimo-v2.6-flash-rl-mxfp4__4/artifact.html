ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night,
Reimagined

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

One night. The doors stay open after dark. Hidden rooms, live sound, and the collection seen by golden light only.

The Concept

A night written
for the collection

Once a year, The Old Gallery closes to the daylight crowd and opens its velvet doors to those who prefer their art after dark. ARTIFACT is not an exhibition — it is an evening, composed like a film: slow openings, rising tension, a midnight crescendo, and a quiet close.

Wander rooms lit only by spotlights, follow curators into spaces normally kept locked, and let live ambient performance rewrite the acoustics of the sculpture hall. Nothing is repeated. Nothing is rushed.

7 hours of after-hours access
01

After-Hours Exhibitions

The permanent collection and three temporary shows stay open until 02:00, re-lit for the night with a dedicated spotlight design.

02

Live Performances

Ambient sets, chamber fragments and projection pieces staged between the halls — sound that moves with the crowd.

03

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small groups are led into the restoration atelier and the archive vault, normally closed to the public entirely.

Venue

The Old Gallery,
Warsaw

A 19th-century exhibition hall on the west bank of the Vistula, five minutes from the old town and its cobbled approach.

Address

14 Gallery Lane, Śródmieście, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Public Transport

Metro M2 to Centrum (8 min walk). Trams 15, 18 and 41 stop at Plac Zamkowy. Night bus N16 stops directly outside.

Parking

Underground car park entrance on Archive Street, 60 spaces, €4 flat evening rate. Street parking is free after 19:00.

Accessibility

Step-free entry via the south ramp, lift access to all floors, accessible WCs on every level. Induction loop in the Reading Room. Assistance dogs welcome.

FAQ

Before you arrive

Doors open at 19:00. We recommend arriving between 19:00 and 19:30 so you can collect your pass, pick up the night map and settle in before the Curator Welcome. Last entry is 01:00.

No formal dress code — come as you would to the theatre. Many guests lean into the evening with dark, elegant attire, but comfort matters most since you will be on your feet across several floors.

The Hidden Collection Tour and Midnight Gallery Walk are included with the Curator and Patron Passes. A small number of places are released for Night Pass holders at 20:00 on a first-come basis at the information desk.

ARTIFACT is an evening event intended for guests aged 16 and over. Visitors under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. The programme contains low light, loud ambient sound and strobe-like projection effects.

Yes. Step-free entry is available via the south ramp, with lift access to every floor and accessible WCs on each level. An induction loop operates in the Reading Room. Please note any requirements during reservation and our team will contact you.

Reservations can be cancelled for a full refund up to 7 days before the event. After that date, tickets are transferable to another person but non-refundable. In the unlikely event the event is cancelled, all reservations are refunded in full.
