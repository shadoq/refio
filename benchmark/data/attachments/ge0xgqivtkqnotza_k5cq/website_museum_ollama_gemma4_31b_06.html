The Experience

A nocturnal journey through the silence of art and the echo of history.

Step into a world where boundaries between observer and object dissolve. ARTIFACT is not just an exhibition; it is a curated sensory odyssey designed to awaken the subconscious in the velvet darkness of The Old Gallery.

The Itinerary

Nightly Sequence

19:00

The Threshold

Arrival and welcome cocktails. Guests are introduced to the night's theme through a curated soundscape.

21:00

Shadow Walk

A guided tour led by historians and artists, focusing on the hidden narratives of the gallery.

23:00

The Resonance

Live ambient performances integrated into the exhibits, creating a dialogue between sound and sculpture.

01:00

Midnight Reflection

Open exploration and intimate discussions in the lounge area with curated refreshments.

The Space

The Old Gallery

Located in the heart of Warsaw, The Old Gallery is a neoclassical masterpiece known for its vaulted ceilings and echoing halls. For one night, we transform this sanctuary into a cinematic landscape.
