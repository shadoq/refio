The Concept

When the doors close, the collection begins to breathe.

ARTIFACT turns the museum into a cinema without a screen. Galleries are re-lit by hand, soundscapes drift between centuries, and rooms normally sealed to the public open for a few quiet hours. It is an invitation to see familiar masterpieces as if for the first time — in shadow, in gold, in silence.

Curators, sculptors, composers and light artists have shaped a single continuous evening. Move at your own pace, follow the program, or simply lose yourself between the pedestals.

I

After-hours exhibitions

The permanent collection and two new installations, re-staged with low, directional light and open until two in the morning.

II

Live performances

Ambient concerts beneath the rotunda, projection pieces across marble, and a closing sound session in the moonlit courtyard.

III

Guided hidden-room tours

Small-group descents into restoration studios and the vault level, led by historians who know every story behind the glass.

The Venue

Finding The Old Gallery

A neoclassical landmark on Warsaw’s Royal Route, a few steps from the Old Town.

The Old Gallery

ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 15, 00-071 Warsaw, Poland

Travel tips

  • Arrive between 19:00 and 20:00 for the shortest queues.
  • Cloakroom is free and open until 02:30.
  • Bring your ticket QR on your phone or printed.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance via the main courtyard gate.
  • Lifts to all levels, including Vault Level −1.
  • Wheelchair loan available at the Grand Foyer desk.
  • Quiet room with low light on the first floor.
  • ISL-interpreted artist talk at 23:00.

Parking & public transport

  • Metro M2 Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet — 6 min walk.
  • Buses 116, 128, 175; night buses (N) after 23:00.
  • Limited parking at Plac Piłsudskiego garage.
  • Bike racks beside the courtyard entrance.
