Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARTIFACT: Museum Night.

What should I wear? +

We recommend smart casual to formal attire. The event has an elegant, sophisticated atmosphere. Comfortable shoes are recommended as you'll be exploring the gallery throughout the evening.

Can I bring a guest? +

Yes, each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring guests, simply purchase additional tickets. Group discounts are available for parties of 10 or more.

Is there parking available? +

Yes, the venue has dedicated parking available for guests. We also recommend using public transportation or ride-sharing services. The gallery is easily accessible by metro.

What if I have dietary restrictions? +

We accommodate all dietary requirements. Please indicate any restrictions when purchasing your ticket, and our team will ensure appropriate refreshments are available for you.

Can I take photographs? +

Photography is permitted in designated areas. Some installations may have photography restrictions to preserve the artistic experience. Our staff will guide you on where photography is allowed.

What is your refund policy? +

Tickets are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person. If you need to cancel, please contact our support team at least 7 days before the event.
