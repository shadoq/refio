The Concept

When the doors close, the art wakes.

ARTIFACT turns The Old Gallery into an after-hours stage. Lights fall, sound rises, and a collection usually seen in daylight is re-lit by projection, performance and whispered stories.

Wander freely through the main halls, join curators in rooms that are normally sealed, and end the night inside a sound session that lasts until the small hours. Limited capacity keeps the evening intimate.

After-hours exhibitions

Main halls re-lit in low gold light, with new works shown only for this one night.

Live performances

Ambient composers, projection artists and sound designers performing among the sculptures.

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups follow curators behind the walls to archives and vaults closed to the public.

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A neoclassical gallery on the edge of the old town — sixty rooms, a sculpture hall, and a courtyard that glows at night.

Address

The Old Gallery, ul. Galeryjna 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Travel tips

  • Walk from the Old Town Square in about 10 minutes.
  • Taxi and ride-share drop-off is on the north side, by the main gate.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early to avoid the 19:00 queue.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance and lifts to all floors, accessible toilets, quiet seating, and assistance dogs welcome. Tell us your needs at the door and staff will guide you.

Parking & public transport

Metro M1 “Muzeum” is a 4-minute walk; buses 175 and 222 stop at “Galeria”. Night buses run until 04:00. Paid parking is available at Garage Plac Sztuki, 300 m away.
