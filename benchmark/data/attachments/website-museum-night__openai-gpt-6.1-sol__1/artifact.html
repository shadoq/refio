06 / Before you arrive

A few
quiet answers.

The practical details, so your attention can stay on the art.

Is this a real event or ticket purchase?

No. ARTIFACT is a fictional event created for this demo landing page. The reservation form demonstrates ticket selection only. There is no payment, no real reservation, and no information is transmitted or stored.

Can I arrive later in the evening?

In the imagined program, all passes allow arrival after 19:00 and entry until 01:00. The hidden collection tour begins at 20:00, so Curator and Patron guests would arrive before then. The evening ends at 02:00.

May I take photographs?

The concept welcomes personal photography without flash in the main galleries. Some works and performances would be marked no-photography. Please respect other guests, keep pathways clear, and leave tripods at home.

Is the venue accessible?

The fictional venue concept includes step-free access, lifts, accessible facilities, and quiet seating. These are design intentions, not verified real-world provisions. For an actual event, confirm access, tour routes, and any accommodations with its organizer.

Can I change my ticket tier?

Yes. Before confirming your demo reservation, change the pass in the form's ticket selector. The total updates automatically. After confirmation, you can start a new demo selection; nothing is saved and no ticket is issued.

How does Save to Calendar work?

The button downloads an .ics calendar file that you can import into your preferred calendar app. It uses the Europe/Warsaw time zone, from 19:00 on 18 October to 02:00 on 19 October 2026. Saving the date does not reserve a ticket.

What should I wear?

Whatever lets you move comfortably and feel like yourself. The imagined evening involves standing and walking between galleries, so comfortable shoes are recommended. There is no formal dress code.
