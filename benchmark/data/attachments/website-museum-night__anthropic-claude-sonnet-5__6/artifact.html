The Concept

An Institution After Dark

When the last visitors leave, ARTIFACT begins. For one night, The Old Gallery is reclaimed — its halls lit only by spotlight and candle-glow, its silence replaced by live sound and whispered tours through rooms rarely seen. This is not a party inside a museum. It is the museum, transformed.

01

After-Hours Exhibitions

Wander galleries lit exclusively for the night, reframing familiar works in gold spotlight and shadow.

02

Live Performances

Ambient composers and performance artists respond live to the architecture and the collection.

03

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Step behind the velvet rope into archive rooms and storage halls never open to the public.

Who's Behind the Night

Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A 19th-century gallery building in the heart of Warsaw, known for its vaulted stone halls and hidden archive wing — the setting for one night only.

ul. Galeryjna 12, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Travel Tips

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your entry slot
  • Cloakroom available in the Main Hall
  • Doors close briefly during performances

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance from the main courtyard
  • Accessible restrooms on ground floor
  • Wheelchair access to all public galleries
  • Hearing loop available in the Auditorium

Parking & Transport

  • Nearest metro: Ratusz Arsenał (6 min walk)
  • Bus stop directly outside the main entrance
  • Street parking limited — public transport recommended
