The Concept

One night, the museum
never sleeps

For one night the galleries stay open after dark — halls lit by candle-warm spotlights, live sound drifting between sculptures, and rooms the public has never seen. ARTIFACT is an invitation to wander slowly, listen closely, and meet the collection at its most cinematic hour.

After-hours exhibitions

Eighteen rooms stay open until 02:00, lit low and slow — the collection as you have never seen it.

Live performances

Cello, modular synth and projection, scored live through the marble rooms and the sculpture court.

Guided hidden-room tours

A once-nightly walk into the vault wing — works held in the dark for decades, shown to thirty guests.

The Venue

The Old Gallery

ul. Złota 14, 00-005 Warsaw, Poland

Getting here

4 minutes on foot from Metro Świętokrzyska. Trams 15 & 18 stop at “Złota”. Taxi drop-off directly on Złota street, beside the east door.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance via the east door. Elevators reach all floors. Accessible restrooms on every level. Assistance dogs welcome. Large-print guides available at the cloakroom.

Parking

Limited street parking after 20:00. NBP garage 300 m away on Imperial Street. There is no on-site parking — we recommend public transport.
