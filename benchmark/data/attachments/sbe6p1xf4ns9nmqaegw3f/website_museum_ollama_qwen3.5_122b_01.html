MUSEUM NIGHT

The Old Gallery, Warsaw 19:00 – 02:00

The Experience

Step into a world where art breathes in the silence of the night. ARTIFACT is not merely an event; it is a curated journey through the hidden corridors of culture, illuminated by golden spotlights and the whispers of history.

From 19:00 to 02:00, The Old Gallery transforms into a sanctuary of sound, light, and visual mastery. Experience exclusive installations, live performances by avant-garde artists, and the rare opportunity to view masterpieces in an atmosphere of velvet darkness.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

A Historic Masterpiece

Built in 1895, The Old Gallery stands as a testament to neoclassical grandeur. Tonight, its marble halls and vaulted ceilings will serve as the canvas for our most ambitious production yet.
