Demo ROM: a face sprite bounces around the screen. Keys 4 5 6 8 (i.e. keyboard Q W E S) steer it left / up / right / down. Key 5 also triggers a beep.

Controls: Run/Pause toggles execution, Single Step executes one instruction, Reset restarts the CPU, and the speed slider sets instructions executed per frame (60 frames/s).
