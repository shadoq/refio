The Concept

Where Art Meets the Night

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms The Old Gallery into an immersive after-hours experience. Walk through dimly lit galleries, discover hidden rooms, hear live performances echoing through marble halls, and step into a world where art comes alive under velvet darkness.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Explore rare installations and rotating shows only accessible during this exclusive evening. Curated works revealed under dramatic lighting.

Live Performances

Contemporary musicians, dancers, and spoken-word artists perform across multiple rooms, each creating their own atmosphere.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Follow curators through secret passages and private collections, unlocking stories never told to the public.
