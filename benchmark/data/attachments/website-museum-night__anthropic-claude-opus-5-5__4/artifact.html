The Concept

When the lights go down, the art wakes up.

For one night, The Old Gallery trades daylight for velvet darkness. Every hall is relit by hand — pools of warm gold falling on marble, oil, and bronze — so that familiar masterpieces reveal details you have never noticed. Music drifts between rooms, curators whisper stories that never make the wall labels, and doors marked “Staff only” quietly swing open.

ARTIFACT is not a party and not a lecture. It is a slow, cinematic walk through art history after hours — designed for the curious, the romantic, and the sleepless.

I

After-hours exhibitions

Wander the permanent collection and three special exhibitions, relit in dramatic spotlight for a single night.

II

Live performances

Ambient concerts in the Atrium, projection art in the Sculpture Hall, and movement pieces that appear without warning.

III

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups led by curators into the vaults, restoration studios, and archives normally closed to visitors.

Venue

A 19th-century neoclassical building on Warsaw’s Royal Route — marble halls, a glass-domed atrium, and vaults beneath.

Travel tips
Arrive between 19:00 and 20:00 to avoid queues. Main entrance is on Krakowskie Przedmieście, marked by gold lanterns.
Public transport
Metro line M2 to Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet (4 min walk). Buses 116, 128, 175 and 180 stop at “Uniwersytet”. Night buses run from 23:30.
Parking
No on-site parking. Nearest garage: Plac Piłsudskiego (7 min walk). We strongly recommend public transport or taxi.
Accessibility
Step-free entrance on the north side, lifts to all public floors, accessible restrooms, and seating in every gallery. Carers enter free.
