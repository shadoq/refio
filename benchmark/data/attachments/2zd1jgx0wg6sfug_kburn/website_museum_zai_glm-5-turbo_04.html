The Concept

When the last visitor leaves and the lights dim, the museum transforms. ARTIFACT is an after-hours experience where contemporary art, live sound, and hidden spaces converge into a single nocturnal journey through one of Warsaw's most storied galleries.

After-Hours Exhibitions

Walk through reimagined galleries where works are revealed under dramatic lighting, far from the daytime crowds.

Live Performances

Ambient soundscapes, projection art, and live sets transform the sculpture halls into immersive environments.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Access restricted chambers, private collections, and the forgotten wings of the gallery with expert curators.
