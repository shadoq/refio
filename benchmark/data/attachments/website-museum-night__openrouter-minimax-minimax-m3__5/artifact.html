The Concept

An evening for those who linger.

ARTIFACT is a quietly ambitious, one-night-only experience staged in the hours when museums traditionally close. The galleries stay open — but transformed. The lights are warmer, the crowds smaller, the rooms deeper. Live performances meet sculpture. Curator-led tours slip through hidden rooms. The night ends with a closing sound session in the marble hall.

01 / After-Hours

After-Hours Exhibitions

The main halls remain open until 02:00, lit only by the curators' original concept lighting — a slow, deliberate, intimate re-staging.

02 / Live

Live Performances

Ambient sets, modular compositions and one live sculpture-projection performance — composed specifically for the venue's acoustics.

03 / Hidden

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Small groups, led by the curatorial team, move through three rooms that are rarely opened to the public — including the vault.

One night, eight movements.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

A 19th-century gallery in the heart of the city.

The Old Gallery has held its main collection since 1894. Three floors of permanent exhibition, a sculpture court, a marble hall, and — for the first time in five years — the west-wing vault.

Address ul. Krucza 16, 00-526 Warsaw, Poland
Travel Tips 15 min walk from Warszawa Centralna. The building's main entrance is on the south side, off Krucza. Look for the small brass plaque — there is no signage on the street.
Accessibility Step-free access via the east entrance. All program rooms are accessible by elevator. Hearing loop available in the Marble Hall.
Parking & Transport No on-site parking. We recommend Metro Centrum (10 min walk) or tram lines 4, 10, 18 (stop: Krucza). Bicycle racks on the east side.
