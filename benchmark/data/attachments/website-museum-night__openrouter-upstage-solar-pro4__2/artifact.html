The Concept

When the lights go down, the museum begins.

ARTIFACT is a single night inside The Old Gallery — doors closed to the public, corridors lit by golden spotlights, and the collection seen as it has never been seen before. Eight hours of exhibitions, performances, talks, and guided tours, unfolding after sunset.

A cinematic evening for those who believe culture does not stop when the doors lock.

Curated in silence. Experienced in wonder.

01

After-Hours Exhibitions

The full collection, open late. Rare works and hidden shelves, lit in gold, seen without the crowds.

02

Live Performances

Ambient music, sculpture projections, and a closing sound session — each performance placed inside the galleries.

03

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Curator-led tours into rooms not usually open to the public — small groups, quiet stories, rare access.

The Venue

The Old Gallery, Warsaw.

A historic gallery in the heart of the city — quiet stone corridors, tall windows, and a collection that has lived here for decades.

Getting There

The Old Gallery

ul. Staromiejska 14, 00-001 Warsaw, Poland

Public Transport

Metro: Staromiejska station, exit 2 — a two-minute walk. Tram lines 4, 12, and 21 stop directly outside the gallery.

Parking

Limited on-site parking for ticket holders. Public garages are available on ul. Rynkowa, two blocks away. Evening rates apply after 18:00.

Accessibility

Step-free access from the main entrance. Elevators serve all gallery floors. Wheelchairs are available to borrow at the entrance desk — ask a member of staff.

Travel Tip

Arrive early. The entrance queue opens at 18:30 and the galleries fill quickly after 19:30. A short walk through the old town before the event makes the evening.
