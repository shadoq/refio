ARTIFACT: Museum Night

Museum Night, Reimagined

Step beyond the velvet ropes when the public leaves. An evening of unilluminated antiquities, site-specific sound sculptures, and hidden archival chambers.

18 October 2026
19:00 – 02:00
The Old Gallery, Warsaw
Strict 500 Guest Capacity Subterranean Vault Access Bespoke Spatial Acoustics

“When daylight recedes from Warsaw’s neoclassical corridors, the silence reveals centuries of untold provenance. ARTIFACT transforms the traditional museum visit into a cinematic sensory communion.”

01

After-Hours Exhibitions

Experience permanent masterworks and unreleased temporary acquisitions under focused low-lux golden spotlights with no daylight contamination.

02

Live Sonic Performances

Six contemporary composers and acoustic vocalists create generative soundscapes custom-tuned to the resonance of marble and limestone rotundas.

03

Hidden-Room Tours

Access the closed subterranean archives, restoration ateliers, and the 17th-century cloistered vaults typically barred from general museum admission.

Location & Heritage

The Old Gallery, Warsaw

ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście 44, 00-325 Warsaw

Erected in the late 18th century as a private aristocratic salon and subsequently restored into Warsaw’s most revered historic exhibition halls. ARTIFACT unlocks wing doors sealed since 1989.

Public Transit & Arrival

Metro M2 (Station Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet) is a 4-minute walk. Buses 116, 180, and 503 stop directly opposite the main courtyard portico.

Accessibility & Accommodations

Step-free elevator access is fitted to all public levels including underground vaults. Braille spatial maps and tactile museum assistants available at reception.

Private Parking

Secure underground valet parking is allocated to Patron Pass holders at the rear entrance (ul. Karowa 12).
