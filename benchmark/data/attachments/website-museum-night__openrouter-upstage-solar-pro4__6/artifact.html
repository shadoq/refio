The Experience

A single night,
after the doors are closed.

ARTIFACT turns the museum inside out for one evening only. The galleries stay dark, the lights move slowly, and the collection reveals itself in fragments — a sculpture catching a spotlight, a corridor humming with live sound, a room that exists only for those who know where to knock.

This is not a tour. It is a walk through a city that has been waiting in the walls all along.

01

After-hours exhibitions

Selected works remain lit long after closing, arranged in quiet sequences that only unfold as the night deepens.

02

Live performances

Ambient musicians and performers respond to the rooms around them, turning each hall into a living composition.

03

Guided hidden-room tours

Small groups are led through spaces normally closed to the public — storage corridors, archive rooms, and one room with no label.

Getting there

The gallery sits two blocks from the central metro station. Evening buses run along ul. Old Gallery until 00:30. Taxis and ride-share drop-off are available at the main entrance on the south side.

Accessibility

Step-free access is available at the main entrance and in all public galleries. A limited number of wheelchair spaces are reserved for each tour — select the accessibility option when reserving. Guide dogs are welcome.

Parking & transport

Paid underground parking is open until 01:00. We recommend public transport: metro line M1 to Old Gallery station, then a three-minute walk. Night buses 143 and 147 stop directly outside.
