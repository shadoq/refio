Museum Night,
Reimagined

18 October 2026 19:00–02:00 The Old Gallery, Warsaw

One night only

After hours, art awakens.

For one long October night, The Old Gallery becomes an instrument: its rooms filled with light, live sound, moving image and encounters that unfold only after dusk.
