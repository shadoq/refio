The Experience

A Night Beyond the Ordinary

When the doors close to the public, the museum awakens. ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms The Old Gallery into a living canvas — where curators whisper secrets of hidden collections, ambient soundscapes drift through sculpture halls, and projections paint stories across centuries-old walls. This is not a tour. This is an immersion.

After-hours Exhibitions

Wander through galleries bathed in golden spotlight, with pieces rarely displayed and stories told by those who know them best.

Live Performances

Ambient musicians, poets, and visual artists create ephemeral works that exist only for this night — never to be repeated.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Descend into the vault levels where restricted collections await. Limited to small groups for an intimate encounter with art.
