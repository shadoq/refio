Elena Volkov

Contemporary Painter

Elena Volkov is a celebrated contemporary painter known for her immersive large-scale installations that blur the boundaries between viewer and artwork. Her work explores the relationship between space, memory, and perception, inviting audiences to become part of the living canvas. With over 15 years of experience, she has exhibited in major galleries across Europe and Asia, and her pieces are part of prestigious private collections worldwide.

Marcus Chen

Digital Artist

Marcus Chen is a pioneer in augmented reality art, creating interactive experiences that transform physical spaces into digital canvases. His work bridges the gap between traditional art forms and cutting-edge technology, offering viewers immersive experiences that respond to their presence and movement. Chen's installations have been featured at major tech festivals and art biennales worldwide.
