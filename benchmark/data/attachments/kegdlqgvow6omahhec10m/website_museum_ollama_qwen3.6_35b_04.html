The Experience

Where Art Comes Alive

ARTIFACT transforms the museum into a living canvas — a nocturnal journey through light, sound, and shadow where every gallery tells a story.

Immersive Installations

Step into world-class installations designed exclusively for this one-night experience, blending classical art with contemporary technology.

Live Performances

Curated musical performances from avant-garde artists, creating an atmospheric soundscape that weaves through every corridor.

Curated Dining

Exquisite culinary experiences crafted by Michelin-recognized chefs, served in the museum's most iconic galleries.

Evening Schedule

The Program

A carefully orchestrated evening of discovery, from the first golden light to the last whisper of music.

19:00 – 19:30

Grand Entrance

Red carpet arrival with champagne reception in the Grand Foyer. Welcome by the ARTIFACT curatorial team.

19:30 – 20:30

Gallery Walk

Guided nocturnal tour through specially illuminated galleries with artist talks and live commentary.

20:30 – 21:30

Live Performance

Exclusive chamber music performance in the Rotunda, followed by a contemporary dance piece in the West Wing.

21:30 – 23:00

Dinner & Networking

Multi-course tasting menu in the Sculpture Hall, paired with rare wines and artisan cocktails.

23:00 – 02:00

Midnight Gala

DJ set in the Underground Gallery, late-night art viewing, and the ceremonial closing of Museum Night.
