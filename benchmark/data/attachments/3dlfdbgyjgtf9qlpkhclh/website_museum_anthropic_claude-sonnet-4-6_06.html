The Experience

Where Art Meets
the Witching Hour

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms Warsaw's most storied gallery into an immersive nocturnal world. As the city sleeps, the art awakens — bathed in golden light, accompanied by live sound, and revealed through perspectives you've never encountered before.

This is not a museum visit. This is a ritual. A curated journey through darkness and illumination, where every corridor holds a secret and every room tells a story that daylight cannot.

🏛️

After-Hours Exhibitions

Exclusive access to permanent and special collections, lit only by dramatic spotlights and your own curiosity. No crowds. No rush. Just art.

🎵

Live Performances

Ambient composers, chamber ensembles, and experimental sound artists perform throughout the galleries, creating a living soundtrack to the night.

🗝️

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Expert curators lead intimate groups through spaces never open to the public — restoration studios, archive vaults, and the legendary East Wing.

Evening Schedule

The Night's Program

A carefully orchestrated sequence of experiences, each building upon the last as the night deepens.

19:00 PM

Doors Open & Welcome Reception

Arrive to champagne, curated canapés, and the first glimpse of the transformed gallery. Meet fellow guests in the Grand Atrium.

Grand Atrium
19:45 PM

Opening Address & Curator's Vision

Chief Curator Dr. Marta Kowalska unveils the evening's thematic thread — "Shadows & Illumination" — and the stories behind tonight's featured works.

Main Hall
20:30 PM

Hidden Rooms Tour — First Session

Small groups of 12 explore the archive vaults and restoration studios. Witness art in its most vulnerable state — mid-conservation, mid-becoming.

East Wing Vaults
21:15 PM

Live Performance: Ensemble Nox

Warsaw's acclaimed chamber ensemble performs original compositions inspired by the permanent collection. The music moves through the galleries as you do.

Sculpture Garden
22:00 PM

Artist Talk: The Nocturnal Gaze

Featured artists discuss their relationship with darkness, solitude, and the creative process. A rare, intimate conversation moderated by art critic Jan Wiśniewski.

Library Room
23:00 PM

Midnight Supper & Wine Pairing

A curated three-course supper in the candlelit Baroque Hall, with wines selected to mirror the evening's artistic themes. Patron Pass guests receive priority seating.

Baroque Hall
00:00 AM

Midnight Ritual: Lights Out

For five minutes, the entire gallery plunges into darkness. Only the art remains — perceived through sound, memory, and imagination. An unforgettable collective moment.

All Galleries
01:00 AM

Closing Sound Session

Electronic composer SABLE performs a final ambient set as guests drift through the galleries one last time. The night ends as it began — in wonder.

Location

The Old Gallery

ul. Królewska 12, 00-065 Warsaw, Poland

Getting There

  • Metro: Line M1, Świętokrzyska station (3 min walk)
  • Tram: Lines 7, 9, 22 — stop Królewska
  • Bus: Lines 111, 116, 178 — stop Saski Park
  • Taxi / Rideshare: Drop-off at main entrance, ul. Królewska

Parking

Limited parking available in the underground car park beneath Saski Garden (entrance from ul. Marszałkowska). We strongly encourage public transport. Bicycle racks available at the east entrance.

Accessibility

  • Fully wheelchair accessible — all floors via lift
  • Audio descriptions available on request
  • Tactile guides for visually impaired guests
  • BSL interpreter available for the 22:00 talk
  • Quiet room available throughout the evening

On the Night

Doors open at 19:00. Please bring your ticket (digital or printed) and a valid photo ID. Dress code: smart casual. The gallery maintains a cool temperature — a light layer is recommended.

Frequently Asked

All tickets include full gallery access from 19:00 to 02:00, the welcome reception, live performances by Ensemble Nox, the Midnight Ritual, and the Closing Sound Session. Curator and Patron Pass holders receive additional experiences — see the ticket comparison above for full details.
We encourage smart casual attire — think what you'd wear to a gallery opening or a fine dinner. There's no strict enforcement, but the atmosphere is elevated and guests tend to dress accordingly. Comfortable shoes are recommended as you'll be on your feet exploring for several hours.
Yes — the gallery is open continuously from 19:00 to 02:00 and you may arrive and depart at any time. However, some experiences (particularly the Hidden Rooms Tour and Artist Talk) are scheduled at specific times and cannot be rescheduled. We recommend arriving by 19:30 to make the most of the evening.
ARTIFACT: Museum Night is designed as an adult experience. Guests must be 18 or older to attend. This allows us to maintain the intimate, contemplative atmosphere that makes the event special, and to serve alcohol freely throughout the evening.
Tickets are fully refundable up to 14 days before the event (4 October 2026). Between 4–11 October, a 50% refund is available. No refunds are issued within 7 days of the event, but tickets may be transferred to another person free of charge. Please contact us at tickets@artifact-night.pl for assistance.
Personal photography is welcome throughout most of the gallery. Some specific artworks and the Hidden Rooms are photography-restricted — these areas will be clearly marked. We ask that you be mindful of other guests and avoid flash photography. Professional equipment (tripods, external flash) requires prior written permission.
Immediately after completing your reservation, you'll receive a confirmation email with your digital tickets as a PDF attachment. Each ticket contains a unique QR code for entry. You can present tickets on your phone or print them — both are accepted. Tickets are also stored in your account if you create one during checkout.
