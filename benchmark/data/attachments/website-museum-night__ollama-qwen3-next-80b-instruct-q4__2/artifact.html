The Concept

ARTIFACT: Museum Night transforms the traditional museum experience into an immersive, cinematic journey after dark. As the city sleeps, the galleries awaken with curated exhibitions, live performances, and hidden narratives waiting to be discovered. This is not merely an event — it is an invitation to explore the soul of art in the quiet hours when imagination reigns.

After-hours Exhibitions

Experience beloved collections in a new light, illuminated by curated spotlights and ambient soundscapes designed to heighten emotional resonance.

Live Performances

From ambient sound installations to intimate theater pieces, discover performances designed specifically for the nocturnal gallery atmosphere.

Guided Hidden-Room Tours

Access previously unseen areas of the gallery with exclusive guided tours led by curators and artists themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is payment processed on this page?
No, this is a demo landing page. No real payment processing occurs. Ticket reservations are held for 15 minutes before release.
Can I arrive late?
Yes, the main galleries remain open until 01:00. However, certain timed experiences (like the Hidden Collection Tour) require attendance at the scheduled time.
Are photos allowed?
Yes, non-flash photography is permitted for personal use. Flash photography and tripods are prohibited.
Is the venue accessible?
Yes, the venue is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms. Audio guides are available upon request.
Can I change my ticket tier?
Yes, ticket tier changes can be made up to 48 hours before the event via your reservation confirmation email.
How do I save the event to my calendar?
Click the "Save to Calendar" button in the hero section. This will download an .ics file compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.
