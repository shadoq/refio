The Venue

A Historic Space Reborn

The Old Gallery stands as a testament to Warsaw's rich cultural heritage. Built in the early 20th century, its grand halls have witnessed countless exhibitions and performances.

For this special night, we've transformed the space into an intimate sanctuary for art lovers. The original architecture provides a dramatic backdrop for our curated experiences.

Located in the heart of the city, The Old Gallery is easily accessible by public transport. Parking is available nearby for those driving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your ticket includes access to all exhibition spaces, a guided tour, a program booklet, and a commemorative pin. Premium and Donor tickets offer additional exclusive experiences.

Each ticket is for one person. If you'd like to bring guests, please purchase additional tickets. Group bookings of 10+ are available — contact us for details.

We suggest smart casual attire. The venue is air-conditioned, so a light jacket may be comfortable. Feel free to dress as you wish — the night is about art and atmosphere.

Refunds are available up to 14 days before the event. After that, tickets are non-refundable but transferable. Contact our team for assistance.

Doors open at 19:00. Late arrivals will be admitted until 19:30. After that, the event is in full swing and we cannot guarantee entry. Please plan accordingly.
